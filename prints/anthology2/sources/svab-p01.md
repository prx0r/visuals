# Svabodhasiddhi svab-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Bow to the giver of freedom, made of every blessing — to Śiva, the sun that burnt away the darkness on every road of becoming.
The Sanskrit: *namaḥ sakalakalyāṇamayakaivalyadāyine / śivāya śamitāśeṣabhavādhvadhvāntabhānave*

## v.2
I praise the highest Śiva — sick with no possession by any creed, to be known only by the feeling of the self, dwelling in himself.
The Sanskrit: *aśeṣadarśanāveśaviśeṣādhivivarjitam / svasaṃvedanasaṃvedyaṃ svasthaṃ vande paraṃ śivam*

## v.3
Bow, let there be bow, to Śambhu — smasher of the net of fancies and their floods, standing past even the highest bliss.
The Sanskrit: *nirastakalpanājālavikalpaughavighātine / namo 'stu paramānandapadātītāya śambhave*

## v.4
Victorious are Śambhu's feet: they teach the wandering world to tell the real from the sham, and wake the knowledge that frees.
The Sanskrit: *jayanty apārasaṃsārasārāsāravibhāgadāḥ / kaivalyajñānasaṃbodhahetavaś śambhavāṅghrayaḥ*

## v.5
Mind, thought, ego, breaths, the senses' subtle fuel and life — all turn back when they see the one worth worshipping, whom we worship.
The Sanskrit: *mano buddhir ahaṃ prāṇās tanmātrendriyajīvanam / yaṃ dṛṣṭvā vinivartante tam upāsyam upāsmahe*

## v.6
I bow — I — to pure awareness: the only knife that cuts the heart's knot of 'mine' and 'I', that swallowed throbbing and its stillness both.
The Sanskrit: *mamāham iti hṛdgranthicchedanaikakṛpāṇikām / spandāspandakṛtagrāsāṃ naumy ahaṃ śuddhasaṃvidam*
