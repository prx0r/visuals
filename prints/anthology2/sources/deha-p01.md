# Dehastha deha-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
The body is proclaimed a temple of God, the own self the eternal God; casting off the sweepings of ignorance, let him worship with the feeling I-am-He.
The Sanskrit: *deho devālayaḥ proktaḥ svātmā devaḥ sanātanaḥ | tyajedajñānanirmālyaṃ so'haṃ bhāvena pūjayet*

## v.2
Him who is saluted by the hosts of demons and gods, engaged in bestowing wished-for boons, worshipable as foremost in a hundred rites, breath-bodied — Gaṇapati I praise.
The Sanskrit: *asurasuravṛndavanditamabhimatavaravitaraṇe niratam | darśanaśatāgryapūjyaṃ prāṇatanuṃ gaṇapatiṃ vande*

## v.3
Whose foot-pair is worshipped by choice heroes, troops of yoginīs, rows of perfected ones; who removes the pain of humble folk — Vaṭuka, named apāna, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *varavīrayoginīgaṇasiddhāvalipūjitāṅghriyugalam | apahṛtavinayijanārtiṃ vaṭukamapānābhidhaṃ vande*

## v.4
By whose mind-strength the universe shines as Śiva's path for devotees — him, intent in attention, the stainless true guru, always I praise.
The Sanskrit: *yaddhībalena viśvaṃ bhaktānāṃ śivapathaṃ bhāti | tamahamavadhānarūpaṃ sadgurumamalaṃ sadā vande*

## v.5
With feasts of their own sense-objects the sense-goddesses ever fully honor in the heart-lotus — whom, him, made of consciousness, bliss-Bhairava, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *ātmīyaviṣayabhogairindriyadevyaḥ sadā hṛdambhoje | abhipūjayanti yaṃ taṃ cinmayamānandabhairavaṃ vande*

## v.6
The play of the dawning, shining forth, and chewing over of the universe, which she performs unceasingly — bliss-Bhairavī, her, whose form is reflection, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *udayāvabhāsacarvaṇalīlāṃ viśvasya yā karotyaniśam | ānandabhairavīṃ tāṃ vimarśarūpāmahaṃ vande*

## v.7
Who worships Bhairava with flowers of resolve, standing in the petal of the lord — to intellect-formed Brāhmaṇī, to her, I ever bow.
The Sanskrit: *arcayati bhairavaṃ yā niścayakusumaiḥ sureśapatrasthā | praṇamāmi buddhirūpāṃ brahmāṇīṃ tāmahaṃ satatam*

## v.8
She performs Bhairava's worship, standing in fire's petal, with flowers of I-making — ever of I-making form, I praise her, Śāmbhavī the mother.
The Sanskrit: *kurute bhairavapūjāmanaladalasthā'bhimānakusumairyā | nityamahaṅkṛtirūpāṃ vande tāṃ śāmbhavīmambām*
