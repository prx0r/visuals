# Mahopadeshavimshatika mpa-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Homage to the world-formed, whose form is beyond the world — to the ever-divine-shining, to the self of unending power.
The Sanskrit: *prapañcottīrṇarūpāya namaste viśvamūrtaye | sadādivyaprakāśāya svātmane'nantaśaktaye*

## v.2
You alone are you; I alone am I. You alone are; I am not at all. As for you and me both being — where is there homage, homage?
The Sanskrit: *tvaṃ tvamevāhamevāhaṃ tvamevāsi na cāsmyaham | ahaṃ tvamityubhau na sto yatra tasmai namo namaḥ*

## v.3
Inside the body, sought by me always — you as self, and the self is never seen. Whatever is seen is you alone, that.
The Sanskrit: *antardehe mayā nityaṃ tvamātmā ca gaveṣitaḥ | na dṛṣṭastvaṃ nacaivātmā yacca dṛṣṭaṃ tvameva tat*

## v.4
Of your devotee who well knows your form, now again: having seen you as the self's form — to you homage, to me homage.
The Sanskrit: *bhavadbhaktasya sajñātabhavadrūpasyatha me punaḥ | tvāmātmarūpaṃ samprekṣya tubhyaṃ mahyaṃ namo namaḥ*

## v.5
What finesse of going is there, what should be done, thus, from the root? Of yours, made of your magic power — by what, in what, from where is the dissolving?
The Sanskrit: *etadgacananaipuṇyaṃ yatkartavyetimūlayā | bhavanmāyātmanastasya kena kasmin kuto layaḥ*

## v.6
I, you; you, I — thus there is no duality of us anywhere. Only the wish to grasp samādhi: that is what the abiding of difference is.
The Sanskrit: *ahaṃ tvaṃ tvamahaṃ ceti bhinnatā nāvayoḥ kvacit | samādhigrahaṇecchāyā bhedasyāvasthitirhyasau*

## v.7
You, I, and so on — so endless, ever in you, they find no room. Words, then — whence the world?
The Sanskrit: *tvamahaṃ so'yamityādi so'nantāni sadā tvayi | na labhante cāvakāśaṃ vacanāni kuto jagat*

## v.8
Enough of difference-talk. From chewing the sap of devotion to you — all this is one, at peace, though even to say so is shy.
The Sanskrit: *alaṃ bhedānukathayā tvadbhaktirasacarvaṇāt | sarvamekamidaṃ śāntamiti vaktuṃ ca lajjate*

## v.9
When your own form expands — you and I and the whole world. When its withdrawal is born — neither you, nor I, nor indeed the world.
The Sanskrit: *tvatsvarūpe jṛmbhamāṇe tvaṃ cāhaṃ cākhilaṃ jagat | jāte tasya tirodhāne na tvaṃ nāhaṃ na vai jagat*

## v.10
Waking, dreaming, deep sleep and the rest — holding them as your own rays. By your own will you shine like an actor, and yet you are partless in truth.
The Sanskrit: *jāgratasvapnasuṣuptyādyā dhārayaṃśca nijāḥ kalāḥ | svecchayā bhāsi naṭavan niṣkalo'si ca tattvataḥḥ*
