# Paramarthasara param-p04 — L2 READ

## v.29
So dharma and adharma, heaven and hell, birth and death, pleasure and pain — caste, life-stage and the rest — though unreal in the self, arise by delusion's force.
The Sanskrit: *tadvad dharmādharmasvarnirayotpattimaraṇasukhaduḥkham | varṇāśramādi cātmany asad api vibhramabalād bhavati*

## v.30
This is that darkness: shining in things, the conceit 'I am not the self' arises even where the self is unsurpassed.
The Sanskrit: *etat tad andhakāraṃ yad bhāveṣu prakāśamānatayā | ātmānatirikteṣv api bhavaty anātmābhimāno 'yam*

## v.31
Darkness upon darkness — a great burst upon the cheek: taking body, breath and the rest, non-selves, for self.
The Sanskrit: *timirād api timiram idaṃ ga.ṅdasyopari mahān ayaṃ sphoṭaḥ | yad anātmany api dehaprāṇadāv ātmamānitvam*

## v.32
By the yoga of body, breath, reflection, mind, knowing, and sky-expanse, the self wraps itself — a wonder — in a net, like the net-maker.
The Sanskrit: *dehaprāṇavimarśanadhījñānanabhaḥprapañcayogena | ātmānam veṣṭayate citraṃ jālena jālakāra iva*

## v.33
By the yoga that shines with one's own knowing glory, unwrapping one's own self: thus supreme Śiva spreads the wondrous play of bondage and release.
The Sanskrit: *svajñānavibhavabhāsanayogenodveṣṭayen nijātmānam | iti bandhamokṣacitrāṃ krīḍāṃ pratanoti paramaśivaḥ*

## v.34
Creating, keeping, dissolving; waking, dreaming, deep sleep — they shine in him, in the fourth abode, yet unveiled he shines.
The Sanskrit: *sṛṣṭisthitisaṃhārā jāgratsvapnau suṣuptam iti tasmin | bhānti turīye dhāmani tathāpi tair nāvṛtaṃ bhāti*

## v.35
Waking is the universe, through difference; dreaming is splendor, through light's glory; the knower is the sleep state, through massed knowing; beyond that, the fourth.
The Sanskrit: *jāgrad viśvaṃ bhedāt svapnas tejaḥ prakāśamāhātmyāt | prājñaḥ suptāvasthā jñānaghanatvāt tataḥ paraṃ turyam*

## v.36
As cloud, smoke and dust do not soil the sky's surface, so the supreme person stands untouched by Māyā's deformings.
The Sanskrit: *jaladharadhūmarajobhir malinīkriyate yathā na gaganatalam | tadvan māyāvikṛtibhir aparāmṛṣṭaḥ paraḥ puruṣaḥ*

## v.37
When one jar-sky is pervaded by dust, the others do not get soiled: so these souls, enjoying pleasure-pain differences.
The Sanskrit: *ekasmin ghaṭagagane rajasā vyāpte bhavanti nānyāni | malināni tadvad ete jīvāḥ sukhaduḥkhabhedajuṣaḥ*
