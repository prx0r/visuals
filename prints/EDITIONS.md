# KDP Print Research (2026-11-05)

## Design constraints (verified from KDP help pages)
- **Trim:** paperback allows **custom trim** (w 4–8.5", h 6–11.69") → **A5 (5.83×8.27") fits
  and stays under "large trim" (6.12×9") → regular pricing. Hardcover = fixed sizes only
  (no A5) → hardbacks need 5×8 / 5.5×8.5 / 6×9 etc.
- **Margins (24–150pp):** gutter ≥0.375" (9.6mm), outside ≥0.25" (6.4mm). Our pages: 18mm
  sides / 20mm top-bottom → comfortably pass. 151–300pp: gutter 0.5".
- **Fonts:** must be **embedded** (ours are: Tiro Devanagari + Cormorant, both SIL OFL —
  embedding allowed). Min size 7pt (ours ≥8.5pt ✓). Chrome print-to-pdf embeds subsets ✓.
- **Bleed:** text-only interior → no bleed, PDF page = trim exactly (our PDFs ✓). Images ≥300
  DPI; single pages not spreads; no crop marks; ≤650MB.
- **Cover:** separate PDF, CMYK (RGB shifts), 0.125" bleed all round, spine = pages ×
  0.002252" (white/B&W). **Spine text needs ≥0.25" → ≥112pp** (Anuttara II 162pp = 0.365"
  ✓; Practical Prayers 107pp = 0.24" ✗ no spine text; vol1 99pp ✗). Leave 2×1.2" barcode
  block bottom-right back (KDP prints it free). Always regenerate template per page count.
- **Cover finish:** 80lb white stock, gloss or matte (matte suits the prayer books).

## Quality
- B&W toner POD is excellent for text; **white paper** for these books (Devanagari hairlines +
  grey English stay crisp; cream muddies them). Matte cover.
- Interior greys print fine in B&W; avoid large solid-black floods.
- **Always order a physical proof** (~$4 + ship) before publishing — check Devanagari ink.

## Imprint / logo / ISBN — verdict
- **Logo: NOT required.** Free KDP ISBN → imprint shows "Independently published".
- **Imprint (own name): optional; worth it for the series** (Patala Press / Patala Editions?).
  Own imprint requires **own ISBNs** (Bowker: $125×1 or $295×10; UK Nielsen similar) and the
  imprint string must match the ISBN registration **character for character** (case-sensitive).
- Simple mark: one shape, reads at 0.5", B&W first, no text needed on spine; appears on
  copyright page + publisher field.
- Recommended path: **test with free ISBN first; buy the ISBN block when committing** —
  changing later means re-issuing the book.
- Etsy PDF e-book: no ISBN, no logo needed.

## Platform rules
- **Do NOT enroll in KDP Select** (optional) — Select makes the digital edition Kindle-exclusive,
  which conflicts with selling the PDF on Etsy. Print books don't need it.
- Kindle ebook optional; print = KDP paperback/hardcover with free barcode + free KDP ISBN.
- 2 categories + 10 keywords; Author Central + A+ content free.

## Part 5 — Edition ladder & pricing tiers (owner ruling, 2026-11-05)

**Three editions per work:**

| Edition | Content | Ref level | Page target | Minimum-list estimates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **English** | translation only | minimal (numbers, no apparatus) | ≤110 flat tier | **$4.60** / £3.86 / €4.10 |
| **Study** | Sanskrit (Deva+IAST) + English | spread format (current prayer-book style) | ≤110 for verse works; priced-to-size for big ones | ≤110: **$4.60** · 250pp: $8.00 · 300pp: $9.20 |
| **Complete** | Study + our essays (intros, c1 essays, glossary, notes) | full | whatever it honestly needs | 400pp: **$9.99** · 500pp: $11.67 · 600pp: $13.67 |

**Stance (owner): sell as close to cost as the platform permits.** The minimum
list price must cover the printing cost after the applicable royalty rate.
Thresholds and rounding can leave a small positive royalty even at that minimum;
the reader's price is not the same as the printer's cost.

### KDP cost curve (Amazon.com, black ink, regular trim, verified 2026-09/11)
- ≤110pp: **$2.30 flat** · 111pp+: **$1.00 + $0.012/page** (each page above 110 = +1.2¢)
- 150pp=$2.80 · 200=$3.40 · 300=$4.60 · 400=$5.80 · 500=$7.00 · 600=$8.20
- UK: £1.93 flat → £0.85+£0.010/pp · EU: €2.05 flat → €0.75+€0.012/pp
- Royalty: **60% ≥ $9.99 / £7.99 / €9.99, 50% below** (rate × list − print)
- Min list (floor) = print÷0.5, or max(threshold, print÷0.6) once past the 50% band

### Minimum list estimates (royalty may be positive at a rate threshold)
| Pages | US print | US floor | UK floor | EU floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≤110 | $2.30 | **$4.60** | £3.86 | €4.10 |
| 200 | $3.40 | $6.80 | £5.70 | €6.30 |
| 300 | $4.60 | $9.20 | £7.70 | €8.70 |
| 400 | $5.80 | $9.99 | £8.09 | €9.99 |
| 500 | $7.00 | $11.67 | £9.75 | €11.25 |
| 600 | $8.20 | $13.67 | £11.42 | €13.25 |

For example, at 400 pages in the US, 60% of $9.99 minus $5.80 is about
$0.19, not $0. Confirm minimum list prices and rounding in KDP's calculator.

### Traps
1. Never price at $9.98/£7.98/€9.98 — one penny under the threshold flips to 50%.
2. Floors are per-marketplace — set each minimum manually at upload (Territories = All).
3. 110 is an optimization, not a law: royalty stays ~$6–7 across a size-priced ladder
   ($14.99@110 / $17.99@250 / $19.99@400); don't mutilate a book to hide under 110.
4. True "reader pays print only" is impossible on KDP (Amazon keeps its half) — the
   Etsy PDF is the genuine at-cost channel.

## CONTENT SOURCING LAW (canonical for prayer books — owner, 2026-11-05)

**You never write any of it yourself. Every block is EXTRACT-AND-PLACE from the
translation layers, then clarity-edits only** (shorten, un-stiffen, fix flow —
never new claims, never new instructions, never your own theology).

| Block | Extracts from |
|---|---|
| brief | C1 essence line / argmap tagline / L2 summary sentence |
| What the prayer does | ARGMAP statements + L2 clause sense (≤60w) |
| How to perform it | the text's OWN practice lines in T1/L2 (occasion, actions, order, count) |
| How to know it's working | claimed effects: C1 effect claims, efficacy lore, witness (Swamiji) effects, rasa tags |
| What to feel / visualise | C1 rasa block / craft_notes feelings — present or absent, never half |

Rules:
- `en` translation is never touched in scaffolding passes (witness-verified).
- Caps: brief ≈100 chars · what ≤60w · how ≤40w · sign/feel ≤30w — keeps every
  guide to one page, book at 44/43 guides, ≤110pp.
- If a block has no extractable layer source → it stays OFF the page (fail
  closed); ask for the layer upstream instead of filling the gap yourself.
- Signs authored 2026-11-05 (pre-law) are provisional: each batch pass
  re-anchors them to a named layer extract or flags them.
- Provenance stays on the entry (source_path · source_ref), integrity-checked
  by bookfactory `check.py` (M1).
