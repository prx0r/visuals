The Recognition

57 questions
What actually changes when someone has a recognition?
The content of experience stays identical. What changes is the observer's relation to its own dependence — a single status flip, not new information.FORMALIZATION-OF-RECOGNITION.md
Is recognition the same as learning a new fact?
No. It 'subtracts a false exemption rather than adding a new object' — you drop a wrong assumption, you don't gain information.FORMALIZATION-OF-RECOGNITION.md
What are the three malas?
Three named failure modes: treating yourself as self-grounding, treating the world as independently real, and locking into habits that keep those errors running.FORMALIZATION-OF-RECOGNITION.md
Does recognition destroy your body and limits?
No. It removes exclusive self-enclosure, not finite embodiment. Body, limits, and private field stay; only the claim that they're exhaustive goes.FORMALIZATION-OF-RECOGNITION.md
Is recognition a felt experience?
Yes — the thesis is that removing the false self-positioning is felt. The feeling registers the removal; it doesn't prove what the removal reveals.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-FELT-RE-COGNITION.md
What survives the critique of Buddhism in this essay?
Most Buddhist schools are refuted for assuming a substrate. Two survive — Ñāṇananda and Ñāṇavīra — who claim only dependence, never an underlying substance.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-FELT-RE-COGNITION.md
What's the difference between a stream-enterer and an arahant?
The stream-enterer sees the deception — the 'I am' conceit as wrong. The arahant has removed it. Seeing is not enacting.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-FELT-RE-COGNITION.md
Does the essay say nibbāna and Śiva-recognition are identical?
No. Both share the removal of false self-positioning, but they differ in what the removal reveals — ground or groundlessness. That choice stays open.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-FELT-RE-COGNITION.md
What happens after recognition?
Perception re-renders. Recognition is the threshold; the real work is the long restructuring of perception, memory, agency, and feeling that follows.POST-RECOGNITION.md
Why call it 're-rendering'?
Because perception is a construction — a controlled hallucination. Recognition updates the self's predictive model, so the experienced world gets rebuilt.POST-RECOGNITION.md
Is this just mysticism?
No. The document maps it onto neural annealing, plasticity, active inference, and psychedelics — a cognitive-science claim, not a supernatural one.POST-RECOGNITION.md
What's this project's distinct angle?
Others study the recognition event. This project studies the aftermath — what recognition does to perception over time, in the world.POST-RECOGNITION.md
What are the research-hook tags for?
They flag each verse's point as touching recognition, felt quality, reflexivity, memory, freedom, or otherness, so the main paper can quote it.IPVV-PLAIN-ENGLISH-BRIDGE.md
What are the five kañcukas?
Five dimensions of finite constraint: limited agency, knowledge, desire, time, and place/cause. Five aspects of one contraction, not five separate things.aperture/pratyabhijna-formal.md
Is a finite mind a filtered piece of a universal one?
No — the correction: subject, body, objects, and constraints are all self-determinations of one conscious power, not parts passed through an external filter.recognition formulation
What's the difference between limitation and bondage?
Limitation makes a perspective possible. Bondage is treating that perspective as an independent, complete self. You can be limited without being bound.recognition formulation
Does recognition just change a belief?
No — it changes the whole subject-world relation. Not 'I now believe I am universal consciousness,' but perception, body-use, action, and otherness all reorganize.recognition formulation
Can recognition happen instantly?
Yes — it's a re-identification, like recognizing a stranger as someone you knew. Content barely changes; the identity relation flips.recognitionexpounded
What exactly does recognition change?
The self-boundary is reclassified from absolute to functional: from 'the edge of subjectivity itself' to 'the edge of my finite access, memory, and action.'recognitionexpounded
What is vimarśa here?
Reflexivity with four jobs: mirroring self, mirroring object, synthesizing both into one conscious event, and fitting it into time. Not just 'mind knows itself.'recognitionexpounded
Is there a rival explanation of the same experience?
Yes — a biological reading: the change is decentring of the organism's self-model. Both predict the same effects; only the Śaiva reading claims one universal consciousness.recognitionexpounded
What separates a real recognition from a profound experience?
Four conditions: transformation, reidentification, veridicality (it's true), and stabilization (it holds). Drop truth and it's conversion, not recognition.recognitionandsiva
If I feel one with everything, is that Śiva-recognition?
Only if five axioms hold — one common ground, that ground is one subject, all is internal to it, your field is its mode, it's free. Otherwise it's Śaiva-form self-transformation.recognitionandsiva
What does recognition remove?
Exclusive identification, not embodiment. Body, limits, and private field remain; what goes is the claim that your standpoint is the whole of you.recognitionandsiva
Is liberation gaining something new?
No. It's recognizing an identity that already obtains — like remembering a face. Nothing is added; the Self's manifestation was never interrupted.recognition.md
Why isn't recognition an inference?
An inference builds a new belief. Recognition returns an identity you already have but weren't attending to — the arguments just redirect attention: 'See! See!'recognition.md
What are the four components of valid recognition?
Transformation, reidentification, veridicality (true about the same thing), and stabilization (it doesn't get cancelled by the next impression).recognition.md
What is dṛḍhavimarśa?
Firm, un-cancelled reflective awareness — the difference between a passing glimpse and the recognition that counts as liberation.recognition.md
Is it a separate sect?
No — a philosophical current intersecting the Trika, with no separate ritual or initiation lineage. Its contribution is conceptual, not ritual.school-pratyabhijna.md
What does this schema do?
It maps one formalization vocabulary onto others — IIT, PyPhi, Dharmakīrti, global workspace theory, free energy — so claims built on them stay consistent.FORMALISATION_SCHEMA.md
What counts as real here?
Following Dharmakīrti: a thing is real exactly if it has causal efficacy. Only particulars with causal power exist; universals are conceptual.FORMALISATION_SCHEMA.md
How can something that can't be named ever be known?
It cannot be named. Anyone who reaches it at all does so by svasaṃvedana (honestly feeling oneself) — no other method.svabodhasiddhi v.7

When your own thought is gripped still, śakti (the power) rests in the śaktimat (holder of power). Their oneness is what yogis are after.svabodhasiddhi v.12

Know your svarūpa (own form), drop the tattvakañcuka (veil of principles), and scatter the cause of striving. Then you are called free.svabodhasiddhi v.13

One self, twofold — measured and unmeasured. Held by breath and the rest it is an atom; as paramātman (supreme self) it is unbroken.ajadapramatrsiddhi v.16

Continuity misses relation entirely — if it counted, it would hold between two iron bars. So relation means fusion of the many's forms; that alone is aikātmya (one-selfhood).sambandhasiddhi v.5
How can two things be joined without either losing itself?
The ṣaṣṭhī (genitive case) relation settles in the qualifier: reaching the qualified-oneness, it does not abandon its own form.sambandhasiddhi v.18
What has this whole teaching been for?
Self-awareness-proved: a saṃbandha (means of relation) unrefuted and useful — the full apparatus for pratyabhijñā (recognition).sambandhasiddhi v.23
Is mind the same thing as pure knowing?
Yes. Caitanya (pure knowing) is the self's form — stainless, utterly stainless. That same knowing, bound by niyama (rule), is called mind.bodhavilasa v.6
What is this teaching called, and what does it do?
Jñānāmṛta (nectar of knowledge), unsurpassed. Whoever drinks it is quenched — his fever of saṃsāra-poison subsides.bodhavilasa v.41
What keeps an all-knowing being from knowing everything?
Five coverings: kañcuka (the sheath) of art, knowing, passion, time and necessity — each makes him do or know only a little. They veil His own form.parapravesika par-p08
Why does the proof use a crystal as its example?
A crystal reflects light but isn't aware of it, so it's inert. Conscious light differs because it feels itself while showing things. Without that felt self-presence, light would be just a mirror — so the felt part is required, not added.ESSAY-ULTIMATE-PROOF-OF-RECOGNITION.md
If recognition adds nothing, why does it feel like something?
The content stays the same; your position in it flips from 'a thing among things' to 'the subject.' That re-positioning is felt as joy (camatkāra: a savouring). The feeling is the register of the flip, not new information.ESSAY-WHY-RECOGNITION-IS-FELT.md
Why call it 're'-cognition if nothing is new?
Because the self was never absent ('I was never not'). Nothing could be added to an eternal self, so recognition can only be a return — knowing again what was always there but mis-positioned.ESSAY-WHY-RECOGNITION-IS-FELT.md
Does recognition change what you see?
No. The field content is identical; only the self-positioning flips (from object to subject). The felt joy isn't extra content either — it's the same field with a different valence.ESSAY-WHY-RECOGNITION-IS-FELT.md
What does the lover image explain?
The lover was always near; unrecognized, he can't be enjoyed. Recognition doesn't bring him — it turns near-but-unrecognized into near-and-enjoyed, and that turn is exactly the felt part.ESSAY-WHY-RECOGNITION-IS-FELT.md
Why need a teacher if the truth is already there?
The guru doesn't hand over new content; he points, and the pointing triggers the recognition. Book study without the living lineage is content without the pointing — and recognition is the turning, not the information.ESSAY-SOTERIOLOGY-OF-RECOGNITION.md
Where exactly do the two disagree?
Nisargadatta refuses to posit any ground behind the felt 'I am' ('not this, not that'). The IPVV argues for a specific ground: the order-less support, the Lord. Same starting felt, different stopping point.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-NISARGADATTA.md
Does Nisargadatta accept the 'one universal self' step?
No. He agrees on the felt, the return, and the removal of false identification, then stops before the posited Lord — exactly where the rivals decline the universalization.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-NISARGADATTA.md
Why compare a 20th-century Bombay sage to a 10th-century text?
Nisargadatta gives the felt side its clearest modern voice; the IPVV gives that felt side a systematic argument. One says 'see,' the other shows why the seeing is possible.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-NISARGADATTA.md
Why does ordered experience need an 'order-less' support?
Awareness flashes through the world's order but isn't itself ordered — you can't have sequences without something they appear in. That something must stand outside the order it shows.ESSAY-FELT-TO-GROUND.md
Is this just an assertion of 'one light'?
No. The essay says the support is derived: order is given, order requires an order-less support, and memory confirms that support is a knower. The gap to 'one support for all fields' is narrowed, not closed.ESSAY-FELT-TO-GROUND.md
What does memory add to the argument?
Memory re-grasps the prior experience with the same knower — 'that alone, not another.' So the support isn't merely order-less; it's the one self-conscious knower holding the field together across time.ESSAY-FELT-TO-GROUND.md
What does this prove for recognition?
If the 'I' were built, recognition could only build a new one. Since it isn't built, recognition re-positions a self-grasp that was always there. It defeats the self-model but doesn't prove all 'I's are one — that's separate.ESSAY-NONCONSTRUCTED-I.md
What is 'exclusion' and why is it one of the three powers?
Exclusion (apohana: defining by what it is not) — to say 'this is a pot' is the same act as excluding 'not a pot.' It's the positive power that makes concepts and self-knowledge possible.ESSAY-EXCLUSION-INDIVIDUATION.md
How are aesthetics and the recognition philosophy connected?
Same doctrine, two jobs. In the IPVV the felt (vimarśa) is consciousness's essence; in the Abhinavabhāratī the felt (rasa) is the substance of aesthetic delight. The essay: both are the same 'wonder' (camatkāra).ESSAY-RASA-IPVV-ABHINAVABHARATI.md
How is watching a drama like achieving recognition?
Both reposition without changing content. Rasa shifts the emotion from 'mine' to 'universal'; recognition shifts the self from 'a thing among things' to 'the primary thing' — and both changes are felt as joy.ESSAY-RASA-IPVV-ABHINAVABHARATI.md

Proof & Doubt

16 questions
Is this system presented as proven?
No. It's a metaphysical hypothesis — coherent and textually grounded, but not provable within the system and not science.aperture/pratyabhijna-formal.md
Can an argument force you to recognize?
No. Consciousness is absolute freedom (svātantrya), so it can only be compelled if it agrees. Recognition can be shown, never coerced.recognition.md
Does this prove recognition is true?
No. It's a consistency schema for formal claims — it defines primitives and validation rules, not a proof of any doctrine.FORMALISATION_SCHEMA.md
Who is praised before the argument even starts?
The one God — Maheśvara (the great Lord). A single maker, praised in one line.isvarasiddhi v.1
What conclusion does the verse draw from all this variety?
Conscious makers are proved, so the Lord's act is proved. To anyone who knows such variety, anīśitā (lordlessness) is impossible.isvarasiddhi v.7
Why do some people still not accept the proof?
A thousand blazing reasons prove it, yet it does not succeed for deluded minds. Maheśvara sits on your palm and still runs from the fate-struck — like a true jewel.isvarasiddhi v.55
How do many separate things come to be seen as one?
By resting in the reflection of that one, through the force of one-word superimposition. The many reach unity, the way 'pot' and 'cloth' are said together.sambandhasiddhi v.13
Does the Lord need to be proved?
No. Proofs cannot grip Him, proved before all proof. Svaprakāśatva (self-shining) is proved for everyone by feeling oneself — the questioner's own shape is the proof.parapravesika par-p05
Can recognition actually be proved, or is it just believed?
Not by formal logic — that cannot force a universal consciousness. The IPVV gives a five-step argument from the structure of experience; the last step (all supports are one Lord) is argued but not forced, it's the tradition's commitment.ESSAY-ULTIMATE-PROOF-OF-RECOGNITION.md
How does memory prove there's only one knower?
Memory doesn't bring anything new; it re-grasps what was already grasped. That needs the same knower continuing across time — so without one knower there's no memory, just two disconnected experiences.ESSAY-ULTIMATE-PROOF-OF-RECOGNITION.md
What's the weak spot in the proof?
Step 5. Steps 1–4 only prove each field needs its own order-less knower. That all those supports are numerically one Lord is argued, not forced — the essay names it plainly as the wager.ESSAY-ULTIMATE-PROOF-OF-RECOGNITION.md
What can formal logic actually prove about recognition?
Two things: the σ-flip (recognition changes self-positioning, not content) and the exclusion (subject and world carved by one operation). It cannot prove one universal consciousness — that step needs premises beyond logic.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-FORMAL-PROOFS.md
Do the formal proofs contradict the IPVV?
No, they're complementary. The formal work proves structure (the σ-flip, the exclusion) and marks the limit (logic can't reach the universal); the IPVV confirms that structure and adds the argument plus the felt register.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-FORMAL-PROOFS.md
So what is missing from the formal proofs?
Three things: the argument for the order-less support, the proof that the 'I' isn't a construction, and the felt face of the σ-flip. Formal work says what's structurally true; the IPVV says why it holds.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-FORMAL-PROOFS.md
Does feeling prove there's one universal consciousness?
Not by itself. The argument proves each ordered, memory-bearing experience needs an order-less knower. That all those knowers are numerically one is argued further but ultimately committed to, not forced.ESSAY-FELT-TO-GROUND.md
What does the essay admit the IPVV can't prove?
That all order-less supports are ONE. The argument shows each ordered experience needs a ground — not that every field's ground is the same one. That final step is the tradition's wager.ESSAY-C-RAZOR-IPVV-AUTHORITY.md
III

The Self

6 questions
Why does contracted consciousness feel trapped?
Three malas: the contraction feels incomplete, the subject-object split makes things feel external, and past habits shape the present.aperture/pratyabhijna-formal.md
How can there be one Śiva and many private minds?
Identity of ground, not of mode or token. Same universal subject articulated differently — your phenomenal field is not mine.recognitionandsiva
Can a self-model explain the feeling of 'mine'?
A model explains what you believe about yourself, not the awareness that the model is yours. Representation is construction; the 'I'-grasp is what isn't constructed — so the model can't be its root.ESSAY-NONCONSTRUCTED-I.md
How do subject and world get created together?
One exclusion operation carves both poles at once: your subject is a set of 'this and not that' boundaries, and the world's objects are determined by those same exclusions. Two poles, one act.ESSAY-EXCLUSION-INDIVIDUATION.md
Why can't you infer someone else's mind from their speech?
The utterance doesn't label itself 'Maitra's intent' — you'd need a further distinction that isn't in the bare sound. Only the aimed action is a wish's effect, and a mere utterance shows no aim.ESSAY-OTHER-INTERSUBJECTIVITY.md
What's the unsolved problem in this account?
The decomposition problem: if all is one self, why are fields opaque — why can't I feel your pain without discipline? The IPVV names māyā as the cause but describes, not explains, the privacy.ESSAY-OTHER-INTERSUBJECTIVITY.md

Even within bheda (real difference) the shining holds good. So pratyabhijñā (recognition) says: this world is made of that one nondual.ajadapramatrsiddhi v.27

Even those called erring are, moment by moment, grounded in svasaṃvitti (endless self-awareness) — reflection that never runs out.sambandhasiddhi v.22

Inside hṛdaya-bīja (the heart-seed), as the whole banyan stands in its seed. Whoever truly knows that heart-seed is initiated in truth, and is free while living.parapravesika par-p09

Isn't the 'I' just a self-model your brain builds?

Joining distinct things (molasses + curd + pepper), turning one into many, and splitting off a doubted other. All are this-and-that operations. The 'I'-grasp is none of them, so it can't be a construction.ESSAY-NONCONSTRUCTED-I.md

Reason establishes the self and action but can't grasp the whole world. So the āgama (revealed teaching) follows, supplying the categories reason can't reach — sequential partners, not rivals.ESSAY-ONTOLOGY-SOTERIOLOGY.md
What are the kañcukas?
Five limiting 'armours' over the pure self: fixity, time, attachment, limited knowledge, limited agency. They're real — the world isn't an illusion — but they're coverings, not the self's essence.ESSAY-ONTOLOGY-SOTERIOLOGY.md
How can a timeless God make a sequenced world?
Like a mirror: one undivided mirror shows countless reflections in order without being torn apart. The one is undivided, so it can host sequence without being affected by it.ESSAY-EPISTEMOLOGY-OF-ACTION.md
What does 'the will is cause, agent, and action' mean?
One will-to-do ('I will make this') binds agent and object together — not external contact. Cause, doer, and deed are all that single will, by which the Lord becomes the world.ESSAY-EPISTEMOLOGY-OF-ACTION.md
How do we infer the same things if each mind is private?
Because the Lord creates the appearances once for all knowers — the fire-appearance isn't split between separate streams. So smoke-implying-fire holds across people.ESSAY-EPISTEMOLOGY-OF-ACTION.md
What is bondage in the recognition school?
Misreading. The differences between things are real: they're the self's own reflection. Bondage is wrongly reading those differences as external, independent objects. Liberation removes the misreading, not the world.ESSAY-ADVAITA-IPVV.md
How does the IPVV flip the Buddhist dream argument?
It turns the example around. The Buddhist says dreams show the world is unreal. Abhinavagupta agrees dreams lack real causes, then says that proves consciousness creates whole object-worlds by will alone, with no material cause.ESSAY-BUDDHIST-IPVV.md
What does 'causality is the conscious knower's agency' mean?
Cause-and-effect is really agent-and-object of action. An inert thing can't cause anything — a seed doesn't hand being to a sprout. What shapes the effect is a knowing will.ESSAY-ABHINAVA-EXTENSIONS.md
How can a timeless Lord make a world full of sequences?
Like a mirror: one face shows countless reflections without stopping being one. Being undivided is exactly what lets the timeless show sequences at all.ESSAY-ABHINAVA-EXTENSIONS.md
Is the felt 'I' just a mental construction?
The IPVV argues no: constructions join distinct things, multiply a one, or split off a doubted other — and the plain 'I'-grasp does none of those. It's the self-presence constructions rest on.ESSAY-C-RAZOR-IPVV-AUTHORITY.md
VI

Practice & Freedom

9 questions
What is the actual practice for reaching the goal?
Stay awake in your own svabodha (self-awakening). From such waking the sage stands whole and undivided — that is the whole way.svabodhasiddhi v.9
Is liberation hard to win?
Not for yogis grounded in themselves whose single mind sweeps aside the traces of grasping and the grasped. For them mokṣalakṣmī (fortune's release) is not hard to gain.svabodhasiddhi v.20
Are the stages of a real spiritual path fixed?
They are kṛtrima (stages made up): as an actor assumes roles by his own will, so the bliss-will-spread self assumes vast views-and-powers.bodhavilasa v.12
Why do these essays say effort-based yoga is useless?
Because bondage is a misreading, not a missing ingredient. You can't add the self (it was never absent) or erase a misreading by stacking effort — only recognition (pratyabhijñā: seeing what was always true) removes it.ESSAY-SOTERIOLOGY-OF-RECOGNITION.md
What is a jīvanmukta?
Someone liberated while still living in the body — still acting in the world, but the false identification is gone. Liberation is a removal of a misreading, not a new state, so it needs no destroyed body.ESSAY-SOTERIOLOGY-OF-RECOGNITION.md
Is being finite the same as being trapped?
No. The exclusions are the real, necessary architecture of any perspective. Bondage is identifying with them — believing your finite standpoint is all you are. A liberated person keeps the senses and loses that conviction.ESSAY-EXCLUSION-INDIVIDUATION.md
How do the two schools differ on what liberation is?
Advaita liberates by knowledge that erases the illusion, leaving the world behind. The recognition school liberates by recognition: you stay in the world, free, seeing it as yourself — the jīvanmukta, one liberated while still living.ESSAY-ADVAITA-IPVV.md
What's the difference in practice between the two schools?
Spanda asks you to cultivate the pulse and rest in the Present; the IPVV says recognition alone liberates, effort-yogas useless. The essay: the practice prepares the recognition.ESSAY-SPANDA-IPVV.md
Is rasa a dry run for liberation?
The essay says yes: rasa is a temporary de-identification — tasting delight without grabbing the particular — and liberation is that same shift made permanent.ESSAY-RASA-IPVV-ABHINAVABHARATI.md
VII

Against Other Schools

8 questions
Is there really a difference between living and non-living things?
The verse says no. Just as there is no difference between the real and the unreal in their own nature, so there is none between jaḍa (utterly inert) and the non-inert.ajadapramatrsiddhi v.1
If everything is really undifferentiated, why do some things work and others don't?
The existent performs arthakriyā (a real function) and the nonexistent does not. Yet in its own self the thing remains without distinction.ajadapramatrsiddhi v.6
Who is praised, and what kind of relation is his?
Śiva, praised by his own will. His saṃbandha (means of relation) to things is difference-and-non-difference at once, and by it all things are proved — his work is world-traffic.sambandhasiddhi v.1
How is this different from Nisargadatta's 'You are that'?
They agree on the core: the felt 'I am' is primary, nothing is added, the self was never absent, drop the false identification. They split on the ground — Nisargadatta only points; the IPVV posits and argues the one Lord.ESSAY-RECOGNITION-AND-NISARGADATTA.md
What's the difference between Advaita Vedanta and the recognition school?
Both say reality is one consciousness. Advaita says the world is an illusion caused by an unexplainable ignorance; the recognition school says the world is real — the self's own reflection — and only our reading of it as separate is wrong.ESSAY-ADVAITA-IPVV.md
Why does the IPVV say Advaita's 'unexplainable ignorance' is incoherent?
Because nobody owns it. One Brahman can't have it — there's nothing else for it to be absent from — and the souls can't have it, since the ignorance is supposed to create them. 'Unexplainable' just refuses to answer.ESSAY-ADVAITA-IPVV.md
How do the Buddhist mind-only school and the recognition school differ?
They start from the same place — only appearances are given to us. The Buddhist reads them as unreal, empty, and momentary; the Śaiva reads them as the real self-expression of consciousness. Same data, opposite verdict.ESSAY-BUDDHIST-IPVV.md
Is the IPVV's 'co-opting' of Buddhist tools honest?
Partly. It validly blocks the Buddhist's negative conclusion: dream, inference, and self-awareness don't prove unreality. But the leap to 'one universal creator' isn't entailed — the essay flags that as smuggled.ESSAY-BUDDHIST-IPVV.md

Scripture & Authority

Why does this text need a plain-English bridge?
The literal translations are word-faithful but unreadable. The bridge answers 'what is this actually saying?' in 1-3 clean sentences per verse.IPVV-PLAIN-ENGLISH-BRIDGE.md
Who uses the bridge's output?
The main paper quotes the plain-English renderings, the Patala workbooks use them as teaching text, and scholarship gets checked against them.IPVV-PLAIN-ENGLISH-BRIDGE.md
Who founded the Recognition school?
Three thinkers in about a century: Somānanda (c. 900), Utpaladeva (c. 925–975), Abhinavagupta (c. 975–1025), all in Kashmir.school-pratyabhijna.md
What is the founding text?
Utpaladeva's Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā ('Verses on the Recognition of the Lord') with his own commentary; Abhinavagupta later wrote the massive IPVV commentary.school-pratyabhijna.md
Why is this school a 'control corpus'?
It defines the key terms — prakāśa, vimarśa, svātantrya, recognition — precisely, and the other schools (Trika, Spanda, Krama) inherit those meanings.school-pratyabhijna.md
What everyday example does the verse use to prove a maker?
A pot needs a potter. Without the potter, the verse says, not even a pot could come about.isvarasiddhi v.28
What exactly is an error, according to this verse?
Not something that merely resembles a shining. It is following after a shining — ābhāsa (a shining astray) is exactly what error is.sambandhasiddhi v.8
Can something unconscious actually cause anything?
The IPVV argues no: a dead seed can't give the sprout being — the sprout is either already there (nothing gained) or not there (no nature to gain existence). Real causation is the conscious knower's agency.ESSAY-EPISTEMOLOGY-OF-ACTION.md
What is the Krama school?
A Kashmir Śaivism school built on sequence (krama): consciousness as the ordered flow of cognition. Its twelve Kālīs are the powers of sensing, thinking, and remembering dressed as goddesses — not separate deities.ESSAY-KRAMA-IPVV.md
How can the source of time itself not be temporal?
Both schools say the ground of sequence isn't inside the sequence — like one who knows an order without being trapped in it. You can't have ordered experience without an order-less supporter behind it.ESSAY-KRAMA-IPVV.md
Why do both schools focus on the Present moment?
The past has stopped, the future hasn't arrived — only the Present is actually real. Both make it the practice spot: the gap where the order-less ground meets time.ESSAY-KRAMA-IPVV.md
Are the Kālīs separate goddesses?
No. They're the powers of the cognition-process dressed as goddesses — senses, thoughts, moments of knowing. The IPVV calls the same things powers (knowing, remembering, excluding). Two vocabularies, one process.ESSAY-KRAMA-IPVV.md
What is the 'Fourth' (turya)?
Not a fourth state after waking, dreaming, and deep sleep. It's the witness present through all three — what stays constant while the states change. The IPVV's order-less support is the argued version.ESSAY-SIVASUTRA-VB-IPVV.md
What are the Vijñānabhairava's 112 ways?
112 short meditation techniques — using breath, senses, or states — that turn attention from the thing noticed to the awareness noticing it. Each repositions attention; none adds new content.ESSAY-SIVASUTRA-VB-IPVV.md
How do the Śivasūtra, Vijñānabhairava, and IPVV relate?
One teaching at three levels: the Śivasūtra points ('consciousness is the Self'), the Vijñānabhairava gives 112 practices, the IPVV argues the case against rival schools. Statement, practice, proof.ESSAY-SIVASUTRA-VB-IPVV.md
What does 'Spanda' mean?
Vibration. The Spanda school holds consciousness isn't a still lamp but a pulse — an active throb that is its very being, and the source of both the world's appearing and its return.ESSAY-SPANDA-IPVV.md
How are the Spanda school and the recognition school related?
Two faces of one claim. Spanda is the dynamic face (consciousness as pulse); the recognition is the reflexive face (consciousness as self-grasp). The IPVV's 'throbbing' (sphurattā) is explicitly named as both.ESSAY-SPANDA-IPVV.md
Abhinavagupta wrote two big books — how do they differ?
The Tantrāloka states the whole system across 37 chapters — ritual, cosmology, aesthetics — often asserting key steps. The IPVV argues every step as objection-and-reply against rivals. One states; one proves.ESSAY-TWO-ABHINAVAGUPTAS-IPVV-TANTRALOKA.md
Which of Abhinavagupta's books handles the six hard problems better?
The IPVV, on four of six (universality, memory and identity, the levels of reality, what recognition changes), and it sharpens a fifth (karma vs. freedom). It's built to argue, so it supplies proofs the Tantrāloka only asserts.ESSAY-TWO-ABHINAVAGUPTAS-IPVV-TANTRALOKA.md
Where do both of Abhinavagupta's texts hit a wall?
The leap from 'my experience needs an order-less ground' to 'all grounds are one consciousness.' Neither book proves the 'all one' part — both just commit to it. The essay calls this the shared wall.ESSAY-TWO-ABHINAVAGUPTAS-IPVV-TANTRALOKA.md
What do the two Abhinavagupta texts agree on?
Five load-bearing claims: the felt is the essence of consciousness; the powers are the self unfolding; the three impurities are the bondage; recognition removes rather than gains; and the world's difference is real, not ignorance.ESSAY-TWO-ABHINAVAGUPTAS-IPVV-TANTRALOKA.md
What did Abhinavagupta add that Utpaladeva didn't?
Four big regions Utpaladeva's text leaves bare: a theory of action (causality = a conscious knower's agency), the 'throbbing' (sphurattā), language as the self-grasp (parā-vāk), and Lordship = self-awareness — plus the categories and the path.ESSAY-ABHINAVA-EXTENSIONS.md
Who was the real innovator, Utpaladeva or Abhinavagupta?
Utpaladeva. Modern scholarship shows he's the real innovator — even the felt 'wonder' (camatkāra) is his concept. Abhinavagupta's contribution was different: he systematized the seed into a system.ESSAY-ABHINAVA-VS-UTPALADEVA.md
What exactly did Abhinavagupta add to Utpaladeva's text?
Three identifications — self-awareness = the supreme Word, = the throbbing, = the Lordship — plus a full theory of action, the world's categories, and the liberation path. Utpaladeva states; Abhinava builds.ESSAY-ABHINAVA-VS-UTPALADEVA.md
What is a 'crux' in these comparison essays?
A hard problem both of Abhinavagupta's books must face — like how 'appears to me' becomes 'the universal perceiver,' or whether memory proves an eternal self. Six of them organize the comparison.ESSAY-IPVV-VS-TANTRALOKA.md
How does the IPVV answer the objection that memory needs no eternal self?
Mechanical causes can explain a memory's content, but not the 'I' serving it: constructions join or split parts, and the plain 'I'-grasp does neither. It's the self-presence all building rests on.ESSAY-IPVV-VS-TANTRALOKA.md
What are the IPVV's three levels of truth?
Real (satya), conventionally-true (saṃvṛtisatya) — real but not substantial, like curds and milk from one cow — and untrue (asatya). The middle level replaces the Tantrāloka's cruder real/illusory split.ESSAY-IPVV-VS-TANTRALOKA.md
Why does it matter that both texts fail at the same point?
If only one book failed, that'd be a gap. Both hitting the identical wall — the leap to one universal consciousness — shows it's a chosen commitment, not a botched proof.ESSAY-IPVV-VS-TANTRALOKA.md
What is rasa, in plain terms?
The tasted flavor of a play. The spectator doesn't just feel the character's fear as his own — the emotion is universalized, and he savors fear-as-such. That tasting is delight.ESSAY-RASA-IPVV-ABHINAVABHARATI.md
What is recognition, according to the IPVV?
The self, already there, recognizing itself as the Lord — the removal of a false self-positioning, nothing added. It changes where you stand, not what you know.ESSAY-C-RAZOR-IPVV-AUTHORITY.md
