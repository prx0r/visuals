# PRICING.md — KDP page/print/floor reference (verified 2026-11, black ink, white paper, regular trim)

Source: KDP help (Paperback Printing Cost / Paperback Royalty / Print Book Pricing
Page), cross-checked Sept–Nov 2026. Royalty = rate × list − print.

## THE AIM RULE (owner, 2026-11-05)
**Target ~100pp or ~300pp. Nothing in between, nothing beyond.**
- **~100pp**: print $2.30 flat → at-cost **$4.60**. English + Study verse books.
- **~300pp**: print $4.60 → at-cost **$9.20**. Complete / essay books.
- 333–416pp is a bonus zone (floor $9.99, royalty slightly positive) — if a book
  lands there naturally, fine; **never pad content to reach it, never split to dodge it**.
- >420pp: only if the work honestly demands it (floor = print/0.60).

## Print cost — two bands (Amazon.com)
| Pages | Print |
|---|---|
| 24–110 | **$2.30 flat** |
| 111–828 | **$1.00 + $0.012/page** |

UK: £1.93 flat → £0.85 + £0.010/pp · EU: €2.05 flat → €0.75 + €0.012/pp

## Royalty rate thresholds (list price, tax-exclusive)
| | 50% rate | 60% rate |
|---|---|---|
| US | ≤ $9.98 | ≥ **$9.99** |
| UK | ≤ £7.98 | ≥ **£7.99** |
| EU | ≤ €9.98 | ≥ **€9.99** |

Expanded Distribution = 40% always (usually near-zero at floor prices).

## Floor tiers (at-cost list = royalty $0) — US
Floor = print ÷ 0.50, except once past $9.98 → max($9.99, print ÷ 0.60).
| Tier | Pages | Print | Floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | 24–110 | $2.30 | **$4.60** |
| F2 | 111–332 | $2.33–4.98 | **2 × print** ($4.66–9.97) |
| F3 | 333–416 | $5.00–5.99 | **$9.99** (royalty +$0.00–1.00) |
| F4 | 417–828 | $6.00–10.94 | **print ÷ 0.60** ($10.01–18.23) |

UK bend: 315pp (£7.99), bonus zone to 394pp. EU bend: 354pp (€9.99), bonus to 437pp.

## Traps
1. **Never price at $9.98 / £7.98 / €9.98** — a penny under the threshold flips
   you to 50% and you can lose money at the same price.
2. Floors are **per-marketplace** — set each min manually at setup (Territories = All).
3. KDP refuses list < floor: the floor IS "at cost" (reader still pays ~2× print —
   Amazon keeps its half). True print-only pricing = Etsy PDF.
4. Hardcover: $6.80 flat ≤108pp, then $5.65 + $0.012/pp.
5. Page counts must be even (KDP requirement); min 24pp, max 828pp.
