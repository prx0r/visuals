# VBT vbt-p04 — L2 READ

## v.31
By that quick filling, to the crown's end broken through, by the brow-toss bridge: thought-free mind made — above all, all go forth.
The Sanskrit: *tayāpūryāśu mūrdhāntaṃ bhaṅktvā bhrūkṣepasetunā | nirvikalpaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā sarvordhve sarvagodgamaḥ*

## v.32
With peacock feathers, with varied forms, with circles, in the five-void: of the meditating one, in the unsurpassed void, entry in the heart may be.
The Sanskrit: *śikhipakṣaiś citrarūpair ma.ṅdalaiḥ śūnyapañcakam | dhyāyato 'nuttare śūnye praveśo hṛdaye bhavet*

## v.33
By just such course — wherever thought: in void, on the wall, on the supreme, on the vessel, self dissolved — the boon-giving.
The Sanskrit: *īdṛśena krameṇaiva yatra kutrāpi cintanā | śūnye kuḍye pare pātre svayaṃ līnā varapradā*

## v.34
Inside the skull, mind placed; standing with eyes half closed: in order, from the mind's firmness, mark the to-be-marked — the highest.
The Sanskrit: *kapālāntar mano nyasya tiṣṭhan mīlitalocanaḥ | krameṇa manaso dārḍhyāt lakṣayet laṣyam uttamam*

## v.35
Middle channel, standing in the middle, like a lotus-fiber; by the meditated inner sky, O Goddess — by that, god shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *madhyanāḍī madhyasaṃsthā bisasūtrābharūpayā | dhyātāntarvyomayā devyā tayā devaḥ prakāśate*

## v.36
By the hand-blocked gaze-weapon, from the brow's splitting, from the door's blocking; when seen in the drop, in order, when dissolved — in that middle, the supreme standing.
The Sanskrit: *kararuddhadṛgastreṇa bhrūbhedād dvārarodhanāt | dṛṣṭe bindau kramāl līne tanmadhye paramā sthitiḥ*

## v.37
The mark of subtle fire born of dwelling-inner-churn — the drop at the crest's end, in the heart, at dissolution's end: for the meditating one, dissolving.
The Sanskrit: *dhāmāntaḥkṣobhasambhūtasūkṣmāgnitilakākṛtim | binduṃ śikhānte hṛdaye layānte dhyāyato layaḥ*

## v.38
In the unstruck, in the vessel-ear, in the broken sound, in the river's rushing; bathed in word-brahman, the supreme brahman is attained.
The Sanskrit: *anāhate pātrakarṇe 'bhagnaśabde sariddrute | śabdabrahmaṇi niṣṇātaḥ param brahmādhigacchati*

## v.39
From the prolonged utterance of oṃ and the rest, at the prolonged end, from contemplating void: by void, by supreme power — to voidness she goes, O Bhairavī.
The Sanskrit: *praṇavādisamuccārāt plutānte śūnyabhāvānāt | śūnyayā parayā śaktyā śūnyatām eti bhairavi*

## v.40
Of any letter whatsoever, the beginning and end should be contemplated: by void, become void, that man becomes void-formed.
The Sanskrit: *yasya kasyāpi varṇasya pūrvāntāv anubhāvayet | śūnyayā śūnyabhūto 'sau śūnyākāraḥ pumān bhavet*
