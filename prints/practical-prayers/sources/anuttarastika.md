## anu-p01 — the frame: no method, just staying (v. 1)

Transition here is no method at all — not contemplation, not speech-connection, not discussion, not visualization, not steadying, not japa-practice-effort. So what is well-decided? Speak the supreme truth, and hear it: not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness, wholly, as staying. The Sanskrit: *saṃkrāmo 'tra na bhāvanā na ca kathāyuktir na carcā na ca dhyānaṃ vā na ca dhāraṇā na ca japābhyāsaprayāso na ca | tat kiṃ nāma suniścitaṃ vada paraṃ satyaṃ ca tacchrūyatāṃ na tyāgī na parigrahī bhaja sukhaṃ sarvaṃ yathāvasthitaḥ.*

## anu-p02 — saṃsāra is delusion (v. 2)

Saṃsāra is not, in truth, for body-holders — of bondage, what news? Bondage never truly is for anyone — so for the free, liberation-rite is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Abandon nothing; grasp nothing; live healthy, staying as you are. The Sanskrit: *saṃsāro 'sti na tattvatas tanubhṛtāṃ bandhasya vārtaiva kā bandho yasya na jātu tasya vitathā muktasya muktikriyā | mithyāmohakṛd eṣa rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhramo mā kiṃcit tyaja mā gṛhāṇa vihara svastho yathāvasthitaḥ.*

## anu-p03 — worship-difference is Māyā (v. 3)

Worship, worshipper, worshipped — what a difference-course in the unsurpassed? Transition — of whom, by whom effected, and who is the entry-course? This Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality, in otherness, though different — alas, stands. All is pure own-experience-nature. Thought in vain — do not do. The Sanskrit: *pūjapūjakapūjyabhedasaraṇiḥ keyaṃ kathānuttare saṃkrāmaḥ kila kasya kena vidadhe ko vā praveśakramaḥ | māyeyam na cidadvayāt paratayā bhinnāpy aho vartate sarvaṃ svānubhavasvabhāvavimalaṃ cintāṃ vṛthā mā kṛthāḥ.*

## anu-p04 — bliss is the forgotten treasure (v. 4)

Bliss here is not wealth-middle-intoxication, nor like enjoying women, nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendour-multitude — and not the light-rise either. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, like burden-lowering, is the attainment of the forgotten treasure of the all-nonduality state — the light-rise. The Sanskrit: *ānando 'tra na vittamadhyamadavan naivāṅganāsaṅgavat dīpārkendukṛtaprabhāprakaravan naiva prakāśodayaḥ | harṣaḥ saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr bhārāvatāropamaḥ sarvādvaitapadasya vismṛtanidheḥ prāptiḥ prakāśodayaḥ.*

## anu-p05 — things are consciousness-form (v. 5)

Passion, aversion, pleasure, displeasure, rise and fall, I-ness, misery and the rest — things shining forth, universe-bodied, are not different-natured. Whosever manifestation you suddenly see — having regarded it as one-self-natured consciousness-form — why do you not delight, full of contemplation? The Sanskrit: *rāgadveṣasukhāsukhodayalayāhaṅkāradainyādayo ye bhāvāḥ pravibhānti viśvavapuṣo bhinnasvabhāvā na te | vyaktiṃ paśyasi yasya yasya sahasā tattattadekātmatāsaṃvidrūpam avekṣya kiṃ na ramase tadbhāvanānirbharaḥ.*

## anu-p06 — things lack truth; awaken (v. 6)

Former non-being, being, action — things always, suddenly, in this existence: middle-formed, transformation-confused — whence their truth? In the non-true, unsteady, manifold, clever dream-delusion — transcending doubt, fear, stain, and reasoning's contriving: awakened, become. The Sanskrit: *pūrvābhāvabhavakriyā hi sahasā bhāvāḥ sadā 'smin bhave madhyākāravikārasaṃkaravatāṃ teṣāṃ kutaḥ satyatā | niḥsatye capale prapañcanicaye svapnabhrame peśale śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ prabuddho bhava.*

## anu-p07 — the universe-glory is will-born (v. 7)

Of things there is no arising; naturally, by your contemplation they shine; even the non-true partakes of truth for a moment, through experience's delusion. This universe-glory is will-born from you, birth from elsewhere it has none; hence by splendor you shine in worlds — one, yet many-selfed. The Sanskrit: *bhāvānāṃ na samudbhavo 'sti sahajas tvadbhāvitā bhānty amī niḥsatyā api satyatām anubhavabhrāntyā bhajanti kṣaṇam | tvatsaṅkalpaja eṣa viśvamahimā nāsty asya janmānyataḥ tasmāt tvaṃ vibhavena bhāsi bhuvaneṣv ekopy anekātmakaḥ.*

## anu-p08 — all shines in the consciousness-mirror; worship Lordhood (v. 8)

What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in the consciousness-mirror shines. That all, from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood — worship. The Sanskrit: *yat satyaṃ yad asatyam alpabahulaṃ nityaṃ na nityaṃ ca yat yan māyāmalinaṃ yad ātmavimalaṃ ciddarpaṇe rājate | tat sarvaṃ svavimarśasaṃvidudayād rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ jñātvā svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja.*
