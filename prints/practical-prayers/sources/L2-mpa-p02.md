# Mahopadeshavimshatika mpa-p02 — L2 READ

## v.11
From your waking, his waking; from your sleep, his sleep. Hence all is of your nature: the world as real and unreal.
The Sanskrit: *tvatprabodhāt prabodho'sya tvannidrāto layo'sya yat | atastvadātmakaṃ sarvaṃ viśva sadasadātmakam*

## v.12
The tongue is tired of your name; the mind is tired of remembering you. For the formless, what meditation? For the qualityless, what name?
The Sanskrit: *jihvā śrāntā bhavannāmni manaḥ śrāntaṃ bhavatsmṛtau | arūpasya kuto dhyāna nirguṇasya ca nāma kim*

## v.13
For the full, whence summoning? For the all-based, whence a seat? For the clean, foot-water and respects? For the pure, sipping?
The Sanskrit: *pūrṇasyāvāhanaṃ kutra sarvādhārasya cāsanam | svacchasya pādyamarghyañca śuddhasyācamanaṃ kutaḥ*

## v.14
For the stainless, what bath? For the world-bellied, what garment? For the unanointed, what scent? For the lovely, what jewel?
The Sanskrit: *nirmalasya kutaḥ snānaṃ vastraṃ viśvodarasya ca | nirlepasya kuto gandho ramyasyābharaṇaṃ kutaḥ*

## v.15
The sacred thread for the propless; flowers for the desireless. Incense for the noseless, lamp for the eyeless — what use?
The Sanskrit: *nirālambasyopavītaṃ puṣpaṃ nirvāsanasya ca | aghrāṇasya kuto dhūpaścakṣurhīnasya dīpakaḥ*

## v.16
Food-offering for the ever-content, betel for the Lord — whence? Circumambulation for the endless, obeisance for the non-dual — whence?
The Sanskrit: *nityatṛptasya naivedyaṃ tāmbūlaṃ ca kuto vibhoḥ | pradakṣiṇamanantasyā'dvitīyasyaya kuto natiḥ*

## v.17
Whence waving-lights for the self-shining Lord? How is a hymn enjoined for the Veda-unspeakable?
The Sanskrit: *svayaṃ prakāśamānasya kuto nīrājanaṃ vibhoḥ | vedavācāmavedyasya kutaḥ stotraṃ vidhīyate*

## v.18
How is there sending-off for the full, inside and out? And how is there oblation for the difference-free, everywhere?
The Sanskrit: *antarbahiśca pūrṇasya kathamudvāsanaṃ bhavet | bhedahīnasya viśvatra kathaṃ ca havanaṃ bhavet*

## v.19
Where is the fee for the full? Libation for the ever-content? Dismissal for the all-pervading? Pardon for the unseen?
The Sanskrit: *pūrṇasya dakṣiṇā kutra nityatṛptasya tarpaṇam | visarjanaṃ vyāpakasyā'pratyakṣasya kṣamāpaṇam*

## v.20
Just so, indeed, is the highest worship, in all states, always: with nondual mind, yoke the mind to God as Lord of all.
The Sanskrit: *evameva parā pūjā sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā | aikyabuddhayā tu sarveśe mano deve niyojayet*
