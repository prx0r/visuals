# Bhairavanukarana bhair-p01 (salute, shelter, conquer) — L2 READ

## v.1
Oṃ. Obeisance to Consciousness-Bhairava. You who shelter in consciousness-ground: take consciousness-state, wander not in vain. Proclaiming thus — Śiva, bent on grace, conquers for devotees.
The Sanskrit: *oṃ namaś cidbhairavāya | citibhūtiṃ śrayata mā mudhā bhramata*

## v.2
Consciousness-Bhairava alone — supreme, supreme-nectar-formed, one, high-blazing. All swallowed by the ring of shining faculties — Śiva I venerate.
The Sanskrit: *cidbhairavam eva paraṃ paramāmṛtarūpam | ekam atidīptam*

## v.3
Praised, praiser, praise — though distinct, nothing is here. It endures: as whatever form, by consciousness-form, so becomes this.
The Sanskrit: *stutyaḥ stotā stutir iti yad api vibhinnaṃ | mṛṣati yathā yad rūpaṃ cidrūpatayā*

## v.4
All-distinctions-melted, all-distinction-self, consciousness-mass-bliss — which Your principle, Lord — its imitation, Your form — I venerate.
The Sanskrit: *vigalitasarvavibhedaṃ sarvavibhedātma cidghanānandam | tasyānukṛtiṃ tvadākṛtiṃ vande*

## v.5
Outline-man, man, letter-script, letter-mass — just as, just so, of varied form, You — shape reveals, supreme self.
The Sanskrit: *rekhāpuruṣaḥ puruṣaṃ varṇalipir varṇasaṃcayaṃ yadvat | tadvat vicitrarūpaṃ tvām ākāro vyanakti paramātman*

## v.6
There concept-free indeed — You consciousness-formed entered — we reflect: what even a bit? Not indeed principle fancy's-object.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāvikalpam eva tvāṃ cidrūpaṃ samāviśya | vimṛśāmaḥ kim api manāṅ na hi tattvaṃ kalpanāviṣayaḥ*

## v.7
Own-light made, speech-goddess, sense-flowers-offering-arranged — first-unfolding guru, then venerated, heart entered.
The Sanskrit: *svātmaprabhāṃ vidhāya vāgdevīṃ | prathamonmeṣaṃ gurum atha vanditvā hṛdayam āviśya*

## v.8
I praise, reflecting, the Great Lord — self, own, consciousness-one-mass. Poured-out-bond-cluster — in this body-god-house.
The Sanskrit: *staumi vimṛśan maheśvaram ātmānaṃ svaṃ cidekaghanam | srāvitapāśakadambakam etasmin dehadevagṛhe*

## v.9
Consciousness-bliss-will-cognition-action-named powers conquer, O Lord. Having got subtle-gross splits — mouth-hood, Brahma-skull-named.
The Sanskrit: *cityānandecchāvitkaraṇākhyāḥ śaktayo jayanti | sūkṣmasthūlabhidāptā vaktratvaṃ brahmamuṇḍabhidhā*

## v.10
Omniscience-objected, heart and the rest, six-limbed — Yours. All up to Sadāśiva — by corpse-self — Your seat, O Lord.
The Sanskrit: *sarvajñatādiviṣayaṃ hṛdayādikam aṅgaṣaṭkaṃ te | sakalaṃ sadāśivāntaṃ pretātmatayā tavāsanaṃ svāmin*
