## anu-p01 — seeing-not-seeing (v. 1)

When the yogin abides with mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim — he, seeing yet not seeing outside with unmoving-pupil gaze — this indeed becomes the śāmbhavī mudrā, by your grace, O guru: devoid of void and non-void becomes that reality, the śāmbhava state. The Sanskrit: *antarlakṣyavilīnacittapavano yogī yadā vartate dṛṣṭyā niścalatārayā bahirasau paśyannapaśyannapi | mudreyaṃ khalu śāmbhavī bhavati sā yuṣmatprasādādguro śūnyāśūnyavivarjitaṃ bhavati yat tattvaṃ padaṃ śāmbhavam.*

## anu-p02 — the portrait and the silence (v. 2)

Half-open-eyed, steady-minded, gaze placed on the nose-tip — moon and sun too have attained dissolution in the interior of the triple-throb state — light-formed, devoid of all exteriority, and one supreme person: reality attains that state, the thing supreme. What more is there to say here? The Sanskrit: *ardhoddhāṭitalocanaḥ sthiramanās nāsāgradattekṣaṇaś candrārkāvapi līnatāmupagatau trispandabhāvāntare | jyotīrūpamaśeṣabāhyarahitaṃ caikaṃ pumāṃsaṃ paraṃ tattvaṃ tatpadameti vastu paramam vācyaṃ kimatrādhikam.*

## anu-p03 — whatever sounds, whatever posture, whatever breath (v. 3)

Whatever sound arises from the mouth — that mantra is transcendent. Whatever posture of the pleasure-pain-born body — that indeed is mudrā. Whatever flowing of breath by its own rhythm — that indeed is wonderful yoga. The supreme śākta abode — while I experience, what indeed would not shine? The Sanskrit: *śabdaḥ kaścana yo mukhādudayate mantraḥ sa lokottaraḥ saṃsthānaṃ sukhaduḥkhajanmavapuṣo yatkāpi mudraiva sā | prāṇasya svarasena yatpravahaṇaṃ yogaḥ sa evādbhutaḥ śāktaṃ dhāma paraṃ mamānubhavataḥ kinnāma na bhrājate.*

## anu-p04 — letters unseen, action dissolved, breath exhausted (v. 4)

That mantra shines forth in which letter-arrangement is not distinguished. That mudrā arises in which entire bodily action is dissolved. That yoga spreads forth from which breath's flowing is exhausted. In the festivals of attaining Your abode, of the wise — what, what is indeed not wonderful? The Sanskrit: *mantraḥ sa pratibhāti varṇaracanā yasminna saṃlakṣyate mudrā sā samudeti yatra galitā kṛtsnā kriyā kāyikī | yogaḥ sa prathate yataḥ pravahaṇaṃ prāṇasya saṃkṣīyate tvaddhāmādhigamotsaveṣu sudhiyāṃ kiṃ kiṃ na nāmādbhutaṃ.*
