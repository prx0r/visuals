# VBT vbt-p13 — L2 READ

## v.121
From the excess of devotion, of the dispassionate one — as which the mind arises: she, power, Śaṅkara's — always should contemplate her; then Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *bhaktyudrekād viraktasya yādṛśī jāyate matiḥ | sā śaktiḥ śāṅkarī nityam bhavayet tāṃ tataḥ śivaḥ*

## v.122
In things inside, being known — in all things, voidness: her indeed, by mind meditated — known even, becomes calm.
The Sanskrit: *vastvantare vedyamāne sarvavastuṣu śūnyatā | tām eva manasā dhyātvā vidito 'pi praśāmyati*

## v.123
By slightly knowing which is remembered purity — that purity in Śambhu's view: not pure, nor impure therefore — thought-free, happy may he be.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃcijjñair yā smṛtā śuddhiḥ sā śuddhiḥ śambhudarśane | na śucir hy aśucis tasmān nirvikalpaḥ sukhī bhavet*

## v.124
Everywhere Bhairava's state — even in common things, the object: not apart from that higher thus — the nondual going.
The Sanskrit: *sarvatra bhairavo bhāvaḥ sāmānyeṣv api gocaraḥ | na ca tadvyatirekteṇa paro 'stīty advayā gatiḥ*

## v.125
Same in enemy and in friend; same in honor and dishonor: of brahman's fullness — thus having known, happy may he be.
The Sanskrit: *samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca samo mānāvamānayoḥ || brahmaṇaḥ paripūrṇatvāt iti jñātvā sukhī bhavet*

## v.126
Not hatred should one contemplate anywhere, not passion anywhere: free of passion and hatred — in the middle, brahman glides forth.
The Sanskrit: *na dveṣam bhāvayet kvāpi na rāgam bhāvayet kvacit | rāgadveṣavinirmuktau madhye brahma prasarpati*

## v.127
Whatever unknowable, whatever ungraspable, whatever void, whatever gone to nonbeing: that all as Bhairava to be contemplated — at that end, awakening arises.
The Sanskrit: *yad avedyaṃ yad agrāhyaṃ yac chūnyaṃ yad abhāvagam | tat sarvam bhairavam bhāvyaṃ tadante bodhasambhavaḥ*

## v.128
In the eternal, in the supportless, in the void, in the pervasive, free of counting: mind made in the outer sky — into the skyless should enter.
The Sanskrit: *nitye nirāśraye śūnye vyāpake kalanojjhite | bāhyākāśe manaḥ kṛtvā nirākāśaṃ samāviśet*

## v.129
Wherever the mind goes — that, that by that very thing at that moment: having left non-standing — waveless then may it be.
The Sanskrit: *yatra yatra mano yāti tat tat tenaiva tatkṣaṇam | parityajyānavasthityā nistaraṅgas tato bhavet*

## v.130
By fear all makes resound — giving all, pervasive in wholeness: hence of Bhairava's word, from constant uttering — Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *bhayā sarvaṃ ravayati sarvado vyāpako 'khile | iti bhairavaśabdasya santatoccāraṇāc chivaḥ*
