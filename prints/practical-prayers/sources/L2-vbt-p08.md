# VBT vbt-p08 — L2 READ

## v.71
In joy, in greatness, when it is won — seen or a kinsman long after: bliss risen, having meditated it, dissolved in that — of that mind may be.
The Sanskrit: *ānande mahati prāpte dṛṣṭe vā bāndhave cirāt | ānandam udgataṃ dhyātvā tallayas tanmanā bhavet*

## v.72
From eating and drinking, the swelling of joy, bliss-blooming: should be fancied the filled state — great bliss then may be.
The Sanskrit: *jagdhipānakṛtollāsarasānandavijṛmbhaṇāt | bhāvayed bharitāvasthāṃ mahānandas tato bhavet*

## v.73
From tasting objects such as song — happiness not shared, one-selfness: of the yogin, by absorbedness, mounted in mind — in that selfness.
The Sanskrit: *gitādiviṣayāsvādāsamasaukhyaikatātmanaḥ | yoginas tanmayatvena manorūḍhes tadātmatā*

## v.74
Wherever the mind is content, right there the mind should be held: there, there — the own-form of supreme bliss proceeds forth.
The Sanskrit: *yatra yatra manas tuṣṭir manas tatraiva dhārayet | tatra tatra parānandasvārūpaṃ sampravartate*

## v.75
In the not-yet-come, in sleep, when the outer range has perished: that state, to be gone to by mind — the supreme Goddess shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *anāgatāyāṃ nidrāyām praṇaṣṭe bāhyagocare | sāvasthā manasā gamyā parā devī prakāśate*

## v.76
By the brilliance of sun, lamp and such, in the sky made dappled: gaze fixed right there — one's own self-form shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *tejasā sūryadīpāder ākāśe śabalīkṛte | dṛṣṭir niveśyā tatraiva svātmarūpam prakāśate*

## v.77
By the skeleton, by the angry, by the licking Bhairavī, by the sky-going: at the gaze's time — the supreme attainment shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *karaṅkiṇyā krodhanayā bhairavyā lelihānayā | khecaryā dṛṣṭikāle ca parāvāptiḥ prakāśate*

## v.78
On a soft seat, with one buttock, hands and feet unsupported placed: by that context — the supreme, full mind may be.
The Sanskrit: *mṛdvāsane sphijaikena hastapādau nirāśrayam | nidhāya tatprasaṅgena parā pūrṇā matir bhavet*

## v.79
Having sat on the seat rightly, arms, two, made half-bent: in the armpit-sky, mind making — calm comes by that dissolving.
The Sanskrit: *upaviśyāsane samyag bāhū kṛtvārdhakuñcitau | kakṣavyomni manaḥ kurvan śamam āyāti tallayāt*

## v.80
Of gross-formed state, fixed gaze cast down, and quickly: unsupported mind made — Śiva one may go to.
The Sanskrit: *sthūlarūpasya bhāvasya stabdhāṃ dṛṣṭiṃ nipātya ca | acireṇa nirādhāraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā śivaṃ vrajet*
