# VBT vbt-p11 — L2 READ

## v.101
In the range of lust, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy — intellect uncaused having been made, that reality remains.
The Sanskrit: *kāmakrodhalobhamohamadamātsaryagocare | buddhiṃ nistimitāṃ kṛtvā tat tattvam avaśiṣyate*

## v.102
The world, Indra-net-made, cast — or picture-work-like; whirling, or the meditating one's all: and seeing — joy upsurges.
The Sanskrit: *indrajālamayaṃ viśvaṃ vyastaṃ vā citrakarmavat | bhramad vā dhyāyataḥ sarvam paśyataś ca sukhodgamaḥ*

## v.103
Not into pain should the mind be cast, not into joy, nor scattered about: O Bhairavī — in the middle, what reality remains may be known.
The Sanskrit: *na cittaṃ nikṣiped duḥkhe na sukhe vā parikṣipet | bhairavi jñāyatāṃ madhye kiṃ tattvam avaśiṣyate*

## v.104
Having left the own-body-standing, contemplating everywhere-I-am-thus: with firm mind, by a gaze not seeing elsewhere — happy may he be.
The Sanskrit: *vihāya nijadehasthaṃ sarvatrāsmīti bhāvayan | dṛḍhena manasā dṛṣṭyā nānyekṣiṇyā sukhī bhavet*

## v.105
In a pot and such — whatever knowing; desire and such, or inside me: never all-pervading born — contemplating thus, all-going.
The Sanskrit: *ghaṭādau yac ca vijñānam icchādyaṃ vā mamāntare | naiva sarvagataṃ jātam bhāvayan iti sarvagaḥ*

## v.106
Grasper-grasped awareness: common to all embodied; of yogins the distinction but — He is, in relation, carefulness.
The Sanskrit: *grāhyagrāhakasaṃvittiḥ sāmānyā sarvadehinām | yogināṃ tu viśeṣo =B9sti sambandhe sāvadhānatā*

## v.107
Like self in another body — He even should perceive awareness: regard of one's own body having left, pervasive in days may he be.
The Sanskrit: *svavad anyaśarīre =B9pi saṃvittim anubhāvayet | apekṣāṃ svaśarīrasya tyaktvā vyāpī dinair bhavet*

## v.108
Supportless mind having been made — thoughts should not be thought: then, in self-supreme-self — Bhairava, O deer-eyed one.
The Sanskrit: *nirādhāraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā vikalpān na vikalpayet | tadātmaparamātmatve bhairavo mṛgalocane*

## v.109
All-knowing, all-doing, and pervasive, the highest Lord: he indeed I — Śiva-natured; hence from firmness, Śiva may one be.
The Sanskrit: *sarvajñaḥ sarvakartā ca vyāpakaḥ parameśvaraḥ | sa evāhaṃ śaivadharmā iti dārḍhyāc chivo bhavet*

## v.110
Like the waves of water, the flame-plays of fire, the sun's light: of me indeed, of Bhairava — these world-plays, manifested.
The Sanskrit: *jalasyevormayo vahner jvālābhaṅgyaḥ prabhā raveḥ | mamaiva bhairavasyaitā viśvabhaṅgyo vibheditāḥ*
