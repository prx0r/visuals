Bhairavastava · verse 1

Veneration

व्याप्तचराचरभावविशेषं चिन्मयम् एकम् अनन्तम् अनादिम् भैरवनाथम् अनाथशरण्यं त्वन्मयचित्ततया हृदि वन्दे
vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣaṃ cinmayam ekam anantam anādim bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande
Pervading every state of moving and unmoving, consciousness itself is one — without end or beginning.Bhairava, Lord, refuge of the helpless — with a mind become Yours, in my heart, I bow.
Plain reading (L2)

Bhairava — pervading the particular states of moving and unmoving things, consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless — Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate. The stotra opens with the Lord's all-pervading nature and the devotee's stance: total surrender of mind to Bhairava, who is the refuge of those without refuge. Veneration is not ritual but mental absorption — the mind becoming His.

1
The Craft

Veneration

Sthāyin: śānta as veneration of all-pervading consciousness; FIRST VERSE — sets the house voice (doctrine first, then the name; owner verdict required). Sanskrit-first: sthāvara-jaṅgama (moving AND unmoving — the pair IS the pervasion, kept paired); namāmi (I BOW — the verse's only act, kept last). Abhinavagupta frame: veneration as mental absorption (manasā bhūtvā — mind-become-His), not offering; the name lived, not glossed (argmap INF). Rows: 9 (moving/unmoving catalogue — pair kept bare), 7 (Bhairava-vocative mid-verse — address AFTER establishment; order IS the recognition: what, then whom), 1 (‘I bow’ end-weighted). Close: ‘in my heart, I bow’. Rupture: em-dash after ‘is one’ (all-pervasion stated, then the person enters). Rejected: ‘I prostrate’ — killed: contemporary diction (license ledger); fronting the vocative — killed: name-after-pervasion is the recognition performed; ‘refuge of helpless ones’ plural — killed: anātha kept singular-abstract (‘the helpless’). Read aloud: per-VAD-ing EV-ery STATE of MOV-ing and un-MOV-ing, CON-scious-ness it-SELF is ONE … BHAI-ra-va, LORD … IN-my-HEART, i-BOW.

The logic

What is the object of veneration, and how does veneration relate to it? → The Absolute is named in full, then venerated by absorption into it; the name is lived, not glossed. Veneration is not offering but becoming.

Identity

tvanmayam etad aśeṣam idānīṃ bhāti mama tvadanugrahaśaktyā tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam
All this, become Yours without remainder, now appears to me by the power of Your grace.You, Maheśa, are ever my own self — made of You, all is mine through You.
Plain reading (L2)

All this, become of You, now appears to me by the power of Your grace. You, Maheśa, are always my self; by You all is mine. The Lord is not other than the self; grace first discloses the world as His, then identity is declared, and through Him, everything becomes mine.

आनन्दः अत्र न वित्तमध्य मदवन् नैवाङ्गनासङ्गवत् दीपार्केन्दुकृत प्रभाप्रकरवन् नैव । प्रकाशोदयः हर्षः संभृतभेदमुक्तिसुखभूर् भारावतारोपमः सर्वाद्वैतपदस्य विस्मृतनिधेः प्राप्तिः प्रकाशोदयः
ānandaḥ atra na vittamadhya madavan naivāṅganāsaṅgavat dīpārkendukṛta prabhāprakaravan naiva | prakāśodayaḥ harṣaḥ saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr bhārāvatāropamaḥ sarvādvaitapadasya vismṛtanidheḥ prāptiḥ prakāśodayaḥ
Bliss here is not wealth-middle-intoxication, nor like enjoying women,nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendour-multitudes — nor even the light-rise.Joy — difference gathered, freed in bliss-ground, like the lowering of a burden —is the forgotten treasure found of the all-nonduality state: the light-rise.
Plain reading (L2)

Bliss here is not wealth-middle-intoxication, nor like enjoying women, nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendour-multitude — and not the light-rise either. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, like burden-lowering, is the attainment of the forgotten treasure of the all-nonduality state — the light-rise.

The Craft

Bliss is the forgotten treasure

Sthāyin: ānanda as forgotten-treasure-found; bhakti as prāpti (finding); vyabhicāri harṣa of burden-lowering. Sanskrit-first: na…na…na (triple refusal — wealth/women/splendour, then pictured-light refused too); bhāraharaṇam iva (LIKE burden-lowering — iva simile kept as simile); smṛti (FOUND-treasure — the forgotten remembered). Rows: 9 (refusal catalogue — each pleasure named then refused; naming IS the finding), 10 (burden image left standing, no gloss), 1 (light-rise AFFIRMED last — the refused word returns; ring closes whether same-or-different, held open). Close: ‘the light-rise’. Rupture: ‘Joy —’ (refusals turn to naming — the dash after Joy holds the turn). Rejected: gilding — killed: bluntness is the music; ‘treasure-trove’ — killed: domesticates the finding; deciding same-vs-different light — killed: held open (the ring's ambiguity IS the verse). Read aloud: BLISS-here is NOT wealth-MIDD-le … NOR lamp-SUN-moon-MADE … JOY — DIFF-er-ence GA-thered, FREED in BLISS-ground, LIKE the LOW-er-ing OF a BUR-den — IS the for-GOTT-en TREA-sure FOUND.

The logic

What is bliss, and how is it found? → Bliss is neither pleasure nor splendour but joy like burden-lowering — the attainment of the forgotten treasure.

Anuttarāṣṭikā · verse 5

Things are consciousness-form

रागद्वेषसुखासुखोदयलयाहङ्कारदैन्यादयो ये भावाः प्रविभान्ति विश्ववपुषो भिन्नस्वभावा न ते । व्यक्तिं पश्यसि यस्य यस्य सहसा तत्तत्तदेकात्मतासंविद्रूपम् अवेक्ष्य किं न रमसे तद्भावनानिर्भरः
rāgadveṣasukhāsukhodayalayāhaṅkāradainyādayo ye bhāvāḥ pravibhānti viśvavapuṣo bhinnasvabhāvā na te | vyaktiṃ paśyasi yasya yasya sahasā tattattadekātmatāsaṃvidrūpam avekṣya kiṃ na ramase tadbhāvanānirbharaḥ
Passion, aversion, pleasure, pain, rise and fall, I-sense, misery and the rest — these states, shining forth, bodied of the universe, are of no separate nature.Whatever form you suddenly see — see it as consciousness of one self-nature: why not delight, full of contemplating it?
Plain reading (L2)

Passion, aversion, pleasure, displeasure, rise and fall, I-ness, misery and the rest — things shining forth, universe-bodied, are not different-natured. Whosever manifestation you suddenly see — having regarded it as one-self-natured consciousness-form — why do you not delight, full of contemplation?

The Craft

Things are consciousness-form

Sthāyin: ānanda as delight in variety; bhakti as rati; vyabhicāri joy (harṣa) of seeing variety as one-self. Sanskrit-first: ekātmatā-compound open kept (one-self-nature, unresolved per craft); ramase (WHY-NOT-DELIGHT — question-mood, not command: the verse asks, never orders, per UNC). Abhinavagupta frame: variety (vicitra) unthreatening — the catalogue of feelings IS the universe bodied, no separate nature to fear. Rows: 9 (passion/aversion/pleasure/pain/rise-fall/I-sense/misery — catalogue kept whole; trimming would shrink the variety the verse blesses), 1 (‘contemplating it’ — dhyāna-full delight closes; seeing ends in beholding), 10 (sudden-seeing left as sudden — sahasā kept). Close: ‘full of contemplating it’. Rupture: ‘why not delight’ (seeing turns to delight — the question IS the turn). Rejected: imperative-only delight — killed by UNC (question holds both moods); resolving ekātmatā — killed; trimming feelings — killed: catalogue is the variety. Read aloud: PASS-ion, a-VER-sion, PLEA-sure, PAIN … ARE of NO-sep-ar-ate NA-ture … WHAT-ev-er FORM you SUDD-en-ly SEE — SEE it AS-con-SCIOUS-ness … WHY-not de-LIGHT.

The logic

Why not delight in variety? → Variety is one nature; regard it as consciousness-form, and delight is the only consistent response.

Anuttarāṣṭikā · verse 6

Things lack truth; awaken

पूर्वाभावभवक्रिया हि सहसा भावाः सदा ऽस्मिन् भवे मध्याकारविकारसंकरवतां तेषां कुतः सत्यता । निःसत्ये चपले प्रपञ्चनिचये स्वप्नभ्रमे पेशले शङ्कातङ्ककलङ्कयुक्तिकलनातीतः प्रबुद्धो भव
pūrvābhāvabhavakriyā hi sahasā bhāvāḥ sadā 'smin bhave madhyākāravikārasaṃkaravatāṃ teṣāṃ kutaḥ satyatā | niḥsatye capale prapañcanicaye svapnabhrame peśale śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ prabuddho bhava
Past non-being, being, doing — things, always, suddenly, in this becoming, half-formed and mixed in change: what truth could they have?In the untrue, restless, teeming, clever dream-delusion — past doubting, fearing, staining, and reason’s scheming: awake — become.
Plain reading (L2)

Former non-being, being, action — things always, suddenly, in this existence: middle-formed, transformation-confused — whence their truth? In the non-true, unsteady, manifold, clever dream-delusion — transcending doubt, fear, stain, and reasoning's contriving: awakened, become.

21
The Craft

Things lack truth; awaken

Sthāyin: śānta as awakening (prabodha); vyabhicāri clarity (prasāda) of transcending. Sanskrit-first: peśala/bhava/madhyākāra opens kept (half-formed/mixed — obscurity marked, not smoothed); prabuddha bhava (AWAKE — BECOME: two imperatives, the verse's only act). Abhinavagupta frame: dream-delusion (svapna) as the accurate ontology of things — clever (peśala) modifies the delusion, not the dreamer. Rows: 9 (past-doubting/fearing/staining/scheming — transcend-list kept whole), 1 (‘become’ end-weighted — the second imperative closes; awakening lands as becoming), 8 (vowel fall: clever-scheming chatter → awake-become stillness). Close: ‘awake — become’. Rupture: the colon before ‘awake’ (delusion inventoried, then the act). Rejected: ‘clever-vs-cunning’ resolved — killed by UNC; ‘planning’ for scheming — killed: scheming schemes (kalpanā contrives); ‘wake up’ — killed: colloquial, prabodha is awakening. Read aloud: PAST non-BE-ing, BE-ing, DO-ing … WHAT-truth COULD they HAVE … PAST-doubt-ing, FEAR-ing, STAIN-ing … a-WAKE — be-COME.

The logic

Whence the truth of things? → Things lack truth; the only response is awakening: transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning; awaken.

Anuttarāṣṭikā · verse 7

The universe-glory is will-born

भावानां न समुद्भवो ऽस्ति सहजस् त्वद्भाविता भान्त्य् अमी निःसत्या अपि सत्यताम् अनुभवभ्रान्त्या भजन्ति क्षणम् । त्वत्सङ्कल्पज एष विश्वमहिमा नास्त्य् अस्य जन्मान्यतः तस्मात् त्वं विभवेन भासि भुवनेष्व् एकोप्य् अनेकात्मकः
bhāvānāṃ na samudbhavo 'sti sahajas tvadbhāvitā bhānty amī niḥsatyā api satyatām anubhavabhrāntyā bhajanti kṣaṇam | tvatsaṅkalpaja eṣa viśvamahimā nāsty asya janmānyataḥ tasmāt tvaṃ vibhavena bhāsi bhuvaneṣv ekopy anekātmakaḥ
Things do not arise; by nature they shine through your beholding — even the untrue partakes of truth for a moment, through experience’s delusion.This glory of worlds is will-born of You, with birth from nowhere else — so in splendor You shine through the worlds, one and yet many-selfed.
Plain reading (L2)

Of things there is no arising; naturally, by your contemplation they shine; even the non-true partakes of truth for a moment, through experience's delusion. This universe-glory is will-born from you, birth from elsewhere it has none; hence by splendor you shine in worlds — one, yet many-selfed.

22
The Craft

The universe-glory is will-born

Sthāyin: śānta as will-born glory; bhakti as pratyabhijñā; vyabhicāri wonder (camatkāra) at one-yet-many. Sanskrit-first: sahaja/bhrānti opens kept (natural/delusion held per UNC); ekopy anekātmakam (ONE yet MANY-SELFed — non-cancelling kept, never resolved); You-capital kept (address IS the Lord). Abhinavagupta frame: ābhāsa shining-by-beholding (no arising needed — being-beheld suffices); will (icchā) as the sole birth. Rows: 9 (one/many pair — kept paired; resolving would cancel the verse), 7 (You addressed mid-verse — beholding MADE second-person), 1 (‘many-selfed’ closes — multiplicity last, uncancelled). Close: ‘one and yet many-selfed’. Rupture: ‘so in splendor’ (cosmogony turns to shining — birth-account becomes praise). Rejected: resolving one-yet-many — killed: non-cancelling IS the verse; lowercasing you — killed: address is the Lord; ‘through your gaze’ for beholding — killed: darśana-beholding kept plain. Read aloud: THINGS do NOT a-RISE … EVEN the un-TRUE par-TAKES of TRUTH … THIS-glo-ry OF-worlds is WILL-born OF-you … ONE and YET man-y-SELFED.

The logic

Do things arise, and whence the universe-glory? → No arising; things shine by contemplation; non-true borrows momentary truth; universe-glory is will-born; you shine one-yet-many.

Anuttarāṣṭikā · verse 8

All shines in the consciousness-mirror; worship Lordhood

यत् सत्यं यद् असत्यम् अल्पबहुलं नित्यं न नित्यं च यत् यन् मायामलिनं यद् आत्मविमलं चिद्दर्पणे राजते । तत् सर्वं स्वविमर्शसंविदुदयाद् रूपप्रकाशात्मकं ज्ञात्वा स्वानुभवाधिरूढमहिमा विश्वेश्वरत्वं भज
yat satyaṃ yad asatyam alpabahulaṃ nityaṃ na nityaṃ ca yat yan māyāmalinaṃ yad ātmavimalaṃ ciddarpaṇe rājate | tat sarvaṃ svavimarśasaṃvidudayād rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ jñātvā svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja
What is true, what untrue, little or much, lasting or lasting-not, Māyā-stained or self-pure — all shines in the mirror of consciousness.All that — risen from self-grasping consciousness, natured as form and light — know it: glory mounted on your own experience — worship lordship of all.
Plain reading (L2)

What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in the consciousness-mirror shines. That all, from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood — worship.

23
The Craft

All shines in the consciousness-mirror; worship Lordhood

Sthāyin: śānta as Lordhood (viśveśvaratva); bhakti as worship; vyabhicāri wonder at all-opposites mirrored. Sanskrit-first: bhaja mood open per UNC (worship kept as act, mood unforced); compounds open kept (own-grasping-consciousness-rise carried whole — density IS the metaphysics). Abhinavagupta frame: mirror (darpaṇa) as the accurate instrument — opposites shine WITHOUT entering; knowing = glory = Lordhood (equation, not ascent). Rows: 9 (true/untrue, little/much, lasting/lasting-not, Māyā-stained/self-pure — all pairs kept; trimming would un-mirror the mirror), 1 (‘lordship of all’ closes — viśveśvaratva last, the equation's answer), 12 (śānta-fall: ‘three-’ … ‘all’ — whole-work close, wide and still). Close: ‘worship lordship of all’. Rupture: ‘know it:’ (all-shown turns to all-known — colon stages the equation). Rejected: trimming pairs — killed: all-pairs IS the mirror; resolving bhaja — killed by UNC; ‘serve the Lord of all’ — killed: bhaja + aiśvarya is worship-lordship, service weakens. Read aloud: WHAT is TRUE, WHAT un-TRUE … ALL-shines IN the MIR-ror OF-con-SCIOUS-ness … KNOW-it: GLO-ry MOUNT-ed ON your OWN ex-PER-i-ence — WOR-ship LORD-ship OF-all.

The logic

What shines in the mirror, and what follows from knowing it? → All opposites shine in the consciousness-mirror; knowing that all is own-grasping-consciousness-rise = glory = Lordhood; worship.
