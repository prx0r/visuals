## pan-p01 — offering (v. 1)

Whatever I have truly done, O Lord — no duty of mine goes beyond this: my pride has been offered by me with devotion on the Lord's lotus feet. This is my all; I know there is nothing other than this. Therefore, having cast all else aside — quickly, O protector of the helpless, O ocean of compassion — be pleased! The Sanskrit: *yatsatyaṃ tu mayā kṛtaṃ mama vibho kṛtyaṃ tu nātaḥ paraṃ yanmanmānamaiśapādakamale bhaktyā mayaivārpitam | sarvasvamata evametaditarannāsyeva jānāmyatastyaktvā kṣipramanāthanātha karuṇāsindho prasanno bhava.*

## pan-p02 — praise at the door (v. 2)

O Maheśa, at Your door let this charming speech of mine flash forth exceedingly — "Victory, victory to Maheśa!" — faultless and unceasing. Let this very speech, auspicious, be bliss-giving for me forever. O Lord, O giver of refuge, make me — whose protector is Maheśa — possessed of a protector! The Sanskrit: *maheśa tvaddvāri sphuratu rucirā vāgatitarāṃ mamaiṣā nirdoṣaṃ jaya jaya maheśeti satatam | śivā saiṣā vāṇī bhavatu śivadā mahyamaniśaṃ maheśanāthaṃ māṃ śaraṇada sanāthaṃ kuru vibho.*

## pan-p03 — reproach (v. 3)

You speak not, O best-limbed one; You look not upon me, wretched as I am. My entreaty, made in many ways, You hear not; You come not into my mind. Fallen amid the pit of the ocean of transmigration, I am mostly left unsupported — yet scripture truly declares You devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like! The Sanskrit: *brūṣe nottamāṅga paśyasi na māmetādṛśaṃ duḥkhitaṃ vijñaptiṃ bahudhā kṛtāṃ na śṛṇuṣe nāyāsi manmānase | saṃsārārṇavagartamadhyapatitaṃ prāyeṇa nālambase vākcakṣuḥśravaṇāṅghriṇādirahitaṃ tvāmāha satyaṃ śrutiḥ.*

## pan-p04 — the cloud lifts (v. 4)

From the guru's word — by the force that wipes away the fabric of heaped-up reasonings — and from the assurance told, fittingly, out of regard for scripture: when the cloud of doubt has dissolved, let me touch the feet of the Lord, whose splendor shines in the sky of the heart — like the sun, the conqueror of darkness. The Sanskrit: *gurorvākyādyukti-pracaya-racana-unmārjana-vaśātsamāśvāsācchāstraṃ prati samucitādapi kathitam | vilīne śaṅkābhre hṛdayagaganodbhāsimahasaḥ prabhoḥ sūryasyeva spṛśatu caraṇān dhvāntajayinaḥ.*

## pan-p05 — forgetting, and the final plea (v. 5)

Much time dwelling with You has passed — alas, in this very moment! What, what has not been done by You for me, for the sake of serving the excellent guru! Again and again I, whose body has attained the incomparable divine plane — yet indeed I forget You. Therefore, by You let there be no further staying-away! The Sanskrit: *yātastvatsahavāsato bahutaraḥ kālo vatāsmin kṣaṇe kiṃ kiṃ vā na kṛtaṃ tvayā varagurūpāsānimittena me | vāraṃ vāramahaṃ punarnirupamāṃ divyaṃ bhuvaṃ prāsuvatkāyastvāṃ kila vismarāmi yadataḥ stheyaṃ na bhūyastvayā.*
