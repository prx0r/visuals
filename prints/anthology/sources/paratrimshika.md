## para-p01–p02 — anuttara and mantra powers
**SUMMARY.** Etad guhyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ kathayasva mama prabho — this secret, great secret, tell me, Lord. Hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulinī kulanāyikā — the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī, clan-leader. Tām me kathaya deveśa yena tṛptiṃ vrajāmy aham — tell me that, Lord of gods, by which I attain satisfaction. Śrībhairava uvāca — Śrī Bhairava said. Śṛṇu devi mahābhage uttarasyāpy anuttaram — listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara. Kathayāmi na saṃdehaḥ sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam — I tell, no doubt, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving. Asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅ mantramudrāgaṇo mahān — when this is pronounced well, the great mantra-mudrā-group. Sadyaḥ sanmukhatām eti svadehāveśalakṣaṇam — instantly faces, characteristic of entering own body. Muhūrtaṃ smarate yas tu cumbake nābhimudritaḥ — one who remembers for a moment, stamped on navel by cumbaka. Sa badhnāti tadā dehaṃ mantramudrāgaṇaṃ naraḥ — he then binds the body, mantra-mudrā-group, man. Atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo 'sau kathayaty api — past/future calamities, asked, he tells too.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes anuttara (of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī) and mantra powers (when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; instantly faces; binds body; tells past/future calamities).

**KEY TERMS.** anuttara (unsurpassed), uttara (higher), kaula (Kaula), kaulinī (Kaulinī), kulanāyikā (clan-leader), kaulikasiddhi (Kaulika-siddhi), mantra (mantra), mudrā (mudrā), cumbaka (cumbaka), nābhi (navel).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Anuttara: this secret, great secret, tell me, Lord; the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī, clan-leader; tell me that, Lord of gods, by which I attain satisfaction; Śrī Bhairava said; listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara; I tell, no doubt, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving. (2) Mantra powers: when this is pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; instantly faces, characteristic of entering own body; one who remembers for a moment, stamped on navel by cumbaka; he then binds the body, mantra-mudrā-group, man; past/future calamities, asked, he tells too.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.19.

## para-p03–p04 — knowledge and close
**SUMMARY.** Anena jñātamātreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ — by this, when known, known by all śaktis. Śākinīkulasāmānyo bhaved yogaṃ vināpi hi — would become common to Śākinī-clan, even without yoga. Avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati — ignorant of rules, becomes knower of rules, regarding worship. Kālāgnim āditaḥ kṛtvā māyāntam brahmadehagam — from Kālāgni first, to Māyā, Brahma-body-going. Śivo viśvadyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam — Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held. Puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅgaṃ vā sthaṇḍilaṃ ca vā — flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila. Caturdaśābhijaptena puṣpeṇāsanakalpanā — by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement. Tatra sṛṣṭiṃ yajed vīraḥ punar evāsanaṃ tataḥ — there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat then. Sṛṣṭiṃ tu sampuṭīkṛtya paścād yajanam ārabhet — creation, having enclosed, afterwards worship begins. Sarvatattvasusampūrṇām sarvāvayavaśobhitām — all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful. Yajed devīṃ mahāb... — worship the Goddess, great...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes knowledge (when known, known by all śaktis; common to Śākinī-clan even without yoga; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; from Kālāgni to Māyā, Brahma-body-going; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad) and close (flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila; by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; creation enclosed, afterwards worship begins; all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful; worship the Goddess).

**KEY TERMS.** jñāna (knowledge), śākinī (Śākinī), kula (clan), yoga (yoga), vidhi (rule), yajana (worship), kālāgni (Kālāgni), māyā (Māyā), brahmadeha (Brahma-body), viśvadya (world-devouring), anantānta (endless-end), śaktitraya (śakti-triad), puṣpa (flower), liṅga (liṅga), sthaṇḍila (sthaṇḍila), sṛṣṭi (creation), vīra (vīra), āsana (seat), devī (Goddess).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p01–p02 (anuttara/mantra powers).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Knowledge: by this, when known, known by all śaktis; would become common to Śākinī-clan even without yoga; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules regarding worship; from Kālāgni first, to Māyā, Brahma-body-going; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held. (2) Close: flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila; by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; creation enclosed, afterwards worship begins; all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful; worship the Goddess.
