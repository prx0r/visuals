## The practice

At mealtime ("when there is time of taking food"), put water in the palms and recite:

> **brahmārpaṇaṁ brahma havir brahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam /**
> **brahmaiva tena gantavyaṁ brahmakarmasamādhinā //**

Identified: **Bhagavadgītā 4.24**. Lakshmanjoo reads *brahma* throughout as **Lord Śiva**.

His gloss (compressed): whatever I take is offered to Śiva; what I offer is itself Śiva; I offer Śiva in Śiva; the stomach is Śiva's residence, so food goes to Śiva in the stomach; the act of offering, the offerer, and the offering are Śiva's play. *Brahmakarmasamādhi* = whatever you do, only brahma/Śiva everywhere.

## The Somananda verse

> **asmadrūpa samāviṣṭaḥ svātmanātma nivāraṇe /**
> **śivaḥ karotu nijayā namaḥ śaktyā tatātmane //**

Identified (provisional): **Somananda, Śivadr̥ṣṭi closing verse (7.103)** — needs verification against edition.
Lakshmanjoo's gloss: Śiva bows to Śiva (the "I" who bows is Śiva; the Śiva bowed to is myself, who is Śiva); purpose is *ātmanivāraṇa*, removing obstacles — and obstacles too are Śiva; removing Śiva (as obstacle) by Śiva, with Śiva's own power (*nijayā śaktyā*).

Motif: **Śiva-bows-to-Śiva; obstacle-is-Śiva**. Direct lineage voice: Somananda → Utpaladeva.
