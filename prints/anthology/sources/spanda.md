# Spandakārikā — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## span-p01–p02 — spanda and its operation
**SUMMARY.** Yasyonmeṣanimeṣābhyāṃ jagataḥ pralayodayau | taṃ śakticakravibhavaprabhavaṃ śaṅkaraṃ stumaḥ — to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise. Yatra sthitam idaṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ yasmāc ca nirgataṃ | tasyānāvṛtarūpatvān na nirodho 'sti kutracit — where all this effect abides, from which it proceeds; by His uncovered nature, no obstruction anywhere. Jāgradādi vibhede 'pi tadabhinne prasarpati | nivartate nijānnaiva svabhāvād upalabdhṛtaḥ — though waking etc. differ, it flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature, from the knower. Ahaṃ sukhī ca duḥkhī ca raktaś ca ityadisamvidaḥ | sukhādyavasthānusyūte vartante 'nyatra tāḥ sphuṭam — I am happy/sad/attached etc. consciousnesses; happiness etc. states, strung through, clearly abide elsewhere. Na duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ yatra na grāhyam grāhakaṃ na ca | na cāsti mūḍhabhāvo 'pi tad asti paramārthataḥ — where no suffering/pleasure, no graspable/grasper, no delusion; that exists in supreme truth. Tam adhiṣṭhātṛbhāvena svabhāvam avalokayan | smayamāna ivāste yas tasyeyaṃ kusṛtiḥ kutaḥ — regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course. Nābhāvo bhāvyatām eti na ca tatrāsty amūḍhatā | yato 'bhiyogasaṃsparśāt tadāsid iti niścayah — non-being does not become being; nor is there delusion there; from contact with effort, "it was then" is determination. Atas tatkṛtrimaṃ jñeyaṃ sauṣuptapadavat sadā | na tv evaṃ smaryamāṇatvaṃ tat tattvam pratipadyate — therefore that is artificial, to be known, like deep sleep always; but thus, being remembered, that tattva is not attained.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes spanda = Śiva's opening/closing (unmeṣa/nimeṣa) = source of śakti-wheel; flows undivided; supreme truth beyond suffering/pleasure; spanda operates through presiding over own-nature.

**KEY TERMS.** spanda (vibration), unmeṣa (opening), nimeṣa (closing), śakticakra (śakti-wheel), śaṅkara (Śaṅkara), pralaya (dissolution), udaya (arising), paramārtha (supreme truth), adhiṣṭhātṛ (presiding), kusṛti (evil course).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Spanda: to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise; where all effect abides, from which it proceeds; by uncovered nature, no obstruction; though waking etc. differ, flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature; I am happy/sad/attached etc.; happiness etc. states strung through abide elsewhere; where no suffering/pleasure/grasper/delusion, that exists in supreme truth. (2) Operation: regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course; non-being does not become being; nor delusion there; from contact with effort, "it was then" is determination; therefore artificial, to be known, like deep sleep; being remembered, tattva not attained.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.1–1.19.

## span-p03–p04 — danger and jīvanmukta
**SUMMARY.** Aprabudhadhiyas tv ete svasthitisthaganodyatāḥ | pātayanti duruttāre ghore saṃsāravartmani — those with unawakened intellect, rising to conceal own-state, cast into terrible, hard-to-cross saṃsāra-path. Ataḥ satatam udyuktaḥ spandatattvaviviktaye | jāgrad eva nijam bhāvam acireṇādhigacchati — therefore constantly striving for spanda-tattva-distinction, waking, quickly attains own-state. Atikruddhaḥ prahṛṣṭo vā kiṃ karomīti vā mṛśan | dhāvan va yat padaṃ gacchet tatra spandaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ — whether very angry or delighted, thinking "what shall I do", running, whatever state one goes to, there spanda is established. Tasmāc chabdārthacintāsu na sāvasthā na yā śivaḥ | bhoktaiva bhogyabhāvena sadā sarvatra saṃsthitaḥ — therefore in sound/meaning/thought, no state that is not Śiva; enjoyer alone, in enjoyable-form, always everywhere established. Iti vā yasya saṃvittiḥ krīḍātvenākhilaṃ jagat | sa paśyan satataṃ yukto jīvanmukto na saṃśayaḥ — thus whose consciousness, playfully, entire world; he, seeing, constantly united, jīvanmukta, no doubt. Ayam evodayas tasya dhyeyasya dhyāyicetasi | tadātmatāsamāpattir icchataḥ sādhakasya yā — this alone is arising of that meditable, in meditator's mind; identity-absorption of that, of wishing practitioner.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes danger (unawakened intellect → saṃsāra), remedy (constant striving, spanda in every state), jīvanmukta (world as play, constantly united, Śiva in all).

**KEY TERMS.** aprabudhadhī (unawakened intellect), saṃsāravartman (saṃsāra-path), udyukta (striving), spandatattva (spanda-tattva), jīvanmukta (liberated while living), krīḍā (play), dhyeya (meditable), samāpatti (absorption).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (spanda/operation); p03–p04 establish danger/jīvanmukta.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Danger/remedy: unawakened intellect conceals own-state → saṃsāra; constantly striving for spanda-distinction, waking, quickly attains own-state; spanda established in every state (anger, delight, running). (2) Jīvanmukta: in sound/meaning/thought, no state not Śiva; enjoyer alone in enjoyable form; world as play; seeing, constantly united, jīvanmukta; identity-absorption of meditable.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.20–1.6.

## span-p05–p06 — body and close
**SUMMARY.** Anenādhiṣṭhite dehe yathā sarvajñatādayaḥ | tathā svātmany adhiṣṭhānāt sarvatraivam bhaviṣyati — in this-presided body, as omniscience etc.; so in self, by presiding, everywhere thus it will be. Glānir viluṅthikā dehe tasyāścājñānataḥ sṛtiḥ | tad unmeṣaviluptaṃ cet kutaḥ sā syād ahetukā — languor/rolling in body; from its ignorance, course; if that is destroyed by opening, whence would it be causeless. Ekacintāprasaktasya yataḥ syād aparodayaḥ | unmeṣaḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ svayaṃ tam upalakṣayet — from single-thought-attachment, whence other-arising; that opening is to be known; oneself should mark it. Seyaṃ kriyātmikā śaktiḥ śivasya paśuvartinī | bandhayitrī svamārgasthā jñāta siddhyupapādikā — this action-natured śakti of Śiva, abiding in paśu; binding, on own-path-established, known, attainment-producing. Tanmātrodayarūpena mano 'haṃbuddhivartinā | puryaṣṭakena saṃruddhas taduttham pratyayodbhavam — by tanmātra-arising-form, mind/I-buddhi-abiding; by eight-city enclosed, that-arising-cognition-arising. Bhuṅkte paravaśo bhogaṃ tadbhāvāt saṃsared ataḥ | saṃsṛtipralayasyāsya kāraṇaṃ sampracakṣmahe — enjoys dependent enjoyment; from that-state, wanders in saṃsāra; of this saṃsāra-dissolution, we declare the cause.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes body's spanda (omniscience by presiding, languor from ignorance, opening destroys it) and close (śakti of Śiva abiding in paśu binds; tanmātra-arising; eight-city; dependent enjoyment → saṃsāra; declares cause of saṃsāra-dissolution).

**KEY TERMS.** glāni (languor), viluṅthikā (rolling), unmeṣa (opening), bindu (bindu), nāda (nāda), kriyātmikā (action-natured), paśu (paśu), puryaṣṭaka (eight-city), paravaśa (dependent), saṃsṛtipralaya (saṃsāra-dissolution).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p03–p04 (danger/jīvanmukta).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Body: in presided body, omniscience etc.; so in self, by presiding, everywhere; languor/rolling in body from ignorance; if destroyed by opening, whence causeless; from single-thought-attachment, whence other-arising; opening to be known and marked. (2) Close: this action-natured śakti of Śiva abiding in paśu binds; by tanmātra-arising, mind/I-buddhi; enclosed in eight-city; dependent enjoyment → saṃsāra; declares cause of saṃsāra-dissolution.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.7–1.2.
