## bh-p01 — veneration (v. 1)

Bhairava — pervading the particular states of moving and unmoving things, consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless — Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate. The stotra opens with the Lord's all-pervading nature and the devotee's stance: total surrender of mind to Bhairava, who is the refuge of those without refuge. Veneration is not ritual but mental absorption — the mind becoming His. The Sanskrit: *vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣaṃ cinmayam ekam anantam anādim | bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande.*

## bh-p02 — identity (v. 2)

All this, become of You, now appears to me by the power of Your grace. You, Maheśa, are always my self; by You all is mine. The Lord is not other than the self; grace first discloses the world as His, then identity is declared, and through Him, everything becomes mine. The Sanskrit: *tvanmayam etad aśeṣam idānīṃ bhāti mama tvadanugrahaśaktyā | tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam.*

## bh-p03 — no fear-talk, suffering conceded (v. 3)

With You as Lord, all-pervading in own self — therefore no talk of transmigration-fear. Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts are present. The devotee denies fear-talk while conceding affliction: no fear is discussed, yet suffering is not denied. The Sanskrit: *svātmani viśvagaye tvayi nāthe tena na saṃsṛtibhītikathāsti | satsv api durdharaduḥkhavimohatrāsavidhāyiṣu karmagaṇeṣu.*

## bh-p04 — the averted gaze, power-filled (v. 4)

O Ender, turn not toward me this most wrathful-terrible gaze. By Śaṅkara-service, contemplation-firm, I am terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled. Service and firm contemplation → Bhairava-power-filled → the gaze of death turned aside. The devotee becomes terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled through service and contemplation. The Sanskrit: *antaka māṃ prati mā dṛśam enāṃ krodhakarālatamāṃ vinidhehi | śaṅkarasevanacintanadhīro bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo 'smi.*

## bh-p05 — darkness dispelled, fearlessness (v. 5)

Thus — my great darkness dispelled by rays of world-awareness: death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never I fear. Awareness itself has burned the darkness away; what remains before death's ministers is salutation, not fear. The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ upoḍhabhavanmayasaṃviddīdhitidāritabhūritamisraḥ | mṛtyuyamāntakakarmapiśācair nātha namo 'stu na jātu bibhemi.*

## bh-p06 — true awakening, bliss (v. 6)

The true essence of world-objects is seen by rays of arisen true awakening. In being-full, in you, I, self, bliss I enter. Bliss = entering the full being of the Lord, disclosed by awakening. The Sanskrit: *proditasatyavibodhamarīciprekṣitaviśvapadārthasatattvaḥ | bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇe tvayy aham ātmani nirvṛtim emi.*

## bh-p07 — affliction arrives, nondifference arises (v. 7)

When the affliction-state, producing slight anguish, comes into the mind's range — Lord, then alone my with-You-nondifference; hymn-supreme-nectar-rain arises. Affliction's arrival is the occasion, not the obstacle: with it comes nondifference, and the hymn rains. The Sanskrit: *mānasagocaram eti yadaiva kleśadaśā 'tanutāpavidhātrī | nātha tadaiva mama tvadabhedastotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti.*

## bh-p08 — rites relativized, scripture contemplated (v. 8)

O Śaṅkara, truly — vow, giving, bathing, austerity dispel becoming's heat. Rites are not despised but relativized. Your-scripture supreme-nectar-contemplation in mind, bliss-stream flows. Scripture-contemplation → bliss-stream in mind. The Sanskrit: *śaṅkara satyam idaṃ vratadānasnānatapo bhavatāpavidāri | tāvakaśāstraparāmṛtacintā syandati cetasi nirvṛtidhārām.*

## bh-p09 — rejoicing consciousness, obtaining (v. 9)

This consciousness of mine dances, sings, deeply rejoices — O Bhairava-Lord. You, dear, having-obtained, beautiful-vision alone, hard-to-get-by-others, samaya-knower. Obtaining the Lord = beautiful vision (rare, samaya-knowing), celebrated dancing. The Sanskrit: *nṛtyati gāyati hṛṣyati gāḍhaṃ saṃvid iyaṃ mama bhairavanātha | tvāṃ priyam āpya sudarśanamekam durlabham anyajanaiḥ samayajñam.*

## bh-p10 — colophon and pacification (v. 10)

In the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn. By whom the Lord becomes, world's great-heat pacifies instantly, compassionate. The dated, signed hymn is sent outward: the Lord pacifies world-heat instantly, compassionately. The Sanskrit: *vasurasapauṣe kṛṣṇadaśamyām abhinavaguptaḥ stavam imam akarot | yena vibhur bhavamarusantāpaṃ śamayati janasya jhaṭiti dayāluḥ.*
