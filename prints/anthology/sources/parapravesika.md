# Parāprāveśikā — L2.v2 READ (+ Sanskrit maxims)

## par-p01 — the vow

> viśvātmikāṃ taduttirṇāṃ hṛdayaṃ parameśituḥ |
> parādiśaktirūpeṇa sphurantīṃ saṃvidaṃ numaḥ

Veneration to Śiva, whose very self is all the levels of reality. Here begins the Entering-into-the-Supreme, composed by glorious Kṣemarāja, noblest of the great Māheśvara teachers. We bow to consciousness itself — the heart of the supreme Lord — which has the world for its form and yet goes beyond it, pulsing in the shape of supreme power.

## par-p02 — light knows

> iha khalu parameśvaraḥ prakāśātmā, prakāśaśca vimarśasvabhāvaḥ
> vimarśo nāma viśvākāreṇa viśvaprakāśena viśvasaṃharaṇena ca akṛtrimāham iti visphuraṇam
> yadi nirvimarśaḥ syāt anīśvaro jaḍaśca prasajyeta

The supreme Lord, here, is made of light — and light is made of awareness. (Why not bare knowing, after the calm-brahman teachers? Because awareness so specified — bounded and boundless, world-shaping, world-lighting, world-dissolving, full-I joy throughout.) Awareness is the flash of an I never put together. And if that awareness were missing, lordlessness and deadness would follow — as sunlight, which lacks awareness, leans on another to shine and sinks to the inert.

## par-p03 — ten names, one throb

> eṣa eva ca vimarśaḥ — cit, caitanyaṃ, svarasoditāparāvāk, svātantryaṃ, paramātmano mukhyamaiśvaryaṃ, kartṛtvaṃ, sphurattā, sāro, hṛdayaṃ, spandaḥ ityādiśabdairāgameṣūddhoṣyate

This very awareness is what the scriptures trumpet under many names. Each earns its name by its work: consciousness as the Self's chief form; the supreme Word speaking the world by grasping it back; freedom binding and loosing the this; sovereignty needing nothing else; doership raising śiva-to-earth into this-ness; pulsing as mine-flashing (else all omniscient or all blind); essence, heart, vibration — ground, rest, and shimmer of the world.

## par-p04 — the world His shining

> akṛtrimāhamiti satattvaḥ svayaṃprakāśarūpaḥ parameśvaraḥ pārameśvaryā śaktyā śivādi-dharaṇyantajagadātmanā sphurati prakāśate ca
> etadeva asya jagataḥ kartṛtvamajaḍatvaṃ ca
> kāryatvamapi etadadhīnaprakāśatvameva

The unmade-I Lord, self-lit, pulses and shines by the Lady's power as the world from Śiva to clay — and this shining is at once His making and the world's aliveness. (Made as different? His freedom breaks. As not different? How made?) Made means shining-by-Him; the world is nothing apart from its maker. (If the known stood apart, nothing could shine at all.) And nothing veils Him: the would-be veil destroys itself, and the world — His life-breath by His shining — cannot throttle what lets it stand.

## par-p05 — proof retired

> pūrvasiddhasya maheśvarasya svayaṃprakāśatvaṃ sarvasya svasaṃvedanasiddham
> pramāṇānyapi vastūnāṃ jīvitaṃ yāni tanvate | teṣāmapi paro jīvaḥ sa eva parameśvaraḥ

Indicators cannot grip the Lord proved before all proof — and the questioner asking "what proves Him?" is himself the proof, feeling himself. Consciousness-light appoints proofs for illusion's business; for Him none is used. Proof leans on Him; for Him who is proof's life, poor proof — whose office is resting fresh flashes in the knower — has no work but body-blue-pleasure knowables. The proofs that draw out things' very life are themselves outlived by a further Life: the supreme Lord.

## par-p06 — the alphabet-I and the thirty-six

> evaṃ ca śabdarāśimayapūrṇāhantāparāmarśasāratvāt paramaśiva eva ṣaṭtriṃśattattvātmakaḥ prapañcaḥ

His essence is the full I-grasp woven of sound-mass — "I alone, light-natured, in the shining" — so supreme Śiva Himself is the thirty-six-leveled display. A level is the one undivided shine that families divided families — hill-town-earth, river-sea-water. The thirty-six run: Śiva, power, eternal Śiva, Lord, pure knowledge, illusion, art, knowing, passion, time, necessity, person, nature, intellect, ego, mind, five knowings, five doings, five subtles, five gross.

## par-p07 — the dimming, the bondsman, the freed

The pure levels dim by faintness of the this-portion — will-first, knowing-first, doing-first — till illusion spreads difference and the Lord, veiling His form by freedom's other name, wears the person-mask: deluded by illusion, bound by deemed chains, wandering. The delusion never touches the Lord — the magician's own trick. Knowledge showing His form brings sovereignty to recognition: dense with consciousness, free while bodied, Śiva when the body falls. His five uncontracted glories take contraction — art, knowing, passion, time, necessity — as a robbed king is dealt back a pittance to live on.

## par-p08 — what each sheath takes, what each level gives

Little-doing, little-knowing, clinging ("may I grow, may I never cease"), time cutting was-am-will, must ("mine to do, not mine") — five, one sheath over full consciousness. Nature balances the three strands undivided. Deciding intellect, proud ego, resolving mind; ten senses in order; five commons ("that and nothing more"); five elements giving room, life, burning, flow, support.

## par-p09 — all inside the seed

> yathā nyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ | tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ viśvametaccarācaram

As tree-in-banyan-seed in power-form, so moving-unmoving world in heart-seed — peacock-egg-plumes, pot-clay, ghee-water all proving it. Of "being" itself, suffix stripped, bare s remains holding thirty-one; the pure levels enter assent-shaped au. The heart-seed — great-mantra-formed, world-formed, world-transcended — is supreme Śiva Himself as His own seat. Who knows and enters it is initiated indeed — this knowing, void of sesame-ghee oblation, the true rite — living free while breathing, the Lord Himself when the body falls.
