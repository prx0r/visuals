Stanza 1

न ध्यायतो न जपतः स्याद् यस्याविधिपूर्वकम् / एवम् एव शिवभासस् तं नुमो भक्तिशालिनम
I bow to that devotee who is glorified with the devotion of Lord Shiva and to whom the appearance of Lord Shiva takes place without conducting meditation or recitation of any kind.
