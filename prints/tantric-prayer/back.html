The prayers here are our own translations of the Sanskrit, checked line by line against the witnesses in our Kashmir Śaiva corpus (translation-work) — thirty-three from Utpaladeva's Śivastotrāvalī, plus Kṣemarāja's Ghatakarpara, Abhinavagupta's Kramastotra and Gītārthasaṅgraha. Each guide links its source file.

The rasa theory is Abhinavagupta's own — Abhinavabhāratī and Tantrāloka, quoted briefly and attributed; the references are flagged for verification against print editions. The three-layer naming follows Monier-Williams glosses and the conventional English rasa terms.

Swami Lakshmanjoo's material — the Aghora Mantra, its1989 letter, and the Śrī Bahurūpa-Garbha Stotra — is © the Hughes Family Trust, archived here and quoted briefly at his own instruction to "spread this message all over the world."
