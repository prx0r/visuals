# Gitarthasangraha a00 — L2 READ

## v.1
He who, made of the widely flashing wheel of diverse states, comes to be the objects of experience — whose one, given to contemplation, is thus the bond of nondifference: He, Śaṃbhura, woe-averting, triumphs — treasury of the lights of knowing.
The Sanskrit: *ya eṣa vitatasphuradvividhabhāvacakrātmakaḥ parasparavibhedavānviṣayatāmupāgacchati | yadekamayabhāvanāvaśata etyabhedānvayaṃ sa śaṃbhuraśivāpaho jayati bodhabhāsāṃ nidhiḥ || 1-1 ||*

## v.2
By the sage Dvaipāyana was this scripture expounded, measured by hundreds of thousands; here liberation is established as the fruit — and mādhānyataḥ [L0-fork], its other dharma-and-rest is recited to nourish it.
The Sanskrit: *dvaipāyanena muninā yadidaṃ vyadhāyi sāstraṃ sahasraśatasaṃmitamatra mokṣaḥ | mādhānyataḥ phalatayā prathitastadanya- dharmādi tasya paripoṣayituṃ pragītam || 1-2 ||*

## v.3
Liberation, by name, complete-differentiation in form, all-knowing, auspicious-natured in all instruments and more — free from need, in Bhagavān the Lord, ever-risen: dissolution — this much, toward it [prathiḥ L0-fork], in summary.
The Sanskrit: *mokṣaśca nāma sakalāpravibhāgarūpe sarvajñasarvakaraṇādiśubhasvabhāve | ākāṅkṣya virahite bhagavatyadhīśe nityodite laya iyānprathiḥ samāsāt || 1-3 ||*

## v.4
Although liberation by name is celebrated in other connections, yet the Bhagavadgītā is the giver of true attainment.
The Sanskrit: *yadyapyanyaprasaṅgeṣu mokṣo nāmātra gīyate | tathāpi bhagavadgītāḥ samyaktatprāptidāyikāḥ || 1-4 ||*

## v.5
In them, although earlier ones made many expositions, yet my attempt too is reasonable — revealing its hidden meaning.
The Sanskrit: *tāsvanyaiḥ prāktanairvyākhyāḥ kṛtā yadyapi bhūyasā | nyāyyastathāpyudyamo me tadgūḍhārthaprakāśakaḥ || 1-5 ||*

## v.6
From the oral tradition of Bhaṭṭa Indurāja, examined long with thought, made by Abhinavagupta — this here is the Gītārthasaṃgraha.
The Sanskrit: *bhaṭṭendurājādāmnāya vivicya ca ciraṃ dhiyā | kṛto'bhinavaguptena so'yaṃ gītārthasaṃgrahaḥ || 1-6 ||*
