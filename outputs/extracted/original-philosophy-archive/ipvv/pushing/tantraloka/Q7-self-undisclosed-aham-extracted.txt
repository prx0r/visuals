This takes place initially seemingly externally, when consciousness operates as and
through the conditioned ego, which it relates to objectivity (idam) as a subject (aham).
Inwardly the subject (aham) abides alone as the one, unique and pure self-awareness of
unconditioned consciousness, independent of it; then, through the activity of the deified
senses, as the great rays of reflective awareness radiate from the Sun of subjectivity at
one (tanmayatayā) with their object. In this way the boundless ocean of the perceiverʼs
consciousness is stirred up by the expansion of consciousness the senses arouse.
