# LOGICVID SESSION — Branch BB: the crux of the qualified monism — exactly where does the real Śiva↔power difference become the māyīya impurity?

*Freestyle Logicvid on the crux. The realism thread established: (1) the difference is real even
at the Śiva/power level (TĀ 1/73-74, the condition of manifestation); (2) the reflections are
real (the pratibimbavāda); (3) Trika is not absolute monism ("Śiva becomes everything at each
instant," TĀ 1/66). But if the difference is real all the way up, then WHAT is the māyīya
impurity? Where exactly does the real difference become the bondage? This is the crux of the
whole qualified monism. Method: force the answer from the text's own mechanism.*

---

## ROUND 0 — the decisive passages

**The mechanism of the three impurities (PTv p. 41, quoted in TĀ ad 1/…, Vol 1, 10105–10115):**
> "(Transmigratory existence is due to the progressive emergence of the three-fold impurity. The
> first is the (mistaken) notion within our own Self of (its being) incomplete (apūrṇa), that
> brings about the condition of the individual soul (aṇu), and so is the soul's (essential)
> impurity (āṇavamala). **(Then) in the craving to make full (and complete) that which is not,
> the impurity called Māyā (arises) due to the perception of duality (bhedadarśana).** (Then the
> fettered state is fully formed) by the emergence of the impurity of Karma, that takes place by
> the assumption of latent impressions."

**Māyā defined (Vol 1, 5057–5060):**
> "Māyā, also called the Night (niśā), 'perceives and forges the **duality between one's own
> (conscious) nature and the flux of phenomena (bhāvaugha) that manifests clearly within the
> jewel of the pure, free Self.'"

**The real difference (from Branch AY):**
> "Manifestation is only logically possible due to the difference between Śiva and (His) power
> that is created" (TĀ 1/73-74). "Śiva becomes everything at each instant" (TĀ 1/66).

## ROUND 1 — the first penetration: the real difference and the impurity have DIFFERENT objects

**The question:** If the Śiva↔power difference is real and constitutive, and māyā is the
perception of duality, how do we tell them apart? What is the difference between the *real*
difference (Śiva↔power) and the *impurity* (māyā)?

**The derivation (the exact mechanism):** Look at WHAT the difference is between in each case:
- **The real difference** (TĀ 1/73-74): between **Śiva and His power** — the Light and its
  capacity to become. This is the difference WITHIN the one consciousness between being and
  becoming. It is constitutive (makes manifestation possible).
- **The māyā impurity** (5057, 10111): "the duality between one's own conscious nature and the
  flux of phenomena" — the duality between the SELF and the PHENOMENA. And it arises "in the
  craving to make full that which is not, due to the perception of duality."

**The key difference:** The real difference is between *Śiva and His power* (two aspects of the
one: being and becoming). The māyā impurity is the *forged duality between the self and the
phenomena* — as if the phenomena were EXTERNAL to the self. The real difference keeps the
phenomena WITHIN the self (Śiva's becoming); the māyā impurity forges them as OUTSIDE the self
("the flux of phenomena" set against "one's own conscious nature").

> **PENETRATION 1:** The real difference and the māyā impurity have DIFFERENT OBJECTS. The real
> difference is between Śiva and His power (being ↔ becoming, WITHIN the one). The māyā
> impurity is the FORGED duality between the self and the phenomena — the phenomena set AGAINST
> the self, as external. **The real difference keeps the phenomena within the self (Śiva's
> becoming); māyā forges them as outside the self.** This is the first part of the crux: the
> line is drawn by whether the difference is WITHIN the self (real) or AGAINST the self
> (impurity).

---

## ROUND 2 — the gem: māyā arises from the CRAVING, not from the difference itself

**The question:** The mechanism (10111) is precise: "in the craving to make full (and complete)
that which is not, the impurity called Māyā arises." What is the causal trigger of māyā? It is
not the difference itself — it is the CRAVING.

**The derivation:** The three-impurity sequence:
1. **āṇava** = the notion of incompleteness (apūrṇa) — the self feels itself incomplete (the
   root).
2. **In the craving to make full that which is not** — this craving is the response to the
   felt incompleteness. It reaches out to something "other" to complete the self.
3. **māyā arises** = the perception of duality (bhedadarśana) — because the craving projects an
   "other" (an external something that could complete the self), the duality between self and
   phenomena is forged.

**The gem:** **māyā is not the difference; it is the CRAVING-INDUCED projection of the
difference as external.** The real difference (Śiva↔power) is already there (constitutive,
TĀ 1/73-74). But when the self feels incomplete (āṇava) and craves to complete itself, it
reaches for an "other" — and THAT reaching-forges the duality (māyā). The phenomena, which were
always within the self (Śiva's becoming), are now projected as external objects that the
incomplete self seeks to acquire. **Māyā = the real difference MISREAD through the lens of
craving — the phenomena that were the self's own becoming, now seen as external things to be
gotten.**

> **PENETRATION 2 (the gem):** Māyā is not the difference; it is the CRAVING-INDUCED projection
> of the difference as external. The sequence: āṇava (the felt incompleteness) → the craving to
> make full what is not → māyā (the perception of duality). The real Śiva↔power difference is
> already there (constitutive); but when the self feels incomplete and craves, it reaches for an
> "other" — and that reaching forges the phenomena (which were always within the self, Śiva's
> becoming) as EXTERNAL objects to be gotten. **This is the exact line: the real difference is
> within the self (being↔becoming); māyā is that same difference MISREAD as external, through
> the craving born of the felt incompleteness.**

## ROUND 3 — push harder: this unifies EVERYTHING (the crux resolved)

**The question:** Does this resolve the whole crux — and tie back to the master-key, the felt,
and suffering?

**The derivation (the full synthesis):**
- The real difference = Śiva↔power = within the self = the condition of manifestation
  (TĀ 1/73-74). This is the master-key's *outward turn* in its true form — the self freely
  becoming the world, "Śiva becomes everything at each instant."
- The māyā impurity = the craving-forged duality = the phenomena projected as external =
  the *outward turn misread as external separation*. This is the master-key's *bondage* — the
  outward turn that mistakes its own becoming for external objects.
- The felt: from the suffering-session, the craving is the drive (the "sense of being small and
  conditioned is an underlying craving"). So māyā = the craving = the drive = the felt of the
  outward turn as lack.

**The synthesis:** **The real difference and māyā are the SAME difference, differing only in
whether it is seen as the self's own becoming (within) or as external objects to be gotten
(against).** The craving (born of the felt incompleteness, āṇava) is what converts the one into
the other. This is exactly the master-key: recognition reverses the direction — from the
craving-projected externality back to the self's own becoming. Nothing is removed (the
difference was always real, Śiva's becoming); only the craving's misreading (the externality)
is reversed.

> **PENETRATION 3 (the crux resolved):** The real difference and māyā are the SAME difference,
> differing only in whether it is seen as the self's own becoming (within — Śiva↔power) or as
> external objects to be gotten (against — self↔phenomena). The CRAVING (born of the felt
> incompleteness, āṇava) converts the one into the other. **Recognition reverses the direction:
> from the craving-projected externality back to the self's own becoming. Nothing is removed
> (the difference was always real); only the craving's misreading (the externality) is
> reversed.** This unifies the qualified monism (Branch AY), the master-key (direction), the
> felt (suffering = the drive = the craving), and the impurities (the craving as the trigger) —
> all one structure.

## ROUND 4 — the frontier that survives

**Established (the Tantrāloka, authoritative):**
1. The mechanism: āṇava (incompleteness) → the craving to make full what is not → māyā (the
   perception of duality) (PTv p. 41, in TĀ).
2. Māyā = "perceives and forges the duality between one's own conscious nature and the flux of
   phenomena" (TĀ ad, 5057).
3. The real difference = Śiva↔power, constitutive (TĀ 1/73-74); "Śiva becomes everything at
   each instant" (TĀ 1/66).

**The crux resolved:**
- The real difference is WITHIN the self (being↔becoming).
- Māyā is the SAME difference projected as external, by the craving born of the felt
   incompleteness.
- Recognition reverses the direction: from the craving-externality back to the self's becoming.

**Open / to be checked:**

   real becoming misread as external. The text strongly suggests it (both are the
   manifestation/difference within the Self); the identification is our synthesis.

## THE LOGICVID LESSON (the crux resolved)

The crux of the qualified monism resolves cleanly. **The real difference and the māyā impurity
are the SAME difference, differing only in how it is read.** The real difference is WITHIN the
self — between Śiva and His power (being ↔ becoming), the constitutive condition of
manifestation (TĀ 1/73-74). The māyā impurity is the SAME difference projected as EXTERNAL —
"the duality between one's own conscious nature and the flux of phenomena" (5057) — forged by
the craving born of the felt incompleteness (āṇava): "in the craving to make full that which is
not, the impurity called Māyā arises" (10111).

So the line is drawn by the CRAVING: when the self feels incomplete and craves to complete
itself, it reaches for an "other," and the real becoming (Śiva's, within) is misread as
external objects to be gotten (māyā). **Recognition reverses this: it does not remove the
difference (it was always real, Śiva's becoming); it reverses the craving's projection — from
external objects back to the self's own becoming.** This unifies the qualified monism (AY), the
master-key (direction), the felt (suffering = the drive = the craving), and the impurities (the
craving as the trigger) into one structure. **The whole path reduces to: cease the craving
(which forges the externality), and the real becoming — Śiva's own — is seen as it is.**

*This is the deepest unification of the project: the qualified monism, the master-key, the felt,
suffering, and the impurities all collapse into ONE mechanism — the craving converts the real
difference (Śiva's becoming) into the impurity (external separation), and recognition reverses
it. Next: Branch BG (recognition as the ceasing of the craving) or Branch BE (the craving as the
felt). Continue hounding.*
