life. These are the predominant ones. To specify, rati is conducive to kama

whom it predominates is conducive to artha but can also end in kama and

always predominant in each drama, so that all are clearly recognised as
being, in turn (that is, one in this drama and the other in another one),

four of them will be seen to be present in the same drama, in various
