This process is technically called apoha, which one could translate as ‘denial’ or

‘negation’. This kind of analysis of the cognitive process and how we reach conclusions

is accepted by various schools of Indian philosophy beginning with its initial major
formulation by the Buddhist idealist Diṅnāga in his Pramāṇasamuccaya (see Torella
2002: 128-130). This is accepted here as a method which is applicable to an

examination of the teachings which establishes their validity, even as it elucidates them.
