## Arc logic (each end is the next start)

- S1 ends: freedom must include eclipse → *but why suffering-shaped eclipse?* → S2.
- S2 ends: terror is what play-gripped feels like → *does the terror do grace's work?* → S3.
- S3 ends: suffering makes thirst, thirst tastes grace → *so no thirst, no tasting, no liberation?* → S4.
- S4 ends: bondage is the background liberation needs → *but what if there's no background?* → S5.
- S5 ends: the ladder dissolves; the dissolution was the point. Branches to IPVV five-step chain (does it survive v14-style non-difference?) and TĀ masterkey (same structure, different mountain).
