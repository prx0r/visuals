## ROUND 0 — what the text actually says

1. `etau bandhavimokṣau ca parameśasvarūpataḥ | na bhidyete na bhedo hi tattvataḥ parameśvare` (v14, Sanskrit only — BLOCKED) — these-two bondage-and-liberation from-supreme-Lord-nature: not they-are-divided; not difference indeed in-truth in-supreme-Lord.
2. `yad etasyāparijñānaṃ tatsvātantryaṃ hi varṇitam | sa eva khalu saṃsāro mūḍānāṃ yo vibhīṣakaḥ` (v11, PASS) — non-knowledge = terrifying saṃsāra of fools.
3. `yat tattvasya parijñānaṃ sa mokṣaḥ parameśataḥ | tat pūrṇatvaṃ prabuddhānāṃ jīvanmuktiś ca sā smṛtā` (v13, PASS) — full knowledge = liberation; remembered living-liberation.

The tension: v11 and v13 build a two-story house (bondage below, liberation above, three doors between). v14 says there's no upstairs and no downstairs — one nature (parameśasvarūpataḥ), no division (na bhidyete), no difference in truth (na bhedo hi tattvataḥ). The text spent 13 verses distinguishing things it now says aren't different.

## ROUND 1 — the first penetration: does v14 cancel v11–v13?

**The question:** if there's no difference "in truth" (tattvataḥ) — then was the terror fake? Was the grace unnecessary? Was the remembering a performance? Did we just waste four sessions?

**What the text says when pushed:** v14 qualifies twice — "from supreme-Lord-nature" (parameśasvarūpataḥ, ablative: *from* the Lord's own-nature) and "in truth in the supreme Lord" (tattvataḥ parameśvare, locative: *in* the Lord). The non-difference is located: *from* and *in* the Lord's nature. It doesn't say "there is no bondage and no liberation" flat-out. It says: considered from-and-in the Lord's svabhāva, the two are undivided. v11's terror and v13's remembering aren't denied — they're *located* (at the fool's level and the awakened's level, respectively), while v14 names the level at which the levels collapse.

> **PENETRATION 1:** v14 doesn't demolish the house; it names the ground the house stands on. Bondage (v11) is true *of fools*; liberation (v13) is true *of the awakened*; non-difference (v14) is true *of the Lord's nature*. Three truths, three levels, no contradiction — because "in truth" (tattvataḥ) is itself a level-marker, not a level-eraser. The text isn't saying the terror wasn't felt. It's saying: from where the Lord is, the felt terror and the felt release are one undivided nature experiencing itself in two tenses. The sessions weren't pointless; they were the downstairs work that lets you understand what "no upstairs" means.

## ROUND 2 — the gem: the hymn is a ladder that shows you there was never ground

**The question:** OK so levels, fine — but then why build the house at all? Why 13 verses of bondage-terror-grace-remembering if the punchline is non-difference? Why not just *start* with v14?

**What the text says when pushed hardest:** because you can't *hear* v14 until you've climbed. "No difference" asserted to a fool in terror (v11) is cruelty or nonsense — it lands as "your suffering isn't real," which is the one thing you must never say to someone drowning (Session 2's limit). The hymn earns the right to v14 by *first* honoring the terror (v11), *then* offering doors (v12), *then* naming the remembering (v13) — so that when v14 says "not divided," the reader has *felt* both sides and can recognize their unity as *their own experience*, not as a doctrine imposed from above. The ladder isn't a mistake the summit corrects. The ladder is how the summit becomes *yours* instead of a rumor.

> **PENETRATION 2 (the gem):** The text's structure *is* its argument. v11–v13 aren't preliminary errors v14 fixes; they're the necessary curriculum without which v14 is just words. "No difference in truth" (na bhedo hi tattvataḥ) means nothing to someone who never distinguished bondage from liberation *first* — you can't un-divide what you never divided. So the hymn makes you divide (Sessions 1–4: forgetting, terror, grace, remembering) precisely so the un-dividing (v14) lands as *recognition* rather than *assertion*. The laugh at the end — :') hahahah — isn't the system failing. It's the system succeeding: you see the whole elaborate house was built to show you there was never ground, and the groundlessness was home all along. The pushing doesn't resolve. It *dissolves*. And the dissolution was the point from v1.
