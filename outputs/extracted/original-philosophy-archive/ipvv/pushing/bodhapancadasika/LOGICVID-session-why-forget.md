# LOGICVID SESSION — why would Śiva forget its liberated state in the first place?

*The question the text forces. v3 says śakti never even WANTS difference from śaktiman — identity eternal, like fire and burning. v11 says non-knowledge IS saṃsāra. So if the identity never wavers, who forgot? Chain shape: Extraction → Branch (like TĀ AY/BB qualified-monism crux).*

---

## ROUND 0 — what the text actually says

1. `śaktiś ca śaktimadrūpād vyatirekam na vāñchati | tādātmyam anayor nityaṃ vahnidāhikayor iva` (v3, PASS) — "Power too from power-possessor-form difference does not desire; identity of these two is eternal, as fire and burning."
2. `yad etasyāparijñānaṃ tatsvātantryaṃ hi varṇitam | sa eva khalu saṃsāro mūḍānāṃ yo vibhīṣakaḥ` (v11, PASS) — "What of it non-knowledge — that its autonomy thus stated; it indeed saṃsāra of fools who is terrifying."
3. `anastamitabhārūpas tejasāṃ tamasām api | ya eko 'ntar yadantaś ca tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca` (v1, Sanskrit) — one reality within, whose "end" are both the powers (light) and the darknesses.

The tension the text gives us: identity is eternal and unwanting-difference (v3), yet non-knowledge = terrifying saṃsāra (v11). Both stated. No bridge offered.

---

## ROUND 1 — the first penetration: what IS the forgetting?

**The question:** if śakti never wants difference — not once, not ever, it's *nityaṃ* — then forgetting looks impossible. So what is aparīkṣā/aparijñāna here?

**What the text says when pushed:** v11 doesn't say knowledge is absent; it says non-knowledge *of it* (etasyāparijñānaṃ) — of *this* autonomy (tatsvātantryaṃ). The autonomy is stated (varṇitam), described, present — and mis-known. So forgetting isn't losing something; it's mis-taking what's right here. Like fire forgetting it's hot while still burning.

> **PENETRATION 1:** The forgetting isn't an event that happened to Śiva. It's the structure of an autonomy so total it includes mis-taking itself. v3's "does not desire difference" (na vāñchati) is about *wanting* — śakti doesn't want out. But v11's non-knowledge isn't wanting-out; it's mis-seeing-while-in. You can be fire, never want to stop burning, and still not know you're fire. The identity holds; the recognition lapses. That's why both verses can be true at once.

---

## ROUND 2 — the gem: he forgets because he can, and canning is what he is

**The question:** OK so mis-seeing while being — but WHY would a liberated state include the capacity to mis-see itself? Why build forgetting into freedom?

**What the text says when pushed harder:** v1 — the one reality's "end" (yadantaḥ) is *both* powers and darknesses (tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca). Darkness isn't outside the one; it's one of its ends. So non-knowledge isn't a foreign invasion — it's one of the two things the one reality terminates in. Light and dark are both *his*.

> **PENETRATION 2 (the gem):** He forgets because an autonomy that couldn't mis-take itself wouldn't be autonomy — it'd be a mechanism. The darkness (tamas) is listed *inside* the one reality (v1), not as its enemy but as its other end. So forgetting isn't a bug Śiva suffers; it's the proof his freedom is total — total enough to include its own eclipse. The terror of saṃsāra (vibhīṣakaḥ, v11) is what total freedom feels like from the inside of its own shadow. He didn't lose liberation; he exercised it all the way down.

---

## Claim statuses

- **STATED:** v3 (identity eternal, fire/burning); v11 (non-knowledge = saṃsāra, terrifying); v1 (one reality ending in both light and dark).
- **ESTABLISHED:** non-knowledge is mis-taking, not absence (from v11's grammar: aparīkṣā *of it*, with autonomy *stated*).
- **REQUIRES UNSTATED PREMISE:** "autonomy must include mis-taking to be total" — the text states both freedom and forgetting but never says one requires the other. That's my bridge.
- **RIVAL UNRESOLVED:** whether tamas in v1 means metaphysical darkness (a real pole) or just the absence that light defines itself against.

## HONEST LIMIT

The text never explains *why* the one reality has two ends instead of one. It states light-and-dark (v1), eternal identity (v3), and terrifying non-knowledge (v11) — and leaves the *why* untouched. The "freedom must include eclipse" move is mine, not his.

## THE BRANCHES (the next Logicvids)

> **Branch B1 — the eclipse test:** if freedom includes its own eclipse, is there anything freedom *can't* do? Push: does svātantrya include ceasing to be svātantrya? (v7 BLOCKED — Sanskrit only; needs English.)
> **Branch B2 — tamas as pole vs absence:** v1 lists darkness as an "end" (anta). Is it a real terminus or a privation? Ask of Spanda (languor/rolling from ignorance — destroyed by opening?) and Śivasūtra (1.9 aviveko māyā sauṣuptam).

## THE LOGICVID LESSON

**Why would Śiva forget?** Because an autonomy that couldn't forget wouldn't be autonomy. The identity holds eternally (v3); the recognition lapses structurally (v11); both terminate in the one reality (v1). Forgetting isn't what happened to freedom — it's what freedom *does* when it's total. The terror is the receipt.
