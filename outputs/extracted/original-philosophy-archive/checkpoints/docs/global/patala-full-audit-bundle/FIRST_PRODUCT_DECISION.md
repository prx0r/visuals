# FIRST PRODUCT DECISION

## The first public scholar product: Translation Audit

Why:
- immediate job-to-be-done;
- can start honestly with deterministic checks;
- exposes existing source/provenance work;
- creates structured correction data;
- naturally feeds benchmark;
- easier adoption than a Workbench;
- narrower failure surface than generic peer review.

## The first strategic asset: PĀṬALA-IPVV Benchmark

Why:
- measures your own claims;
- creates research credibility;
- attracts model builders;
- converts expert judgment into defensible data;
- calibrates Audit;
- private locked set creates durable evaluation value.

## The first internal engine: Autonomous Factory

Why:
- continuously creates difficult cases;
- gives Audit/Benchmark real material;
- lets Pāṭala optimize for human review burden.

## The first mega-product later: Pāṭala Review

Why:
- uniquely combines primary source grounding + argument structure + executable correction;
- could become the "research compiler";
- but only credible once source/argument/audit layers are measured.

## Workbench comes last

Workbench has highest lock-in but also highest switching cost and largest UX burden.
Do not ask scholars to move their research process into Pāṭala until Pāṭala already saves them meaningful time.
