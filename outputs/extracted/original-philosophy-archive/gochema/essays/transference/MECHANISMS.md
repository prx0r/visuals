# TRANSFERENCE MECHANISMS — Tibetan vs Hindu, step by step (2026-10-24)

> The how-it-works layer. Every entry points at the banked passage, not a summary
> of a summary. Tibetan = ejection mechanics (trainable, testable by signs).
> Hindu = voluntary exit + body-entry (fenced by Abhinavagupta, lineage intact).

## TIBETAN PHOWA — the mechanism

**Training (Karma Chakme, banked in SOURCE.md; TBOTB Ch.10 lines 8476–8599):**
1. Self-generation as Avalokiteśvara (white, 4 arms, vajra posture); inside, the
   body is hollow — central channel with 4 specs: white outside (bliss), red
   inside (clarity), upright (bodhisattva path), sealed base (lower doors shut),
   open crown (brahma-aperture).
2. Amitābha + retinue (Avalokiteśvara right, Vajrapāṇi left) a forearm's length
   above the crown, big toes at the channel's upper tip.
3. Heart HRĪḤ (red, moon-disc, prāṇa-mind bindu) emits HRĪḤ syllables that seal
   the nine doors one by one: hells → animal → hungry ghost → desire gods →
   demi/form gods → yakṣa/human → form realm → formless (cranial spot).
4. HIK (bindu shoots up, touches Amitābha's big toe) → KA (descends to heart),
   ~21×, with breath retention + vajra-fists. Signs of success: heat/itch at
   crown, soft skull spot, blood/serum at aperture, kuśa grass stands planted.
   Then STOP (persisting risks the life-force).

**Application at death — self (TBOTB 8599–8660; Karma Chakme ii):** after 3×
deceiving-death ritual; seal nine doors with HRĪḤ; propel bindu+HRĪḤ into
Amitābha's heart repeatedly until breathing stops; others recite alongside.

**Application — others (TBOTB 9140+; Karma Chakme ii):** head upright after outer
breath ceases + vital channel cut; refuge/bodhicitta aloud; same visualization
done mentally by the practitioner; verses recited melodiously 7–21×; loud PHAṬ;
pull one crown hair (lifts freely = success; else warmth/serum/steam); window =
death to 3 nights, acceptable to day 7 (Book of Kadam).

**Nine doors → destiny (Karma Chakme; TBOTB Ch.10 corroborates):** crown→pure
lands/formless (two traditions on which aperture); eyes→cakravartin; left
nostril→clean human; right nostril→yakṣa; ears→form realm; navel→desire gods;
urethra→animal; secret place→hungry ghost; anus→hells.

**Forceful phowa (TBOTB 8915–8955; Karma Chakme):** condemned as deity-murder +
suicide unless the lifespan is exhausted and deceiving-death rites done thrice.
The fence around the whole practice.

## HINDU UTKRĀNTI — the mechanism

**Voluntary exit (TĀ 14/32cd–39, indexed vol11:27240):** the yogin quits the body
at will (utkrāntikrama, vol10:7836). Non-yogi fallback chain: sacred bathing
site → starvation (kālavancana, cheating death, vol10:7825–7850).

**Ritual core (MV 17/25–27 via TĀ 19/54cd ff., ahnika-04:737):** Mālinīvijayottara
method of ritual suicide; Abhinavagupta quotes it but treats it SEPARATELY from
yoga praxis — the fence, same structural role as the forceful-phowa ban.

**Exit route (vol11:3582):** piercing marman points to force the vital breath out
(utkrānti). Dvādaśānta line (ahnika-04:16183) = the 12-finger upper terminus the
breath is driven toward. Compare Tibetan: bindu driven up the avadhūtī to the
brahma-aperture — same vertical mechanics, different cartography.

**Entering another body (TĀ vol10:15715, parapurapraveśa):** disembodied Kaula
teachers + consorts "play with these and those bodies" of the living via yogic
entry; Kṣemarāja's Spandasaṃdoha gloss + Jayaratha commentary in the notes.
Compare Tibetan: grong 'jug lineage DEAD (Karma Chakme + Achard agree) — the
Hindu line is textually alive, practice status unknown; the Tibetan line is
declared extinct by its own holders.
