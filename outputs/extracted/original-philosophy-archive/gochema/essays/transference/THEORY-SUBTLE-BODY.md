# THEORY — subtle body mechanics behind transference (2026-10-24)

> The hardware manual both traditions assume. Verbatim where quoted; Flood =
> archive.org full text (fair-use extracts, book buyable); layayoga guide =
> in-volume comparative notes (Goswami's *Layayoga*).

## 1. THE MASTER KEY: pur = city, body = city

*Parapurapraveśa* = entering (praveśa) another's (para) city (pura) — and *pura*
is the standard Sanskrit word for the body as a city (cf. *puryastaka*, the
"eightfold city" = the subtle body of eight constituents). Grong 'jug is the
exact calque: 'jug = enter, grong = city/body. Both traditions name the same
operation: moving the occupant between cities.

Flood's textual anchor (The Tantric Body, possession chapter):

"soul enters the inner lotus of this subtle body (puryastaka) through the
aperture of the absolute from which it had earlier vacated its residence."

## 2. FLOOD — āveśa: possession as the general category (verbatim)

"samavesa, whose primary designation is, like avesa, 'possession', coming from
the root vis, 'to enter', but which comes to mean 'immersion' in non-dual
consciousness for the Saiva theologian Abhinavagupta."

"Possession has a 'good' aspect when the deity enters a performer and so gives
a blessing (darsana)… or a 'bad' aspect when possession is uninvited and
manifested as illness."

"the body becoming divine through entextualisation, through the identification
of the self with the 'text' both oral and written."

Use: parapraveśa/grong 'jug are SPECIAL CASES of āveśa — willed, yogic,
doctrinally fenced. The film's theory beat: entering bodies was NORMAL (gods,
spirits, yogis all do it); the traditions argue only over WHO may and WHEN.

## 3. TIBETAN HARDWARE — tsa / lung / thigle (from banked Karma Chakme)

- Central channel, 4 specs: white outside (bliss), red inside (clarity),
  upright (bodhisattva path), sealed base (lower doors shut), open crown
  (brahma-aperture). Full quote in quotes-karmachakme.txt.
- Vehicle: bindu + white HRĪḤ (prāṇa-mind gathered to a drop). Propulsion:
  HIK (up) / KA (down), ×21, with breath retention + vajra-fists.
- Dissolution at death, 8 stages (in-repo glossary/concepts/dissolution.json):
  earth→water→fire→wind→space, then redness, whiteness, blackness, then the
  Inner Radiance (clear light). Each stage = cessation of a conceptual pattern.
- Exit map: 9 doors → 9 destinies (quotes-karmachakme.txt).

## 4. HINDU HARDWARE — nāḍī / prāṇa / kuṇḍalinī (from banked TĀ + layayoga guide)

- Ascent: "upward ascent out through the Cavity of Brahmā (brahmarandhra) at
  the top of the head into the End of the Twelve and beyond, as takes place in
  the perfect death that leads to liberation" (quotes-tantraloka.txt, vol9).
- Exit sign: kuṇḍalinī at the Cavity of Brahmā "as if pulsing with light
  (sphurantī)", body below throat lifeless (vol9:27830).
- Laya = ABSORPTION not dissolution (layayoga guide, Goswami): emitted
  principles reabsorbed into source — the return trip, not destruction.
- Bindu reduction (layayoga guide, Goswami): "The atom is the bindu of matter;
  the nucleus the bindu of a protoplasmic cell; and samadhi consciousness the
  bindu of the mind." The whole operation reduces multiform consciousness to a
  point — compare Tibetan bindu+HRĪḤ drop.
- Mantra = kundalinī in sound form (layayoga guide): "Mantra is an aspect of
  Kundalini, and it is in sound-form." Compare HIK/KA syllables as the Tibetan
  propulsion mantras — both systems DRIVE the drop with syllables.

## 5. THE COMPARISON (film-ready)

Both move a concentrated drop (bindu) up a central channel out the crown
aperture into/through light, propelled by syllables, with breath held. Tibetan
maps the EXITS (9 doors → destinies, post-mortem navigation). Hindu maps the
ASCENT (brahmarandhra → dvādaśānta → liberation-or-exit, willed death).
Same hardware, different dashboard: Tibet drives the dead, Kashmir drives the
dying.
