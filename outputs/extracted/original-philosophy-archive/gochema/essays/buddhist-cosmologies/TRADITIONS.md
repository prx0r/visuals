## 1. EARLY SUTTAS (the source layer — CH.3)
- **Universe:** the six sense-fields as "world" [WEAVE B7, SN 35.107:
  origin-of-world = origin-of-contact]; the four elements; devas/brahmā
  present as social facts of the teaching, non-load-bearing for liberation.
- **How it starts:** no first cause — paṭiccasamuppāda is beginningless
  chain [B13-frame]; the cosmos-cycles are background, not thesis. TWO
  origin-stories do explicit work: (a) **Aggañña (DN 27)** — the Buddha's
  OWN social origin-myth, told to break the Brahmin caste-claim "born of
  the mouth of Brahma" [FETCHED — BuddhaSasana: "primarily to refute the
  false claims of the Brahmin Caste"; ACQUIRE: full sutta extract via
  FoJin] — first king = elected, decline = greed; (b) **DN 1's Brahmā**
  [WEAVE B3/B4] — the creator who forgot his own mind-made origin: the
  tradition's instruction manual for reading ALL origin-stories.
- **Made of:** experience + kamma; the map optional, the chain not.
- **Frontier:** Gethin (no system in the Nikāyas — SCHOLARSHIP) ·
  Analayo S19 ("world as construct of the senses") · MN 63's refusal [B13].

## 2. THERAVĀDA COMMENTARIAL (the Vism system — CH.4)
- **Universe:** 31 planes (kāma 11 / rūpa 16 / arūpa 4) as psychophysical
  addresses; flat-earth Sumeru + four continents; sun/moon circle it
  [ACQUIRE: Vism chapter extracts — in-house BPS PDF].
- **How it starts — the four-phase cycle** [FETCHED — Berzin,
  StudyBuddhism Abhidharma-vs-Kālacakra, full read this session]:
  EVOLVING — "From the potentials for the elements and from the
  collective karma of those who will be reborn in this universe, there
  first develops the gentle breeze of the wind mandala… a cloud with the
  essence of gold… from the best elements comes a Mount Meru" — the world
  condenses out of collective karma, element by element; then "Since
  Brahmā appears first, he thinks that he creates all that appears after
  him, and from this comes the notion of a creator god" (the Vism mirrors
  DN 1's trap in its own cosmogony). First beings = mind-made, fed on
  concentration, luminous, no sun/moon yet; eating soil → light lost →
  sun/moon appear → laziness → hoarding → theft → elected king → decline.
  ENDURING — 20 intermediate eons, lifespans swing 10 ↔ 80,000 (the
  DN 26 [WEAVE B2] ten-year floor is this machine's low point). DISINTEGRATING
  — beings ascend by jhāna, then 7 suns (last leaves "not even the ashes").
  EMPTY — then the cycle repeats, superclusters out of sync (there is
  always somewhere to be born).
- **Made of:** no creator; kamma-wind physics; rūpa-arūpa as immersion-
  depth; top floors have no location [banked: Berzin "the plane of
  formless beings has no location"].
- **Frontier:** Buddhadasa-vs-Bodhi (S9-shelf) · Gornall 2024 (history of
  Theravāda cosmological literature, citation-only) · Analayo S19/S20.

## 3. SARVĀSTIVĀDA / ABHIDHARMAKOŚA (Vasubandhu — CH.4 companion)
- **Universe:** same skeleton as #2 ("substantially the same, differing
  only occasionally on minor points" — Enc. of Buddhism, banked) — plus
  **atomic kalāpas**: rūpa = discrete compounds; 75 dharmas as the real.
- **How it starts:** same collective-karma condensation, with more
  mechanics: "The physical world is brought about due to the collective
  karma of all beings… not organized according to some plan or divine
  willpower. There is no Creator" [FETCHED — Kotyk 2022, S16]; sun/moon
  propelled by karma-wind ("he attempts to explain the orbit of the Sun
  and the Moon… propelled by a 'wind'… brought about by the collective
  karma of beings" — Kotyk fetched]; "Vasubandhu shows no awareness of a
  spherical-earth or the planets."
- **Made of:** atoms + dharmas; causation without agent.
- **Frontier:** Kotyk 2022 ("inherited… remaining faithful" — S16) · the
  Dalai Lama's doubt about whether Vasubandhu believed his own map
  (S-frontier) · SEP/IEP Vasubandhu (fetched).

## 4. YOGĀCĀRA (mind-only — CH.9's mind-made reading, institutional)
- **Universe:** vijñaptimātra — "what we are aware of and have access to
  in cognition is nothing more than the very contents of our own minds
  caused, or at least deeply influenced, by the ālayavijñāna's seeds"
  [FETCHED — SEP Yogācāra 2024].
- **How it starts:** seed-ripening — karmic bīja in the storehouse
  consciousness flower into world-experience (parallel to be made at
  draft with Ñāṇananda's seed-field [WEAVE N5]: kamma-field/seed/moisture
  — the SAME image doing the same job in two schools).
- **Made of:** consciousness (+ its parikalpita/svabhāvata-trikāya
  analysis); three natures: imagined (linguistic-conceptual) / dependent /
  consummate. Positions itself as middle between "Madhyamaka nihilism"
  and "Abhidharma reification" [FETCHED — SEP via Saṃdhinirmocana].
- **Frontier:** the idealism debate — ontological vs epistemological
  "mere consciousness" ["hotly debated doctrine… quite difficult to
  determine whether… holds true at the ultimate level" — SEP 2024,
  fetched] · Madhyamaka-vs-Yogācāra allies/rivals (Garfield & Westerhoff
  2015, fetched) · Laṅkāvatāra "three worlds are mind-only" line
  [ACQUIRE: Chinese original via FoJin — earlier search missed it].

## 5. MADHYAMAKA (two truths — CH.9's deconstructive reading, institutional)
- **Universe:** conventional only — "Causality and arising… may be
  indispensible to conventional truth, but they cannot be features of
  reality" [FETCHED — SEP Madhyamaka].
- **How it starts:** at the conventional level: dependently, without
  first cause (beginningless); at the ultimate: "phenomena do not come
  into being" [FETCHED — SEP]. No creation-problem, because the question
  is a conventional-level question with conventional-level answers only.
- **Made of:** designation + emptiness; svabhāva nowhere.
- **Frontier:** Svātantrika-vs-Prāsangika true-convention fight ["true
  convention" vs collapse-into-anything-goes — SEP/IEP, fetched] ·
  **the radical line** [FETCHED — Britannica]: "If this world of constant
  change is not real, neither can the serial transmigration be real, nor
  its opposite, nirvana. Transmigration and nirvana being equally unreal,
  they are one and the same." — samsāra=nirvāṇa as the frontier bomb.

## 6. TIBETAN ABHIDHARMA (the default map — CH.6's institutional home)
- **Universe/origin:** inherits #2/#3 as the base layer (same condensation
  from collective karma); adds a scholarly astronomy tradition (skar rtsis
  = "calculation of the stars") [FETCHED — Sheehy 2020].
- **Made of:** same atoms+kamma physics; three-poisons weather system for
  the wheel [IN-HOUSE: tibetan-book-of-dead-reference.md deity/poison/
  realm table].
- **Frontier:** the repair-manual tradition — Khedrup Norsang Gyatso
  (1423–1513) "Resolving Contradictions Between Kālacakra and Abhidharma
  Cosmology" [FETCHED — Sheehy] · Gendun Chopel's spherical-earth question
  ("not raised in Tibet until the twentieth century" — Lopez via Sheehy) ·
  the Dalai Lama two-step [S-frontier: "abandon many aspects… did
  Vasubandhu believe it?"].

## 7. KĀLACRAKA (the parallel universe — CH.10's body-measures source)
- **Universe:** a SECOND geometry: round Meru (vs square), six rings (vs
  seven), 16 form-heavens (vs 17), double-length mile, colors shifted,
  hells inside the elemental mandalas [FETCHED — Berzin side-by-side,
  banked S4]. Sun/moon motion differs; planets known; spherical planets
  from Kālacakra already [FETCHED — Sheehy].
- **How it starts:** cosmogony-after-destruction from SPACE + WIND:
  "the mind is the [productive] agent, the environment and inhabitants
  are the objects [created]… energy-winds gather the subtle particles
  [that remain in space following the destruction of the previous
  world-system]" [FETCHED — Kongtrul, Myriad Worlds excerpt via
  Shambhala] — then the body-measure parallel: cosmos built the way a
  body is built [S4].
- **Made of:** space-particles + energy-winds + mind-as-agent —
  "syncretism of Vaibhāṣika atomic theory, Sāṃkhya… and Jaina and
  Purāṇic cosmographies" [FETCHED — Sheehy footnote].
- **Frontier:** Sheehy 2020 (contemplative+historical contexts) ·
  Berzin's purpose-relativity [S4: "not formulated for navigation"] ·
  the Reconciliation chapter (see #6).

## 8. DZOGCHEN (the non-cosmology — the film's far pole)
- **Universe:** the DISPLAY of the primordial ground (gzhi). No origin:
  the ground "transcends time, any temporal language used to describe it
  … purely conventional … a state in which time is not a factor"
  [FETCHED — Wikipedia/Ground(Dzogchen)]; "before realization produced
  buddhas and nonrealization produced sentient beings" [FETCHED, same];
  Longchenpa's "groundless ground" [FETCHED].
- **How it starts:** it doesn't — and its proponents say so: the
  Dzogchen entry in Tibet's own four-system table is "the dazzling
  non-cosmology… revealing the creative principle to be awareness alone"
  [FETCHED — Kongtrul blurb]. What STARTS (apparent universes):
  ignorance-over-luminosity — "All the galaxies of this universe have
  appeared out of space… appears only within the primordial basis.
  Awareness produces enlightenment, and ignorance produces our ordinary
  mind" [FETCHED — Orgyen Chowang, Tricycle, 2024 = the most recent
  scholarly-adjacent voice banked].
- **Made of:** empty essence + luminous nature + responsive energy;
  **the identity line** [FETCHED — Namkhai Norbu]: "the fundamental
  ground of existence, both at the universal level and at the level of
  the individual, the two being essentially the same" — the strongest
  cosmos=person statement in ANY tradition banked (CH.10's thesis in
  Dzogchen words).
- **Frontier:** living-teacher exposition (Orgyen Chowang 2024, fetched)
  · academic: Dudjom/Kongtrul translations [ACQUIRE: Myriad Worlds book
  is paid — Enc. of Buddhism + Shambhala excerpts already banked] ·
  connects to CH.9's mind-made reading at the extreme pole.

## 9. PURE LAND (cosmology as promise — CH.8)
- **Universe:** Sukhāvatī — jeweled geography, lotus-birth, seven rows
  [FETCHED — Enc. of Buddhism/Britannica]; a buddha-field (buddhakṣetra)
  to the west.
- **How it starts:** **cosmogony-as-vow** — Dharmākara bodhisattva's 48
  vows, "fulfilled with the certainty of natural law" [FETCHED —
  Britannica] — the ONLY tradition whose universe begins as a PROMISE
  (CH.8's line, sourced).
- **Made of / the fight:** other-direction (a realm far away) vs mind-only
  ("non-dual with our world which only appears impure due to our minds")
  [FETCHED — Wikipedia] · sambhogakāya vs nirmāṇakāya readings [FETCHED]
  · the text itself evolved: hard-line "no women" → "nymphs" ambiguity
  [FETCHED — Harrison 1998] — the geography visibly redacted across
  centuries.
- **Frontier:** Harrison 1998 · self-power vs other-power (Hōnen/Shinran,
  fetched) · the commentarial "purity is in the mind" line [banked
  Chinese source: 淨穢在心，不在國土也].

## 10. ZEN / CHAN (no map — CH.9's zen fourth)
- **Universe:** not a place — mind's own movement: the wind/flag
  exchange (Huineng: not the wind, not the flag — "動自心耳" / "是見動"
  [FETCHED — T2075/X1554 excerpts] — ACQUIRE: exact passage + cite at
  draft).
- **How it starts:** it doesn't: Huang Po — "This Mind, which is without
  beginning, is unborn and indestructible… the arising and the
  elimination of illusion are both illusory" [FETCHED — Grove/
  Dharmapedia/Blofeld excerpts, S13] — no cosmogony because no
  arising-that-needs-one.
- **Made of:** One Mind; "All the Buddhas and all sentient beings are
  nothing but the One Mind, beside which nothing exists" [S13].
- **Frontier:** the one-mind idealism debate (ties to #4's frontier) ·
  Chan-history questions on Huang Po texts [ACQUIRE: T2012 verify].
