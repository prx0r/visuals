# THE SIX COMPRESSIONS — what / why / how per school

> House rule: three beats each. No hedging, no footnotes; sources in TREATMENT.md + SUTTAS.md.

## 1. TIBETAN — the transit
- **WHAT:** Dying is a power cut in stages. The five elements switch off one
  by one — earth (body goes heavy), water (mouth dries), fire (heat retreats
  to the heart), wind (breath rattles out) — and then the lights go on: Ground
  Luminosity, reality with the filters off. Miss it and you wake up dead in the
  bardo, a mental body with your habits, for up to 49 days, until karma-winds
  blow you into a womb.
- **WHY:** Because the grasping didn't stop when the body did. The same
  momentum that kept you scrolling keeps you going — craving is the fuel that
  outlives the engine.
- **HOW:** Recognize the Luminosity and you're done (liberation on the spot —
  this is tukdam: masters sitting undecayed for days). Don't recognize it and
  the bardo runs its program: peaceful deities, then wrathful ones (both your
  own mind, externalized), then the pull toward a birth matching your heaviest
  habits. Lamas read the manual aloud to the corpse so the dead mind can use it.

## 2. YOGĀCĀRA — the storehouse
- **WHAT:** There is no soul, but there IS a store. Every intentional act drops
  a seed (bīja) into the ālaya-vijñāna, perfuming it like sesame in oil
  (vāsanā). You are not a thing that continues — you are a harvest schedule.
- **WHY:** Because nothing is ever wasted. Every perception you ever had was
  grown from a past seed, and every perception plants the next one. The loop
  runs itself; "you" are its current weather.
- **HOW:** At death the active senses shut down and the storehouse withdraws,
  carrying the seed account. Under the right conditions a ripe seed sprouts as
  a new birth-consciousness — which is why your next life rhymes with this
  one's habits. Stop planting (wisdom, path) and the store empties; what
  remains is not nothing, it's unperfumed awareness.

## 3. THERAVĀDA — the relinking
- **WHAT:** Death is 17 thought-moments long. The life-continuum (bhavanga)
  vibrates, five weak javana moments take the death-object (a past deed, its
  symbol, or a sign of the destination), then cuti-citta — death consciousness —
  falls. In the very next moment, paṭisandhi-citta rises in a new existence on
  the same object. No gap, no traveller, no luggage carousel.
- **WHY:** Because the last javana moments registered a direction. Karma isn't
  a judge handing down sentences — it's the momentum of the final mental
  posture, like falling asleep in a chair facing a door and waking up walking
  through it.
- **HOW:** Conception needs three things at once: parents, season, and the
  being-to-be-reborn present (MN 38's gandhabba). Miss any one, no birth.
  The new being then runs its own continuum until its own cuti-citta — links
  in a chain, no chain-owner.

## 4. EARLY BUDDHISM (Ñāṇavīra + Ñāṇananda) — the vortex
- **WHAT:** Nothing travels because nothing is a thing. What you call "me" is
  a whirlpool between consciousness and name-and-form — Sāriputta's two
  bundles of reeds holding each other up. Pull one and the other falls. Death
  is the whirlpool dispersing; rebirth is one re-forming wherever the current
  of craving runs.
- **WHY:** Because the current never stopped. Ignorance + craving keep
  bending the stream back on itself (paṭicca-samuppāda), and a bent stream
  whirls. The whirlpool isn't punished into existence — it's what running
  water does around an obstruction called "I".
- **HOW:** The Buddha's fire sermon IS the mechanism: fire burns dependent on
  fuel (upādāna = grasping = fuel, one word); remove fuel, no fire — and it is
  absurd to ask which direction it went. Ask "where do I go after death" and
  you've smuggled in the self the whole teaching dissolves. Craving-sustained
  while craving lasts (SN 44.9); extinguished when it doesn't.

## 5. PURE LAND — the rescue
- **WHAT:** You can't do it yourself — this is the age of degenerate dharma
  and your practice will fail. So Amida did it for you: Dharmākara's vows
  built Sukhāvatī, and the 18th vow promises birth there to anyone who calls
  his Name in trust. At death he arrives with a retinue holding your lotus
  platform; you climb on and wake up in the Pure Land in one of nine grades.
- **WHY:** Because self-power is bankrupt and other-power isn't. Your
  karmic account is deep red; Amida's merit account is infinite and he signed
  it over. Refusing the transfer out of pride is the only thing that can stop it.
- **HOW:** Entrust (shinjin), say the Name (nembutsu), vow to be born there.
  That's the entire technology — Hōnen stripped 90-day mountain rituals down
  to one sentence. In Sukhāvatī the lotus opens (fast for the sincere, slow
  semesters in the bud for the doubtful), non-retrogression is guaranteed, and
  graduation means returning to save everyone else. Rebirth as a layover, not
  a sentence.

## 6. MADHYAMAKA — the demolition
- **WHAT:** Rebirth with no one who is reborn and no thing that travels.
  Everything — including nirvāṇa, including emptiness itself — is empty of
  inherent existence. What continues is designation-on-conditions, like
  "chariot" for assembled parts: useful word, no chariot-essence inside.
- **WHY:** Because the tetralemma leaves no alternative. Does the Tathāgata
  exist after death? Not exist? Both? Neither? Every option assumes a findable
  Tathāgata, and analysis finds none — not in the aggregates, not apart from
  them, not as their owner. The question dissolves; dependent arising remains.
- **HOW:** Conventionally, causes produce results (karma works, births happen,
  the film you're watching exists). Ultimately, nothing stands alone long
  enough to be pinned. The two truths aren't two worlds — they're two ways of
  talking, and liberation is stopping the demand that one of them be the real one.

## 7. THAI FOREST (extracted from Thanissaro, The Truth of Rebirth — verbatim-sourced)
- WHAT: Standard Theravāda mechanics (paṭisandhi, gandhabba) run by monks
  whose teachers checked past lives by direct knowledge. Rebirth here is not
  doctrine to defend but a wager to take: "you're making a wager as to the
  consequences of your choice" — even "I don't know" is a wager that it
  won't matter.
- WHY: Because dependent co-arising is self-sustaining feedback loops —
  every factor carries an implicit "re-" from re-ignorance to re-death — and
  because the moral case collapses without multi-life accounting: "all
  misconduct suffers here-and-now" is flatly false (the king's killer wears
  garlands; the murderer walks to the drum).
- HOW: Upādāna = clinging AND fuel; the flame is craving-sustained (SN 44.9).
  Consciousness = seed, karma = field, craving = moisture (AN 3.77).
  Strategy: STARVE, don't destroy. Exit: passion for the four nutriments
  withdrawn → consciousness does not land → "like a sunbeam with no western
  wall" (SN 12:64). Arahant: "this is the last birth"; "the world would see
  him no more" (DN 1).
- Images (Thanissaro's own): tears for lost mothers greater than the four
  oceans (SN 15:3) · dust on a fingernail vs the great earth · 300 spears a
  day for a century in exchange for the Four Noble Truths — take the deal
  (SN 56:35).
