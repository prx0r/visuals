## [2:00] ONE — THE TRANSIT (Tibetan)

Dying is a power cut in stages. The body goes heavy — earth. The mouth dries — water. Heat retreats to the heart — fire. Breath rattles out — wind. Then the lights go on: Ground Luminosity, reality with the filters off. Miss it, and you wake up dead in the bardo — a mental body with your habits, for up to forty-nine days — until karma-winds blow you into a womb.

> DIR: CLIP Wallace on substrate consciousness (substrate = what travels).

Why does it keep going? Because the grasping didn't stop when the body did. The same momentum that kept you scrolling keeps you going. Recognize the Luminosity and you're done — this is tukdam, masters sitting undecayed for days. Don't, and the bardo runs its program: peaceful deities, wrathful deities, then the pull toward the birth matching your heaviest habits.

## [4:00] TWO — THE STOREHOUSE (Yogācāra)

No soul — but a store. Every intentional act drops a seed into the storehouse consciousness, perfuming it like sesame in oil. You are not a thing that continues. You are a harvest schedule.

> DIR: OVERLAY granary diagram — seeds in, sprouts out, perfume returns.

Why does anything carry over? Because nothing is wasted. Every perception you ever had was grown from a past seed, and every perception plants the next one. At death the senses shut and the store withdraws, carrying the seed account. A ripe seed sprouts as a new birth — which is why your next life rhymes with this one's habits.

## [5:45] THREE — THE RELINKING (Theravāda)

Death is seventeen thought-moments long. The life-continuum vibrates. Five weak flashes take the death-object — a past deed, its symbol, or a sign of the destination. Then death-consciousness falls. And in the very next moment, relinking-consciousness rises in a new existence, on the same object. No gap. No traveller. No luggage carousel.

> DIR: OVERLAY 17-moment strip — javanas marked, cuti/patisandhi adjacent.

Why that birth? Because the last flashes registered a direction. Karma isn't a judge handing sentences — it's the momentum of the final mental posture. Fall asleep in a chair facing a door, wake up walking through it. Conception needs three things at once: parents, season, and the being-to-be-reborn present. Miss any one — no birth.

## [7:30] FOUR — THE VORTEX (Early Buddhism)

Nothing travels because nothing is a thing. What you call "me" is a whirlpool between consciousness and name-and-form — two bundles of reeds holding each other up. Pull one and the other falls.

> DIR: CLIP Upton — reincarnation as framework, not dogma.

Why rebirth, then? Because the current never stopped. Ignorance and craving keep bending the stream back on itself, and a bent stream whirls. The Buddha's fire sermon IS the mechanism: fire burns dependent on fuel — grasping IS fuel, one word. Remove fuel, no fire. And it is absurd to ask which direction it went.

## [9:15] FIVE — THE RESCUE (Pure Land)

You can't do it yourself. This is the age of degenerate dharma and your practice will fail. So Amida did it for you. His vows built Sukhāvatī, and the eighteenth promises birth there to anyone who calls his Name in trust. At death he arrives with a retinue holding your lotus platform. You climb on. You wake up in the Pure Land, in one of nine grades.

> DIR: OVERLAY nine-grades ladder card.

Why does grace work? Because your account is deep red and his is infinite — and pride is the only thing that can refuse the transfer. Entrust, say the Name, vow. In the Land the lotus opens — fast for the sincere, slow semesters in the bud for the doubtful. Graduation means returning to save everyone else.

## [10:45] SIX — THE DEMOLITION (Madhyamaka)

Rebirth with no one reborn and nothing traveling. Everything — including nirvāṇa, including emptiness itself — is empty of inherent existence. What continues is designation-on-conditions, like "chariot" for assembled parts. Useful word. No chariot-essence inside.

> DIR: OVERLAY chariot-parts diagram.

The tetralemma leaves no alternative. Does the Tathāgata exist after death? Not exist? Both? Neither? Every option assumes a findable Tathāgata, and analysis finds none. Conventionally it all works — karma, births, this film. Ultimately nothing stands alone long enough to be pinned.

## [11:45] THE TABLE

Six theories. One shared spine: no soul travels. Karma is momentum. Consciousness leads. Test it yourself. They differ only on the carrier — continuum, store, winds, vow, or no carrier at all.
