## The six anchors

1. **Theravāda — MN 38 Mahātaṇhāsaṅkhaya.** Sati's error ("this same
   consciousness transmigrates") corrected; conception = three things
   (parents + season + gandhabba present); full 12-link DO arising/cessation.
   Mechanics companion: Abhidhammatthasaṅgaha §§11–13 (cuti → paṭisandhi,
   5 javanas, gati-nimitta; access: wisdomlib mirror).
2. **Early Buddhism (Ñāṇavīra) — MN 72 Aggivacchagotta + SN 22.85 Yamaka +
   SN 22.86 Anurādha.** Fire gone out (already our fire-simile film);
   Yamaka: annihilationism dismantled aggregate by aggregate ("can't pin down
   the Tathagata even in this life"); Anurādha: same via Sāriputta parallel.
   Logic root: SN 12.15 Kaccāyanagotta (existence/non-existence extremes →
   middle = dependent arising; full annotated text via dharmafarer).
3. **Yogācāra — Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra** (ālaya + tathāgatagarbha identity) with
   Samdhinirmocana as backup. Treatise layer: Waldron Ālaya Treatise paper
   (feedback loop, in hand via search). NOTE: verify passage refs at draft time.
4. **Tibetan — Bardo Thödol** (Karma Lingpa terma; root: Guhyagarbha Tantra).
   Not a sutta — say so on screen. Sutra-side support: none claimed; the
   authority chain is terma + Abhidharma antarabhava position.
5. **Pure Land — Larger Sukhāvatīvyūha** (Dharmākara's 48 vows, esp. 18th) +
   **Contemplation Sūtra** (nine grades, the deathbed welcome scenes).
   Both in `library/pureland/three-pure-land-sutras.pdf`. Commentary layer:
   Vasubandhu's Treatise on Rebirth + Tanluan (Easy vs Difficult Path).
6. **Madhyamaka — MMK** (Nāgārjuna; not a sutta) anchored to SN 12.15
   Kaccāyanagotta (shared with #2 — the film states this once: one sutta,
   two inheritors).
