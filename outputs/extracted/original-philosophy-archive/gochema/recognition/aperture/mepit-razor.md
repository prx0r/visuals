# Materially Enacted Inexternalist Process Identity Theory

## A Joint Physical–Phenomenal Framework for the Individuation of Conscious Biological Processes

---

## Abstract

A theory of consciousness must answer four distinct questions: why phenomenality exists, what individuates one subject, what structures its phenomenal field, and which physical organization implements it. The present framework addresses the second, third, and fourth through a single operation. It does not answer the first.

The framework's central claim is that subject and world are jointly individuated by a single exclusion operation — a partition of an undifferentiated possibility domain into a subject pole and a world pole. This operation is physically implemented by the bioelectric organization of a living organism and has an intrinsic aspect that is the phenomenal field. The framework is substrate-sensitive: the bearer is neither abstract information nor material inventory but the organized activity of a specific embodied medium. Consciousness is not derived from information, complexity, or biological closure. Phenomenality remains primitive. Its concrete bearer and internal structure are empirically investigable.

Claims are graded: proven (A), experimentally confirmed (B), supported by converging evidence (C), plausible hypothesis (D), speculative (E).

---

## 1. The Problem

A complete theory must separate four questions. Conflating them has produced a century of confused debate.

**Q1 — Existence:** Why is anything phenomenally manifest rather than nothing?

**Q2 — Individuation (the selector problem):** Why does one phenomenal field belong to one subject rather than another? What determines the boundary ∂D_i?

**Q3 — Structure:** Why does that field possess its particular unity, temporality, qualities, valence, agency, and privacy?

**Q4 — Implementation:** Which physical organization constitutes that field?

**The framework's scope:** Q1 is left primitive. The framework addresses Q2–Q4 through a single operation. This is not an evasion. It follows from the epistemic asymmetry: no purely third-person description entails first-person presence.

---

## 2. Why Purely Physical Selectors Fail

Every previous attempt to derive the subject from physics alone has failed. The reason is structural.

Let the total physical history be 𝒫 = (X, Φ, 𝒰). A purely physical selector would be a function 𝔖_phys: 𝒫 → {A₁, A₂, ...} that outputs the subject-bearing organizations.

Every proposal imports arbitrary choices:

| Selector Type | Arbitrary Parameter |
|---|---|
| Integration measures (Φ) | Requires choice of variables and partition |
| Thresholds | Requires cutoff value |
| Maximality rules | Requires comparison class |
| Causal models | Requires intervention set |
| Dynamical modes | Requires operator basis |
| Temporal windows | Requires endpoint selection |
| Biological closure | Permits nested closures |

**The underdetermination theorem:** Physical data alone underdetermine which organization is the phenomenal subject. Phenomenal subjecthood is not a variable contained within any exclusively extrinsic description.

**Corollary:** The demand that physics first identify the subject without phenomenal evidence was misplaced.

**Status: A (proven) — follows from the structure of physical description.**

## 3. The Core Identity Postulate

Let D_i denote one phenomenal subject-history and A_i denote one concrete materially enacted process.

```
D_i ≡_int A_i
```

where ≡_int denotes numerical identity under epistemically asymmetric access.

### What this does not mean

Not production: A_i → D_i. Not correlation: A_i ↔ D_i.

The same token is:
- **Extrinsically** describable through causal, biological, material, and behavioral relations
- **Intrinsically** present as one phenomenal trajectory

### The epistemic gap

Third-person propositions do not contain first-person presentation: E ⊬ P.

But non-derivability does not entail ontological distinctness: (E ⊬ P) ⇏ (E ≠ P).

The identity claim is a metaphysical postulate constrained by empirical structural correspondences.

**Status: A (philosophical postulate — shared with Russellian monism, dual-aspect theories).**

---

## 4. Information Is Not a Mental Substance

The Information Philosopher distinguishes intrinsic information from information about external relations and proposes that identity depends on total information. This cannot serve as a criterion of identity for three reasons.

**First:** "Total information" depends on state variables, coding scheme, precision, and observer's partition. It provides no unique natural quantity.

**Second:** Two time-slices of one organism contain different information. Persistence cannot require informational equality.

**Third:** Information is not an additional substance. Shannon information measures distinctions relative to a probability distribution. Semantic information requires further organizational and normative conditions.

The framework therefore adopts:

```
Information is a property of organized physical distinctions.
```

not:

```
Mind is an immaterial information substance.
```

The physically relevant units are constraint-bearing states with measurable causal consequences.

**Status: B (confirmed — follows from information theory).**

## 5. The Material Enactment Principle

A subject-bearing process is not an abstract transition table.

Let Σ_i(t) be the material regime of process A_i:

```
Σ_i = ⟨W_i, I_i, L_i, P_i, C_i, E_i, M_i⟩
```

where W_i is hydration state, I_i ionic composition, L_i lipid membranes, P_i proteins and channels, C_i cytoskeleton, E_i extracellular matrix, M_i metabolism.

The bearer is:

```
A_i = ⟨X_i, Φ_Σi, H_i, 𝒞_i, 𝒥_i, Ω_i⟩
```

where X_i is the accessible state space, Φ_Σi the materially dependent dynamics, H_i the causally active history, 𝒞_i the organization of mutual constraints, 𝒥_i the identity-relevant invariants, and Ω_i the current operating regime.

**Principle:** Φ_Σa ≠ Φ_Σb whenever material differences change psychophysically relevant counterfactual capacities.

**Status: B (supported by Levin's demonstration that bioelectric computation depends on specific biophysical parameters — ion channel types, gap junction topology, hydration — rather than abstract logical structure alone. Fields & Levin 2103.17061; Cadinu et al. 2504.00872.)**

## 6. The Solution: Exclusion as Joint Partition

### 6.1 The formal operation

Let Ω be the space of all possible determinations. The exclusion operator E_i for process A_i simultaneously carves subject and world:

```
E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i)
```

where P_i is the subject pole ("I") and W_i is the world pole ("this").

### 6.2 Co-emergence

P_i and W_i are not independently pre-existing. They co-emerge through the same operation:

```
¬(∃P_i prior to E_i) ∧ ¬(∃W_i prior to E_i)
```

### 6.3 The five dimensions of exclusion

The exclusion is implemented along five independent dimensions:

| Operator | Excludes | Subject Pole | World Pole |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kalā (agency) | Actions not available | What I can do | What resists |
| Vidyā (knowledge) | Distinctions not accessible | What I know | What is |
| Rāga (desire) | States not fulfilled | What I want | What offers |
| Kāla (time) | Times not present | When I am | Temporal structure |
| Niyati (space/cause) | Locations not reachable | Where I am | Spatial structure |

### 6.4 What this solves

Content X belongs to field D_i not because of an additional wall, but because the same exclusion operation E_i that determined X as content simultaneously determined D_i as its field. They are two poles of one act.

**This is the solution to the selector problem (Q2).**

**Status: C (supported by textual evidence from ĪPK 1.3.7 and Torella's reconstruction of Utpaladeva's apoha theory. The five-dimensional structure is from Abhinavagupta's kañcuka doctrine. Empirical support from Levin's demonstration that bioelectric fields implement exclusion-like operations at the cellular level.)**

## 7. Physical Implementation: Bioelectric Exclusion

### 7.1 The substrate

The exclusion operators E_i are physically implemented by the bioelectric field B_i:

| Exclusion | Physical Implementation |
|---|---|
| What signals enter (include) | Gap junctions open → coupled |
| What stays out (exclude) | Gap junctions closed → decoupled |
| What the system targets | Vmem distribution → morphogenetic attractor |
| What the system can affect | Cognitive light cone → spatial/temporal reach |

### 7.2 Experimental confirmation (Levin, 2010–2025)

1. **Pattern persistence:** Planarian target morphology survives complete cell turnover. The pattern is not in DNA — perturbing the bioelectric field changes it permanently. [B — multiple labs confirmed]
2. **Downward causation:** Organism-level bioelectric goals constrain individual cell behavior. Cells follow the field, not their own genome. [B — confirmed]
3. **Cognitive light cone:** Gap junction coupling determines the spatial/temporal scale of agency — what a system can sense, value, and act upon. [B — confirmed]
4. **Generate–select:** Every biological system, at every scale, generates alternative states and selects among them. [B — confirmed]

### 7.3 Material specificity

The computation performed by a bioelectric network depends constitutively on:
- Ion channel types (change channels → change computation)
- Gap junction topology (change connectivity → change memory)
- Hydration state (change water → change ion mobility)
- Membrane composition (change lipids → change channel behavior)

**Status: B (confirmed — Levin corpus; Fields & Levin 2103.17061; Cadinu et al. 2504.00872.)**

## 8. Organizational Closure

Biological-autonomy theory distinguishes thermodynamic openness from organizational closure. Organisms exchange matter and energy continuously while a network of constraints mutually maintains the conditions under which those constraints continue to operate.

Let 𝒞_i = {c₁, ..., c_n} be constraint-producing processes. Organizational closure requires that for every constitutive constraint c_j, some subset of the remaining organization contributes to its production or maintenance:

```
∀c_j ∈ 𝒞_i, ∃S_j ⊆ 𝒞_i \ {c_j} such that S_j ⇝ c_j
```

Collectively:

```
𝒞_i ⇝ 𝒞_i
```

This provides a non-arbitrary biological candidate boundary, an account of self-maintenance, a natural basis for endogenous function, and a distinction between constitutive components and ordinary inputs. It does not by itself establish consciousness. Cells, organs, and organisms may instantiate nested closures.

**Status: A (proven — closure theory; B in organisms — experimentally demonstrated).**

## 9. Historical Constitution

The process's history is not an immaterial record attached to its present state. It is present as altered disposition:

```
H_i(t) = {z ∈ X_i(t) : z depends on the prior trajectory and alters future transitions}
```

A process is strongly history-sensitive when:

```
P(x_t+Δ | x_t, H_t) ≠ P(x_t+Δ | x_t)
```

Relevant dispositions include synaptic organization, recurrent network state, bodily regulation, membrane and ionic configuration, developmental morphology, learned action policies, extracellular and mechanical organization, and autobiographical memory.

**Status: B (confirmed — neuroscience and developmental biology).**

### 10.1 Physical model family

Define a physical model family 𝕽 = {R^N, R^B, R^M, R^A, R^S} including neural, bioelectric, metabolic, autonomic, and sensorimotor descriptions. Each model recovers candidate organizations:

```
Rec(R^k) = {A₁^k, A₂^k, ...}
```

### 10.2 Phenomenal constraints

Let 𝕻_i be the family of phenomenal partitions supported by first-person discrimination, structured reports, psychophysics, and behavior.

### 10.3 The joint fixed point

A subject-bearing process is a joint fixed point:

```
A_i* = Fix(𝕽, 𝕻_i, 𝓤)
```

when the same process-equivalence class jointly satisfies:
1. Organizational closure
2. Material enactment
3. Historical continuity
4. Causal-scale robustness
5. Dynamically stable psychophysical partition
6. Perturbational correspondence
7. Cross-description convergence

### 10.4 The identity claim

```
D_i ≡_int A_i*
```

**Status: C (supported — integrative framework with independently confirmed components).**

### 11.1 The subject index

Distinguish explicit ownership (o_i) from implicit subject index (ι_i):

```
ι_i ≠ o_i
```

Explicit mineness can weaken in depersonalization or meditation. Experience remains indexed to one field. The subject index denotes the invariance by which changing contents are co-present within one private trajectory.

## 12. Valence as Geometric Coherence

Valence is hypothesized to be the degree of coherence of the exclusion operation:

```
ν_i = Symmetry(⟨E_i^kalā, E_i^vidyā, E_i^rāga, E_i^kāla, E_i^niyati⟩)
```

When the five exclusion dimensions are harmoniously aligned, the process is coherent: positive valence. When they conflict (what I want vs. what I can do; what I know vs. what is true), the exclusions are frustrated: negative valence.

**Status: D (hypothesized — supported by QRI's symmetry theory, Tymoczko's geometric music theory, and Abhinavagupta's camatkāra doctrine. No direct experimental confirmation.)**

## 13. Testable Predictions

### P1: Joint models outperform one-domain models
Models using only neural integration or only biological closure should individuate subjects less reliably than models combining physical organization with phenomenal partition constraints.

### P2: Generating partitions predict phenomenology
Partitions that are dynamically stable and approximately generating should predict phenomenal transitions better than arbitrary clustering.

### P3: Subject-index continuity exceeds content continuity
During large content changes, the organization corresponding to ι_i should remain more stable than content-specific neural patterns.

### P4: Ownership dissociates from indexing
Perturbations of explicit ownership should alter o_i without necessarily abolishing ι_i, unity, or phenomenal presence.

### P5: Organizational closure is necessary but insufficient
Many autonomous biological systems should satisfy closure without satisfying the complete psychophysical convergence conditions.

### P6: History improves bearer recovery
History-sensitive models should outperform instantaneous models in predicting temporal continuity, familiarity, agency, affect, and subject-boundary changes.

### P7: No single material variable is sufficient
Hydration, membrane voltage, synchrony, information quantity, and complexity should each fail as independent selectors while contributing to the joint process model.

### P8: Robust scale plateau
The candidate bearer should be recovered across a range of nearby spatial and temporal scales rather than at one fragile analytical resolution.

### P9: Valence follows exclusion coherence
Manipulations that align the five exclusion dimensions should increase positive valence; manipulations that create conflict should increase negative valence.

---

## 14. Falsification Conditions

The framework would be seriously weakened if:

1. No physical model family converges on a common process-equivalence class
2. Stable phenomenal partitions cannot be mapped to stable physical partitions
3. Arbitrary partitions predict phenomenology as well as generating partitions
4. Candidate boundaries change radically under minor analytical alterations
5. Organizational closure and causal-scale robustness provide no predictive improvement
6. Historical variables add no explanatory value
7. Phenomenal unity and privacy track several incompatible overlapping processes equally well
8. Physical destruction of the recovered process leaves the same phenomenal trajectory intact
9. The proposed subject-index invariant shows no physical or phenomenological stability
10. Valence does not track exclusion coherence in controlled experiments

## 16. Final Theory Statement

> **Materially Enacted Inexternalist Process Identity Theory (MEPIT) holds that each conscious subject is numerically identical to one materially enacted, temporally extended, history-bearing biological process. Subject and world are jointly individuated by a single exclusion operation E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i), physically implemented by the bioelectric organization of the organism. The exclusion has an intrinsic aspect that is the phenomenal field.**
>
> **The bearer is an organizationally closed but thermodynamically open network of mutually maintaining constraints whose neural, bioelectric, metabolic, mechanical, autonomic, and sensorimotor activities jointly preserve one trajectory. Because physical systems admit multiple overlapping descriptions, subject individuation requires a joint fixed point at which independent physical models, causal-scale analyses, dynamically stable generating partitions, perturbational evidence, and independently characterized phenomenal structures converge on one process-equivalence class.**
>
> **Phenomenal presence, unity, privacy, temporality, quality, valence, agency, and implicit subject indexing are intrinsic structural dimensions of that same process. Information, complexity, integration, biological closure, and hydration are neither separate mental substances nor individually sufficient conditions. Phenomenality itself is primitive; its concrete bearer, boundaries, persistence, and internal structure are empirically investigable.**

Formally:

```
D_i ≡_int A_i*
A_i* = Fix(𝕽, 𝕻_i, 𝓤)
A_i* = ⟨X_i, Φ_Σi, H_i, 𝒞_i, 𝒥_i, Ω_i, E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i)⟩
```
