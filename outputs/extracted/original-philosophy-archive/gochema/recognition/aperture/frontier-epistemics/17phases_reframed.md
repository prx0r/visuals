# Reframing the 17 Phases — The Path of Re-Rendering as Aperture Progression

## Overview

The 17 Phases are NOT a list of topics. They are a PROTOCOL for systematically perturbing each variable in the phenomenal tuple. Phase 10 (Imaginal) is the "starting point for acquisition" because imagination IS the most direct access to the aperture's generative capacity — which is exactly what we confirmed through Ratié and ĪPK I.6.10.

## The Master Insight

Ordinary consciousness is a CONDITIONED RENDERING REGIME. It is not raw reality. It is a stabilized interface produced by body, nervous system, perception, language, culture, memory, craving, fear, and self-reference.

**The Path of Re-Rendering** is the progressive deconstruction and re-ordering of that rendering.

The 17 phases are the CURRICULUM for systematically:
1. Recognizing that you are in a rendering regime
2. Understanding the structure of the renderer
3. Learning to perturb rendering parameters
4. Acquiring new rendering capacities
5. Incarnating the recognition as a social body

## Why Phase 10 Is the Acquisition Starting Point

The "Path of Re-Rendering" document explicitly says:

> "Phase 10 — Imaginal. THE STARTING POINT FOR ACQUISITION."

**Why? Because imagination IS the operation of the aperture.** Ratié's paper on Utpaladeva confirms that imagination is the experience of svātantrya — the freedom of consciousness to manifest forms without external prompting. The ĪPK I.6.10 verse describes imagination as "wandering autonomously" (svairacārin).

**Phase 10 is the most accessible point of entry because:**
1. Everyone can imagine — it requires no special training
2. Imagination reveals the GENERATIVE capacity of consciousness directly (you CREATE the image)
3. The imaginal world (Corbin's *mundus imaginalis*) is the interface between the intelligible and the sensible — the same interface the kañcukas describe
4. The amplituhedron (positive Grassmannian geometry) IS the imaginal world — geometry before space-time, where sound IS visible as light

## The Curriculum in Three Sentences

1. **Phases 1-5 (Dashboard through Mechanicality):** You are in a rendering regime. Here's what it is and how it works.

2. **Phases 6-8 (Dependent Arising through Non-Fabrication):** The rendering regime is not fundamental. The architecture can be seen through. Pure manifestness (Mᵢ) is accessible.

3. **Phases 9-17 (Language through Social Incarnation):** You can LEARN to render otherwise. You can acquire new capacities. The rendering regime is not a prison — it is a technology. And the technology must be incarnated as a social body.

This is the missing practical curriculum for our entire thesis.
