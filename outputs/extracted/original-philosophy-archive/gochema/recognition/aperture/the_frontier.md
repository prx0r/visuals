### Corrected Status of Overstated Claims

**"The TĀ is a systematic elaboration of the ĪPK triad"** — [R, not T]. The correspondence jñāna→prakāśa, smṛti→vimarśa/spanda, apohana→kañcukas is insightful but not exact. Safer: "The Tantrāloka develops a broader architecture containing functions analogous to manifestation, continuity, and determination."

**"Vimarśa = camatkāra"** — [T for specific passages, not lexical identity]. ĪPK 1.5.11 Vṛtti renders vimarśa as camatkāra, but possible relations include: vimarśa ⇒ camatkāra (entailment), camatkāra = affective mode of vimarśa (modulation), or vimarśa and camatkāra = two descriptions of reflexive manifestation (co-extensive). The exact Sanskrit and commentary context must decide.

**"One body = one phenomenal field"** — [T for the boundary condition, not the phenomenal identity]. TĀ 16.242 gives: cross-body anusandhāna is unavailable. It does not prove one body = one phenomenal token. Defensible: one body is a candidate maximal domain of connective synthesis.

**"Imagination = svātantrya confirmed"** — [T for privileged evidence, not identity]. Ratié confirms imagination is used as a privileged manifestation of svātantrya. Not identical to proving Iᵢ = svātantrya. Correct: imagination provides the clearest ordinary evidence for finite creative freedom in Pratyabhijñā.

## Solved: The Cross-Cultural Convergences

### The Neoplatonic Participation Model [C — confirmed]

Proclus's *Elements of Theology* arrives at the same structural solution as the Śaiva reflection model: many real centres participate in one prior causal order without becoming numerically identical with it or each other. The Proclean correction: "ground unity need not entail subject identity." This is an independent tradition converging on the same architecture.

**Critical difference:** Proclus places the One beyond consciousness. Pratyabhijñā identifies it with consciousness. The debate between them is the same as our debate between process inexternalism and full Pratyabhijñā.

### The Iamblichean Theurgy Model [C — confirmed]

Iamblichus provides the embodied/ritual dimension that the Śaiva upāya system also provides. The key: "Philosophy is united with the art of sacred things since this art is concerned with the purification of the luminous body, but if you separate philosophical thinking from this art, you will find that it no longer has the same power." This IS the Vijñānabhairava's position: practice is necessary because the whole embodied subject must be transformed.

### The Imaginal Convergence [C — structural, not ontological]

Utpaladeva (svātantrya as imagination), Suhrawardi (ʻālam al-mithāl), Ibn Arabi (khayāl/barzakh), and Corbin (mundus imaginalis) converge on the inadequacy of reducing imagination to unreal fantasy. The true common denominator: images can possess reality, efficacy, and structure without being ordinary physical objects.

This leaves open whether the imaginal is: consciousness's self-manifestation, a separate ontological realm, a mediating barzakh, a relational mode, or a phenomenological construction. Structural convergence ⇏ one ontology.

## The Three Master Gaps

The five gaps compress into three.

### Gap 1 — The Individuation Gap

Content exclusion ⇏ phenomenal exclusion.

```
E_contentᵢ ⇏ E_subject
```

ĪPK 1.3.7 confirms that exclusion (apohana) determines CONTENT — this rather than that. TĀ 16.242 gives the boundary condition (anusandhāna stops at the body). Neither establishes that determining one object IS the same operation as determining which phenomenal field it belongs to.

### Gap 2 — The Co-Presence Gap

Functional integration ⇏ phenomenal co-presence.

```
Integration(x,y) ⇏ CoPresent(x,y)
```

Science can identify systems that are causally integrated, semantically autonomous, self-modelling, and globally coherent. It cannot establish that x and y are EXPERIENCED TOGETHER rather than merely processed together.

### Gap 3 — The Manifest-Value Gap

Objective significance ⇏ felt valence.

```
(Semᵢ, Vᵢ) ⇏ νᵢ
```

Kolchinsky & Wolpert establish that information can be causally necessary for persistence. Panksepp establishes that primary-process affect exists. Neither establishes that these entail felt valence. The adaptation-rate model is a candidate but not confirmed.

### The freedom and imaginal gaps are extensions of these:

```
operational generation ⇏ absolute freedom
cross-cultural structural resemblance ⇏ one ontology
```

## The Three Surviving Frameworks

All three remain viable. The texts and science do not decide among them.

| Framework | Claim | Strength | Weakness |
|-----------|-------|----------|----------|
| **Process inexternalism** | Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ*. The closed aperture IS the phenomenal field intrinsically. | Explains everything without positing universal consciousness; compatible with science | Cannot derive why closure entails presence; it's a postulate |
| **Participatory inexternalism** | Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ* AND Dᵢ = πᵢ(M₀). A common manifest ground is locally actualized. | Explains cross-modal convergence, cross-cultural convergence, the reflection model | Needs independent evidence for M₀; Proclus-type challenge: ground ⇏ manifestness |
| **Full Pratyabhijñā** | Oᵢ* = Contractionᵢ(C∞). One universal consciousness freely contracts into local fields. | Explains everything from one principle | Cannot prove numerical identity of subjects; overcompresses levels (Proclus's challenge) |

## The Three Frameworks, Clarified

All three remain viable. The texts and science do not decide among them.

### Process Inexternalism

```
Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ*
```

Parsimonious and scientifically compatible. Its weakness is not merely that identity is postulated (every identity theory needs bridge principles), but that it lacks an independent criterion explaining why THIS organization, rather than another, is the intrinsic bearer.

### Participatory Inexternalism

```
Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ*  AND  Dᵢ = πᵢ(M₀)
```

Advantage: distinguishes local token individuality from common manifest ground. Weakness: M₀ may simply redescribe manifestness rather than explain it.

### Full Pratyabhijñā

```
Oᵢ* = Contractionᵢ(C∞)
```

Advantage: maximal explanatory unity — manifestation, agency, value, plurality, and recognition all derive from one principle. Weakness: the universalization step — each stream is self-manifest ⇏ all streams are numerically one subject. That remains the sharpest challenge from Buddhism, Proclus, and process inexternalism.

## The Actual Frontier

The unified problem:

```
MaximalClosure(memory, value, counterfactuals, source attribution,
               reciprocal control) ⟷? (co-presence, privacy, felt significance)
```

This is the problem behind: consciousness, self-boundary, valence, agency, memory, and time.

**The most important strategic conclusion:**

> The textual research has largely completed the functional anatomy. Further progress now depends less on finding additional analogies and more on testing whether maximal embodied closure predicts phenomenal unity, ownership, and valence better than rival boundary criteria.
