## The Three Exclusions (Critical Distinction)

**Content exclusion (E_contentᵢ):** Ωᵢ → xᵢ. Determines one content as this rather than that.
**Textually supported by ĪPK 1.3.7.**

**World exclusion (E_worldᵢ):** Ω → Wᵢ. Determines which distinctions constitute the participant's world.
**Plausible extension through the kañcukas.**

**Subject exclusion (E_subject):** Ω → {D₁, D₂, ..., Dₙ}. Determines why one field is exclusive of another.
**NOT established by any text. This is the open problem.**

## The Four Corrected Wins (Post-Torella Audit)

### Win 1 — ĪPK 1.3.7: Exclusion is a Fundamental Power

**The verse:** "syād ekaś cid-vapur jñāna-smṛty-apohana-śaktimān"
**Translation:** "There would be one reality whose nature is consciousness, possessing the powers of cognition, memory, and exclusion."

**Confirmed:** Exclusion (apohana) is a constitutive power of consciousness alongside knowledge and memory. This supports E_contentᵢ.

**NOT confirmed:** That E_contentᵢ = E_subject. The verse does not prove that content exclusion causes phenomenal partition. This gap remains open.

### Win 2 — Reflection Model (Corrected Reading)

**What the passage actually says:** The behavior of another subject within MY stream is the reflection (pratibimba). The other conscious stream is INFERRED as the original (bimba) that occasions it.

**Confirmed:** Other subjects appear in my consciousness as reflections. This solves ontological compatibility: many participants, one source.

**NOT confirmed:** That phenomenal privacy (∂ᵢ) is explained. The mirror analogy secures unity without division but does NOT derive why πᵢ ≠ πⱼ.

### Win 3 — Vimarśa as Active Reflexive Function

**Confirmed:** Vimarśa is a function, not a substance. Consciousness has two inseparable functions: manifestation (prakāśa) and reflexive self-articulation (vimarśa).

**NOT confirmed:** That consciousness IS nothing but the act of self-grasping. Timalsina explicitly says vimarśa is a FUNCTION — a function requires something that has the function.

### Win 4 — Imagination = Svātantrya

**Confirmed:** ĪPK I.6.10 identifies imagination as autonomous (svairacārin) — self-prompted, independent of external prompting.

**NOT confirmed:** That this establishes absolute freedom or that imagination = apoha. Innovation is possible within constraint; the constraint does not disappear.

## What Remains Unresolved

### The Four Gaps

1. **Endogenous generation ⇏ absolute freedom** — imagination shows limited spontaneity, not omnipotence
2. **Content exclusion ⇏ phenomenal exclusion** — apoha determines objects, not subject boundaries
3. **Cross-modal integration ⇏ primordial field unity** — trained binding does not prove an undifferentiated baseline
4. **Functional reflexivity ⇏ intrinsic self-presence** — a system can monitor itself without phenomenal self-affection
