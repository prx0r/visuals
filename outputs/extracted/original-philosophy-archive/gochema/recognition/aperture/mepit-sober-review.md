### Survives: Rasa = Geometry = Valence (as hypothesis)

**Current state (RASAANALYSIS):** "Consciousness IS aesthetic relish... The universe is condensed rasa."

**Sober version:**
> Three independently developed frameworks (Tantrāloka's camatkāra doctrine,
> QRI's symmetry theory of valence, Tymoczko's geometric music theory) converge
> on a structural hypothesis: the felt quality of an experience corresponds to
> the geometric structure of its state space. This convergence is striking but
> does not constitute proof. The hypothesis generates testable predictions:
> the Chordonomicon dataset (666k songs) can test whether emotional quality
> maps to geometric distance in a 6D space.

**What to cut:**
- "The universe is condensed rasa" → remove, not testable
- "Geometry IS feeling" (identity) → replace with "Geometry CORRELATES WITH feeling" (empirical)
- "Consciousness = aesthetic relish" → keep as a philosophical claim from the Tantrāloka

**Status: TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS — requires Chordonomicon analysis**

## Part 3: What to Include from the Channeled Material

**Nothing directly.** The channeled material (Ra, Seth, Cassiopaeans) is not
admissible as evidence in a scientific framework.

**But the STRUCTURAL CONVERGENCES are worth noting:**

> The Ra material describes an octave of seven densities. Independently,
> Fuller's synergetics describes an octave of geometric transformations,
> and MEPIT describes aperture regimes of increasing information
> integration. The structural convergence does not validate the Ra
> material's truth claims, but it suggests that the density framework
> captures a real pattern in how information-processing capacity scales.

This is the correct tone — note the convergence without claiming validation.

## The Verdict

The rasa/frontier work is a **goldmine of leads** filtered through the
wrong presentation style. The insights are real — apoha as partition,
rasa as geometry, the three-body decomposition, the three master gaps —
but they need to be **rebuilt from the ground up** in the style of
subtle-body-formal-position.md:

1. Start from first principles or proven results
2. Present proof before evidence
3. Grade every claim
4. State open gaps explicitly
5. Never say "IS" when "MAY CORRESPOND TO" will do
6. End with what remains unproven

The Sanskritree/truth docs found the ore. The density project refines it
into steel. Don't collapse the two processes.
