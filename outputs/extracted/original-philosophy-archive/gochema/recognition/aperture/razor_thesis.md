# Aperture Process Identity Theory — Razor Thesis

## I. Core Ontology

A conscious subject is one concrete, maximally closed, temporally persistent, embodied process. That process is externally describable as causal and functional organization and intrinsically exists as one private phenomenal field.

```
Dᵢ ≡_int Oᵢ*
```

This is a token identity hypothesis, not a derivation. It cannot be proven from exclusively extrinsic premises because the intrinsic side is absent from those premises. This is the epistemic asymmetry defense: failure of derivation does not entail ontological separation.

### The aperture

```
Oᵢ* = MaxClosure(Mᵢ, Vᵢ, Iᵢ, Eᵢ, Cᵢ, Sᵢ, Aᵢ, Bᵢ, Γᵢ)
```

where:
- Mᵢ: memory and historical retention
- Vᵢ: endogenous valuation
- Iᵢ: counterfactual generation
- Eᵢ: determinate exclusion
- Cᵢ: connective synthesis
- Sᵢ: source attribution and self-modelling
- Aᵢ: reciprocal agency
- Bᵢ: embodied regulation
- Γᵢ: developmental trajectory

### The phenomenal field

```
Dᵢ = ⟨(Mᵢ,ρᵢ), ∂ᵢ, Uᵢ, τᵢ, Gᵢ, νᵢ, αᵢ⟩
```

where:
- (Mᵢ,ρᵢ): conjoined manifest presence and basal self-affection — appearing that is intrinsically self-manifesting. Mᵢ without ρᵢ is jaḍa (insentient mirroring) per the source tradition. The claim that ρᵢ = camatkāra (aesthetic relish) is the substantive horn of an unresolved fork, held on textual authority.
- ∂ᵢ: phenomenal boundary
- Uᵢ: phenomenal unity
- τᵢ: lived temporality
- Gᵢ: phenomenal geometry
- νᵢ: local valence
- αᵢ: experienced agency

### Individuation criterion

Subjecthood tracks the maximal domain of reciprocal closure across memory, value, counterfactual generation, determination, integration, agency, and embodiment. The aggregation rule is an open research question. Whether this criterion governs individuation or bare phenomenal existence is also open.

## IV. Rasa Thesis

The claim: consciousness is intrinsically self-affective rather than affectively neutral. This is the (Mᵢ,ρᵢ) pair — specifically the ρᵢ side.

### Textual grounding
- ĪPK 1.5.11 Vṛtti: vimarśa = camatkāra. The distinction between sentient and insentient IS the presence of camatkāra.
- PTv p.19: consciousness is "by its very nature essentially the aesthetic relish of consciousness."
- Ahaṅcamatkāra (TĀ Vol 10:23762): the aesthetic rapture of "I am" — selfhood IS wonder.
- TĀ Vol 5:4146: the universe is "condensed rasa" — physical reality is consciousness's aesthetic juice thickened into form.
- Biernacki: avicchinnacamatkāra compound — "the uninterrupted wonder of I-consciousness."

### What it adds
- ρᵢ cannot be reduced to νᵢ (local valence). Pain is a contracted mode of ρᵢ, not its absence. The Śaiva position: ρᵢ > 0 while νᵢ(t) < 0.
- The senses are not neutral channels but differentiated forms of wonder (ciccamatkāra).

### Limitation
The rasa thesis is a substantive metaphysical claim inherited from the textual tradition. It has not been derived from the identity thesis or from empirical evidence. It occupies the substantive horn of the deflationary/substantive fork, which is unresolved.

## V. Recognition Framework

Recognition is downstream of the core. It does not constitute consciousness or close any of the three gaps.

### Cascade

```
Core identity (Dᵢ ≡_int Oᵢ*)
  → aperture constitution (Oᵢ* = MaxClosure of 10 operations)
    → self-model generated within aperture (SMᵢ)
      → recognition as transformation of self-model (ℛᵢ: SMᵢ⁻ → SMᵢ^R)
        → embodied reorganization through subtle body interface
```

### Three strengths

| Level | Claim | Status |
|---|---|---|
| R₀: Process recognition | "I am not an independent inner controller; I am this embodied process" | Compatible with minimal APIT |
| R₁: Participatory recognition | "This aperture is a local actualization of a common manifest ground" | Metaphysically plausible, unproven |
| R₂: Śaiva recognition | "This aperture is universal consciousness determining itself as this finite standpoint" | Full Pratyabhijñā commitment |

### Behavioral invariants
Recognition, if genuine, should tend toward:
- Reduced self-exemption (eᵢ ↓)
- Reduced defensive rigidity
- Improved source discrimination
- Expanded care without assumed fusion
- Greater integration between insight and action

These distinguish recognition from ego inflation, which shows the opposite pattern.

### Subtle body
Defined as the control interface:

```
SBᵢ = Interface(Vᵢ, Iᵢ, Eᵢ, Bᵢ, SMᵢ, Πᵢ)
```

Recognition is fully integrated only when it propagates through this interface — altering bodily regulation, policy, and automatic response. This is the "insight must descend" claim. No literal substance descends. The new self-model propagates through the aperture's value, prediction, affect, and action systems.

## VI. Optional Metaphysics

The core identity thesis is compatible with three interpretations, none derivable from it:

| Interpretation | Claim | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Process inexternalism | Dᵢ ≡_int Oᵢ*, no further ground | Cannot derive presence from closure |
| Participatory inexternalism | Dᵢ = πᵢ(M₀) AND Dᵢ ≡_int Oᵢ* | No independent evidence for M₀ |
| Full Pratyabhijñā | Oᵢ* = Contractionᵢ(C∞) | Cannot prove numerical identity of subjects |

Proclus's correction holds: ground unity does not entail subject identity. The Śaiva claim that all subjects are numerically one consciousness is the strongest optional extension, not the minimal position.

## VII. The Three Gaps

| Gap | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Individuation | Content exclusion does not entail phenomenal exclusion (E_contentᵢ ⇏ E_subject) | Open — TĀ 16.242 gives the boundary condition but not the derivation |
| Co-presence | Functional integration does not entail phenomenal unity (Integration(x,y) ⇏ CoPresent(x,y)) | Open — connective synthesis proposed as identical, not causal |
| Manifest-value | Objective significance does not entail felt valence ((Semᵢ,Vᵢ) ⇏ νᵢ) | Open — valuation proposed as identical, not causal |

Each is a site for empirical testing. If the identity fails under perturbation, the framework fails.

## VIII. What Makes This Distinct

| Framework | What it covers | What it misses |
|---|---|---|
| Active inference | Functional unification of value, prediction, action, self-model | No account of phenomenal boundaries, privacy, or why the unified function has intrinsic character |
| IIT Phenomenal axioms + Φ | Integrated information as consciousness measure | Bridge-principle selection contested; no functional anatomy of what integration consists in |
| Enactivism | Sense-making, embodiment, autonomy | No account of manifest presence or phenomenal character |
| APIT (this framework) | Token identity + closure-based individuation + convergent functional anatomy | Aggregation rule unfinished; manifestness a postulate |

The distinct contribution: the claim that a unified functional organization (convergent across Śaiva and scientific taxonomies) constitutes one private field under two modes of access — not that it produces, correlates with, or realizes phenomenality, but that it IS phenomenality intrinsically.

## IX. Honest Open Problems

| Problem | Status |
|---|---|
| All three gaps | Open — sites for empirical testing |
| Closure aggregation | Open — unfinished |
| Manifestness | Open — postulate defended by epistemic asymmetry |
| Bridge-principle selection | Open — not shown narrower than single-scalar theories |
| Mᵢ/ρᵢ fork | Open — substantive horn (camatkāra) held by textual authority, not derivation |
| Recognition integration | Open — behavioral invariants proposed but not systematically tested |
| Existence/individuation | Closed — concrete fix owed in predictions |

The framework is a defensible research program — not a completed theory. Its core identity thesis is philosophically coherent. Its functional anatomy shows structural parallels across the Śaiva tradition and contemporary research programs at the level of analogous taxonomic positions — a testable convergence claim, not an established one. Its gaps are explicitly acknowledged and testable. Its recognition framework and rasa thesis are downstream contributions that add explanatory power without redefining the core.

What it does not have is a derivation of manifestness, an aggregation rule, or a principled solution to bridge-principle selection. Those are the frontiers.
