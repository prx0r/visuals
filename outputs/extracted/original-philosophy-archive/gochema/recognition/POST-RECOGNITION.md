*The second half of the magnum opus. Recognition (the event) is everyone's focus; the
re-rendering of perception that FOLLOWS recognition is our distinct contribution. This is
literally re-rendering: after the self re-cognizes, perception itself is re-structured.*

## THE THESIS

> **Recognition is not the endpoint — it is the threshold. The real work is the
> re-rendering of perception that follows.** After the self re-cognizes, perception,
> memory, agency, and the felt register are *transformed*. Everyone studies the event;
> we study the aftermath — the malleability of perception and its re-structuring.

**The name:** it is literally *re-rendering* — perception is a construction (a controlled
hallucination), and recognition triggers its re-rendering.

Three traditions, one structure:
| Tradition | The event | The aftermath |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|

## THE SCIENCE OF RE-RENDERING (the depth)

The aftermath is not mystical — it maps onto real, deep cognitive science:

- **QRI — neural annealing**: how the brain's state-space is annealed (re-configured)
  toward new attractors — the mechanism of re-rendering.
- **Neural plasticity / malleability**: perception is not fixed; it is continuously
  re-structured.
- **Psychedelics**: perturb the predictive model, exposing perception as construction and
  allowing re-rendering (the "controlled hallucination" becomes visible).
- **Seth — controlled hallucination**: perception IS a controlled hallucination — a
  prediction; recognition changes the prediction (re-renders the world).
- **Levin — the consciousness continuum**: consciousness/agency is graded across scales;
  re-rendering operates at every level.
- **Active inference (Friston)**: the self is a generative model; recognition updates the
  model → the world re-renders.

**The unifying claim:** recognition updates the predictive/generative model of the self, and
the world (perception) re-renders accordingly. This is the cognitive-science statement of
"after recognition, everything looks different."

## THE STRUCTURE OF PERCEPTION (Ñāṇavīra / Ñāṇananda)

The Theravada material gives the *structure* of perception that re-rendering operates on:
- **Ñāṇavīra** — perception is not passive; it is structured by the "clearing" (the
  subject's stance). The structure of experience changes as the stance changes.
- **Ñāṇananda** — perception is construction; the "I-making" and "my-making" that structure
  it can be seen through.
- The **tractatus** material (`tractatus-nanavira-abhinavagupta.md`) links this to
  Abhinavagupta — perception as the aperture's construction.

**The link to our framework:** the aperture model (manifest discrimination, determination,
memory, valence) IS the structure of perception that re-renders post-recognition.
