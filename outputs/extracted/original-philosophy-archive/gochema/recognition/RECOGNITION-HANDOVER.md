## 9. THE NEXT STEPS — THE WRITING ORDER (reading vs. generation)

**The reading/presentation order is C→B→A→F→E→D→G→H (rigor to speculation). BUT the
WRITING/generation order is different — the IPVV comes FIRST, not last.**

### Why the IPVV comes first (the insight-first principle)
The IPVV is our **untranslated, unexplored primary source**. Every essay's thesis is
currently a *hypothesis* about what the IPVV will confirm. If the IPVV **contradicts or
refines** any claim (recognition = the removal of a mis-knowledge; the σ-flip; the felt),
writing the essays first means writing on a possibly-wrong understanding — forcing a
rewrite. **The IPVV is the load-bearing primary source; its insights must be banked BEFORE
the essays that cite it.**

### The writing order (in phases)
```
PHASE 0 — THE IPVV (the foundation — bank the insights)
   the plain-English bridge on the essay-critical passages
   (the camatkāra/vimarśa/reflexion/recognition chunks)
   → harvest the FRESH INSIGHTS → adjust the thesis if needed

PHASE 1 — THE RAZOR (Essay C) — now grounded in the IPVV
PHASE 2 — THE BUDDHIST STREAM (Essay B)
PHASE 3 — THE GREEK DAIMON STREAM (Essay A)

PHASE 4 — THE DEBATE + THE FRONTIER (F, E)
PHASE 5 — THE SOLMS/VALENCE THESIS (D)
PHASE 6 — THE IPVV COMPLETION (G) + THE COMPARATIVE WEB (H)
```

### The steps in order
1. **The IPVV plain-English bridge** on the essay-critical chunks (the camatkāra/vimarśa/
   reflexion passages). Fast (the accuracy is banked in the T1); de-risks everything.
2. **Harvest the fresh IPVV insights** — adjust the thesis (recognition = the felt
   re-cognition; the removal of the parasite) if the primary source refines it.
3. **Draft the razor (Essay C)** — now grounded in what the IPVV actually says, not the
   hypothesis. Draw on the Tantrāloka/IPK passages.
4. **Draft the Buddhist stream (Essay B)** — Ñāṇavīra's tetrahedron + Ñāṇananda's vortex +
   the modernized feedback-loop argument.
5. **Draft the Greek daimon stream (Essay A)** — the mythic/participatory face.
6. **Map the Patala C1s to the post-recognition analysis** — the verified translations are
   the scholarly authority; wire them into the essays.
7. **Draft the Solms/Valence thesis (Essay D)** — the felt-is-primary + the valence-lineage,
   held as [C].
8. **The IPVV Vol 2 finish** (the last ~1,000 lines) + the Vol 3 translation (the
   completion).
