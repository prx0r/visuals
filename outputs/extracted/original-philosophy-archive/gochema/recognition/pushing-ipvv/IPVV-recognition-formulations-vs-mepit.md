*The recognition-avenue. Our recognition-formulation (and the mepit razor) holds: finite subjects
are NOT generated by a filter on universal consciousness (M∞→Li→Mi); they are INTERNALLY manifested
forms of the same conscious power. The IPVV's L2 gives several distinct formulations of recognition
(the foregrounding, the "I was never not", the lover-image). This session mines them and tests them
against our formulation. Method: force the connection.*

## ROUND 0 — our formulation (the guiding framework)

> "finite consciousness is not generated by a sufficiently complex nonconscious mechanism... Rather,
> finite subjects, bodies, cognition, and worlds are determinate manifestations of an already

## ROUND 1 — Formulation 1: recognition as FOREGROUNDING (V1E)

**The IPVV's formulation (V1E):**
> "The pramāṇa here is not establishing something new. It is *foregrounding* what is already present
> but hidden. The recognition (pratyabhijñā) does not bring the Lord into being; it brings the Lord,
> who was always near and always present, into the foreground of our own awareness... It is the
> turning of the attention — the 'see, see' — upon what was always before us."

**The connection to our formulation:** This is the σ-flip, made precise. The recognition does not
change the content (the Lord was always there); it changes the POSITIONING (σ) — from the Lord as "an
instrument lost among the things of the body and the world" (σ=Sv, the self as a thing among things)
to "the primary thing that we see" (σ=¬Sv, the self as the foreground). The foreground/background
shift IS the σ-flip: the same content, re-positioned.

> **PENETRATION 1:** V1E's foregrounding IS the σ-flip made precise. Recognition "does not bring the
> Lord into being; it brings the Lord, who was always near, into the foreground." This is the change
> in positioning (σ), not content — from the self as "an instrument lost among the things" (Sv) to
> "the primary thing we see" (¬Sv). **The foreground/background shift is exactly the σ-flip: the same
> content, re-positioned. Our AM0 formulation and the IPVV's V1E are the same move.**

## ROUND 2 — Formulation 2: the "I was never not" (V1D) — the eternal knower as the ground of recognition

**The IPVV's formulation (V1D):**
> "The manifestation of the self is not un-precedented (apūrva). It does not begin to shine at some
> moment; it was always shining. The being-ness of the self rests in its manifestation, and its being
> was never otherwise... So, in truth, there is no un-precedented manifestation at all."
> "The self — the param-essence knower — is un-cut by all three times: past, present, and future. It
> did not come into being, and it will not pass away."

**The connection to our formulation:** This grounds the "recognition adds nothing" in the eternity of
the self. Because the self "was never not," there is nothing for recognition to ADD — it can only
foreground what was always shining. This is the recognition-not-attainment thesis at its root: the
"un-precedented manifestation" (the new appearance) is only apparent; it rests on the eternal
always-manifested knower. Our formulation (recognition removes the false self-positioning, nothing
added) is here grounded in the eternity of the self.

> **PENETRATION 2:** V1D grounds "recognition adds nothing" in the eternity of the self. Because the
> self "was never not" (un-cut by all three times), there is nothing for recognition to add — it can
> only foreground what was always shining. The "un-precedented manifestation" is only apparent; it
> rests on the eternal always-manifested knower. **The recognition-not-attainment thesis is here
> grounded in the self's eternity: nothing is added because the self was never absent.**

## ROUND 3 — Formulation 3: the lover-image (V3P) — recognition as the turning of the near into the enjoyed

**The IPVV's formulation (V3P):**
> "The lover stands near, sought by the slender woman; but as long as he is not recognized, he cannot
> be enjoyed. So the world-Lord, always present, always near, is not experienced in his own splendor
> until the recognition arises. The world has him before it, and yet does not see him, until the
> single act of recognition turns the un-known nearness into the joy of union."

**The connection to our formulation:** This is the σ-flip in aesthetic form. The lover is NEAR
(present, never absent) — the content is there. What changes with recognition is the RELATION: from
un-known nearness to the joy of union. The "un-known nearness" is σ=Sv (the self-present-but-un-nown);
the "joy of union" is σ=¬Sv (the self-recognized). The recognition does not bring the lover (he was
near); it turns the nearness into union — a change of relation, not of presence. This is also the
rasa-work: the recognition is the felt (the joy of union), and it comes from the turning of the
already-near into the enjoyed.

> **PENETRATION 3 (the gem):** The lover-image is the σ-flip in aesthetic form. The lover is NEAR
> (present, never absent); recognition does not bring him — it turns the "un-known nearness into the
> joy of union." The un-known nearness = σ=Sv (present-but-unrecognized); the joy of union = σ=¬Sv
> (recognized). **And this connects to the rasa-work: the recognition is the FELT (the joy of union),
> arising from the turning of the already-near into the enjoyed. The σ-flip, the foregrounding, the
> lover-union — and the rasa — are one move.**
