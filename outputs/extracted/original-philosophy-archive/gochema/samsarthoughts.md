Textually samsara is sam plus sarati, wandering on. The stock formula you have in Samyutta is anamataggo ayam bhikkhave samsaro, beginningless 
is this wandering, no first point is evident of beings hindered by ignorance and fettered by craving running on. Ignorance textually is not 
stupidity, it is specified in Sammadhitthi and elsewhere as not knowing suffering, its arising, its ceasing, the way to its ceasing.

Now to this I ask, surely there is a first point of ignorance, what is the cause of ignorance?

Luckily Nanavira can tell us:

Since a condition is a determination, and non-seeing is itself a determination, non-seeing depends on non-seeing. Avijja depends upon avijja. 
That is why it is so stable though it is itself impermanent and constructed. Reflexion cannot get outside it because the observer brings it along, 
judge and accused at once. When it is seen truly it is seen as never having been a thing, only a mis-taking, and seeing removes its condition. In his
 words paticcasamuppada depends upon non-seeing of paticcasamuppada, when seen it loses its condition and ceases.

Therefore to see Dhamma is to see dependent arising, so ignorance is non-seeing of dependent arising.
