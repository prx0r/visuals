## THE GAP TO ADDRESS

The cross-life patisandhi claim requires:
1. **Specificity**: This specific previous life, not any other
2. **Temporal displacement**: Months to years between death and birth (Tucker cases)
3. **Phenomenological verifiability**: Meditators report tracing back, children report verifiable facts

**Mechanisms to test**:
- Resonance coupling: fails on temporal gap
- Quantum Darwinism: fails on selectivity
- Block universe (Deutsch): handles both, gives up local causation
- Retrocausal coupling (Aharonov/Cramer): most precise, needs specification
