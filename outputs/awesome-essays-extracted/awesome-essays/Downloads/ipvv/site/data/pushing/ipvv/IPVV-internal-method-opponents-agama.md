# LOGICVID SESSION — the IPVV's own internal method: the nanu→āha opponent-geography, and why the āgamādhikāra comes after the arguments

*Focusing on the IPVV's internal structure. Two questions from the text's own anatomy: (1) WHICH
opponents appear WHERE (the nanu→āha speaker-geography), and what the geography reveals; (2) WHY the
āgamādhikāra (teaching-section) comes AFTER the argument-sections (jñāna/kriyā). Method: read the
argument maps and the L2 for the internal method, no frameworks.*

---

## PART 1 — THE OPPONENT-GEOGRAPHY (who is argued against, where)

**The data (from the argument maps):**

| Section | The opponents |
|---------|---------------|
| V1 upoddhāta (self-as-Lord) | Buddhist (Sautrāntika), Sāṃkhya |
| V1B-H (self, citsvarūpa, ajaḍa) | Buddhist, Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika |
| V1K-M (memory, determination, reflexion) | Buddhist, Kāṇāda, Sāṃkhya |
| V1N | advaita |
| V2D-G (jñānaśakti, manifestation) | Vijñānavādin, Buddhist, Nyāya |
| V2L-N (smṛti/apohana) | Buddhist (the construction debate) |
| V2O (one-support) | Vaiśeṣika |
| V2R-S (great-Lordship, unity) | Jaiminīya, Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika |
| V3A-D (kriyā, time, measure) | Buddhist, grammarian, Sāṃkhya, Vaiśeṣika/Kāṇāda |
| V3G-I (causality, action) | Buddhist, Vijñānavādin, Sāṃkhya, grammarian |
| V3J-M (āgama, the categories) | the teaching itself |

**The pattern (the internal structure):**
1. **The Buddhist dominates the first half** (V1, V2D-G, V2L-N) — the no-self, the external-object,
   the construction. The jñānādhikāra is primarily a Buddhist-bashing/co-opting.
2. **The Vaiśeṣika/Kāṇāda appears in the metaphysics** (V1H, V2O, V3B) — the substance, the
   inherence, the measure. The reality-structure is argued against the Vaiśeṣika.
3. **The Sāṃkhya appears throughout as the near-rival** (V1F/G, V2S, V3A/G/H) — "not wholly far
   from us," but with the puruṣa as the un-recognized own-lordship.
4. **The grammarians appear in the word/power sections** (V1I, V2I, V3I) — the sphoṭa, the paśyantī.
5. **The Advaita appears only at V1N** — the world-as-illusion is a minor opponent (the real enemy
   is the Buddhist).

> **PENETRATION 1 (the geography):** The opponent-geography is NOT random — it follows the text's own
> needs. The BUDDHIST dominates the knowledge-sections (no-self, external-object, construction);
> the VAIŚEṢIKA appears in the metaphysics (substance, inherence, measure); the SĀṂKHYA is the
> near-rival throughout (the puruṣa as un-recognized lordship); the GRAMMARIANS appear in the
> word/power sections (sphoṭa); and the ADVATIN is a minor opponent (V1N only). **The IPVV argues
> against each rival where that rival's doctrine is at issue — the geography mirrors the doctrine.**

## PART 2 — WHY THE ĀGAMĀDHIKĀRA COMES LAST (the internal method)

**The text's own reason (V3J):**
> "Our perception is not an omniscient eye; our inference lacks the pervasion that would reach every
> thing. Neither can grasp the whole. Only the āgama — the revealed teaching — can do so; for it is
> nothing other than the re-recollection of the un-contracted manifestation of the supreme Lord."

**The internal method:** The IPVV's own order is:
1. **jñānādhikāra** — establish the self, the powers, the knower, by ARGUMENT (against the Buddhist,
   etc.).
2. **kriyādhikāra** — establish action, the will, causality, by ARGUMENT.
3. **āgamādhikāra** — NOW, having established the self by reason, the CATEGORIES (the tattvas) are
   given by the TEACHING, because reason alone cannot reach the whole (perception isn't omniscient,
   inference lacks pervasion).

**The deep internal point:** The IPVV does NOT claim reason can do everything. Its own structure is:
reason establishes the SELF (the jñāna/kriyā adhikāras); then the TEACHING supplies the CONTENT of
the world (the categories), because the whole world cannot be reached by reason alone. **Reason and
revelation are not rivals in the IPVV; they are sequential — reason clears the ground, the āgama
fills in the whole.** This is the text's own internal epistemology of its own method.

> **PENETRATION 2 (the internal method):** The āgamādhikāra comes last because the IPVV's own
> structure is: reason establishes the SELF (jñāna/kriyā), then the TEACHING supplies the WORLD
> (the categories) — because "perception is not an omniscient eye; inference lacks the pervasion;
> only the āgama measures the whole." **Reason and revelation are sequential, not rival: reason
> clears the ground, the āgama fills in the whole. This is the IPVV's own internal epistemology of its
> own method.**

## PART 3 — the Śivatattva as "the body of every category" (the internal pivot of the āgama)

**The text (V3J):**
> "At the heart of it all is the Śivatattva, which is the body of every category... knowledge and
> action — manifestation and reflexive-awareness — are un-joined, inseparable; and this inseparable
> pair is the supreme form of all beings."

**The internal structure:** The āgamādhikāra's deepest claim is that the Śivatattva is the BODY of
every category — the one reality that unfolds into Sadāśiva, Īśvara, śuddhavidyā, and down to the
earth. And its essence is the inseparable pair: knowledge (manifestation) + action (reflexive-
awareness). This is the āgama's version of the powers — the categories are the unfolding of the one
reality whose essence is the jñāna-kriyā pair.

> **PENETRATION 3 (the pivot):** The āgamādhikāra's pivot is the Śivatattva as "the body of every
> category" (V3J) — the one reality unfolding into the tattvas, whose essence is the inseparable
> jñāna-kriyā (manifestation + reflexive-awareness) pair. **The categories are not a separate
> cosmology; they are the unfolding of the one reality whose essence the argument-sections already
> established.** The āgama fills in the WORLD as the self-articulation of what the arguments found in
> the SELF.

## PART 4 — the synthesis: the IPVV's complete internal method

**The whole structure (the text's own architecture):**
1. **Establish the self by reason** (jñānādhikāra): the self is real, self-luminous, the knower —
   against the Buddhist no-self.
2. **Establish the powers by reason** (jñāna/smṛti/apohana/kriyā): the powers unfold in order, and
   the great-Lordship = self-awareness.
3. **Establish action** (kriyādhikāra): the same one acts.
4. **Supply the world by teaching** (āgamādhikāra): the categories unfold from the Śivatattva, the
   body of all.
5. **Close in the tattvādhikāra** (the sum): the recognition, transmitted from guru, śāstra, and
   self.

**The deep internal finding:** The IPVV's method is NOT "reason alone" NOR "revelation alone" — it is
a precise SEQUENCE: reason establishes the SELF (the first two adhikāras), teaching supplies the
WORLD (the third), and the fourth closes the whole. This is why the āgamādhikāra comes after the
arguments: it completes what reason began, by supplying the content reason cannot reach. The IPVV's
own internal epistemology is a reconciliation of reason and revelation — not their opposition.

> **PENETRATION 4 (the complete method):** The IPVV's internal method is a precise SEQUENCE: reason
> establishes the SELF (jñāna/kriyā), teaching supplies the WORLD (āgama), and the fourth adhikāra
> closes the whole. The āgamādhikāra comes after the arguments because it completes what reason began
> — the content (the categories) that reason alone cannot reach. **The IPVV's own epistemology is a
> reconciliation of reason and revelation, not their opposition.**

## THE LOGICVID LESSON

Reading the IPVV by its own internal structure yields the text's complete method:

1. **The opponent-geography mirrors the doctrine:** the Buddhist dominates the knowledge-sections;
   the Vaiśeṣika the metaphysics; the Sāṃkhya the near-rival; the grammarians the word-sections; the
   Advaitin is minor (V1N).

2. **The āgamādhikāra comes last because it supplies what reason can't:** "perception is not an
   omniscient eye; inference lacks the pervasion; only the āgama measures the whole." Reason
   establishes the SELF; the teaching supplies the WORLD.

3. **The Śivatattva is "the body of every category"** — the categories unfold from the one reality
   whose essence (jñāna-kriyā) the argument-sections established.

4. **The IPVV's method is reason + revelation, in sequence** — not their opposition. Reason clears
   the ground; the āgama fills in the whole.

**The deep internal finding:** The IPVV is a reconciliation of reason and revelation — a precise
sequence where reason establishes the self and the teaching supplies the world. This is its own
internal structure, visible only when read as a whole arc, not as separate vimarśas.

*This is the internal-method session. The opponent-geography mirrors the doctrine; the āgamādhikāra
completes what reason began; the Śivatattva is the body of all; and the IPVV's method is reason +
revelation in sequence.*
