# NIBBANA SERMONS — the magnum opus (33 sermons + Analayo analysis)

## Sources (all in repo)
- `library/nanananda/nibbana-mind-stilled-LE.pdf` — Library Edition, all 33
  sermons, 802pp. THE transcript.
- `library/analayo/Nibbana-01.pdf … Nibbana-33.pdf` — Bhikkhu Analayo's full
  3-year lecture series (2017/18/19, Barre/Numata): per-sermon notes + Chinese
  parallels + translation corrections. THE analysis to build on.
- Audio (not in repo, reference): 33 English MP3s (~50–65 min each) at
  seeingthroughthenet.net + 33-video YouTube playlist + Analayo lecture audio
  on Dharma Seed (retreat 4373).

## Analayo's role (from his interview)
He transcribed all 33 sermons from Ñāṇananda's tapes over ten years and
supplied the references. His lectures: contextualize (Chinese parallels),
correct (PTS vs Bodhi translations), and connect to practice. Our v2 videos
inherit his corrections — never voice an outdated translation he flagged.

## Build order (proposed)
1. Sermon 1 + Analayo Lecture 1 as the pilot pair (fire-simile is the trailer).
2. Thematic clusters next (vortex sermons, anatta sermons, cessation sermons),
   not 1→33 straight (correspondence rule applies: mine, don't march).
3. Each video: sermon sculpt (v1) + Analayo-corrected analysis (v2).
