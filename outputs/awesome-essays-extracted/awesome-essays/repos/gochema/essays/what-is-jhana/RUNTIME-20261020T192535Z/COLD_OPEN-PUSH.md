## OBJECTION 1 — "jhana is actually perpetually being ruined by you, each moment you are actually actively taking yourself out of a state of jhana"

**The claim:** The mind is perpetually in jhana; you are actively pulling yourself out of it moment by moment.

**The answer from the banked sources:** SILENT. No banked file asserts jhana as a perpetual baseline or that you are "actively" leaving it each moment. The closest banked material describes jhana as something that results from practice — HH 205: "Jhana is an establishment of mind that results from your own efforts of purifying your lifestyle. It's not a magical experience that will purify your life for you." That is the opposite framing: jhana as an achievement, not a default. MN 27 describes jhana as something entered after abandoning the five hindrances — a state you arrive at, not one you're already in. The PUSHBACK file (n2) calls the line "poetically right and mechanism-correct at the level the video operates" but does not source it.

**Resolution:** The claim is a rhetorical inversion, not a sourced teaching. It works as a hook but has no banked textual support.

**Honest remainder:** If the video presents this as Nyanamoli's or the suttas' position, it is unsourced. If presented as the owner's own framing, it needs to be flagged as such.

## OBJECTION 2 — "mastery is the inverse" / "grabbing is the interference"

**The claim:** Treating jhana as something to master or attain is the inverse of what jhana is; the grabbing itself is the obstacle.

**The answer from the banked sources:** Partially supported. HH 38 (natural-state extract) contains the owl-and-mouse comparison: "when a person doesn't know how to meditate rightly, he mis-meditates... like an owl waiting for a mouse to show its head so he can grab it." This supports the anti-grabbing framing. HH 38 also distinguishes right samādhi (composing the nature of things to endure) from wrong concentration (hunting the particular). But neither source says "mastery is the inverse" as a formulation — that is the owner's synthesis.

**Resolution:** The anti-grabbing mechanism is sourced (HH 38). The specific "mastery is the inverse" phrasing is the owner's rhetorical compression, not a quote.

**Honest remainder:** The script should not attribute "mastery is the inverse" to Nyanamoli or the suttas without a verbatim source.
