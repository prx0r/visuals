## THE PROBE (SUPERSEDED — ESSAY v1.1+ ch.2 carries the owner's verbatim draft1 #3 for this beat; the agent-drafted paragraph below is reference only, do not reintroduce without owner sign-off)
"right now: are you being born? are you dying? eh, doesn't really work like
that, does it. you'd say: i was born, i am now in a state of aliveness, then
i die and i am in a state of unaliveness. two events, one state between them.
that's the logic we all start from. but buddhism's whole trip is that the
state isn't a state — it's a doing. birth isn't over, death isn't scheduled.
the question isn't what happens after the machinery shuts off. the question
is: what exactly is the 'i am in a state of aliveness' made of, moment to
moment — and if you can answer that, the after becomes a smaller question
than you thought. that's the journey: death and rebirth of the present
moment. and if that sounds like too much — skip to the end, the last school
says it in four words."
