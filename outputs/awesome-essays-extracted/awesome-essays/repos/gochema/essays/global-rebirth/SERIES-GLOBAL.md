### The mechanism, per Tantrāloka 10.292–307 (in house, deepdive plans)
- V.292-293 (verbatim, Sanskrit in hand): "eteṣāṁ maraṇābhikhyo bhogo nāsti
  tu ye tanum | dhāraṇābhis tyajanty āśu paradehapraveśavat" — the realized
  have no death-like experience: they abandon the body swiftly BY dhāraṇā
  (meditative stabilization on the reality-levels) — "like entering another body."
- WHY it's possible: (1) identification with the body was already abandoned —
  withdrawal, not expiry; (2) at the 36-tattva summit the yogi IS in Bhairava
  consciousness, and (TĀ 10.303-307) "his consciousness expands to fill the
  universe" — a universal consciousness RELOCALIZING is not travel (light
  reaching a second room without leaving the first); (3) svātantrya —
  consciousness is fundamentally FREE, so it can re-anchor. Against whom does
  this work? only the bound: niyati/kalā ("limited actions… are binding") —
  the logicvid crux: karma-as-law vs svātantrya-as-freedom.
- Ordinary rebirth in-system (pushing-tantraloka session, PRE-BUILT):
  three-impurity chain āṇava → māyā → karmamala (PTv/TĀ): karmamala "gives
  rise to rebirth and the experience of reward and retribution."
- IPVV/Torella (in hand): the karmic identity-crisis "makes him consider his
  own actions as the causes of the series of rebirths, miring him in the
  samsara" — liberation = inverting the relation, bodha-svātantrya at the
  centre of the individual.
