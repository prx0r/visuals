## 2. THE RESEARCH OBJECTS (the structured evidence)

### ro-nanavira-clearing-path (`basecamp/knowledge/research-objects/buddhism/` + `research-library/research-objects/`)
**"Clearing the Path / Notes on Dhamma — Ñāṇavīra Thera"** — a stub RO (v0.1.0) centered on Fundamental Structure.

Key passage (p_001 — the tetrahedron as the constraint space):
> "The diagram is a tetrahedron: four vertices (subject, object, intention, act) with six
> edges… This IS the constraint space (S, C, A) approached from the phenomenological side. S
> is the space of possible experience, C is the structure of conditionality
> (paṭicca-samuppāda), A is nibbāna as the cessation of the dynamic. The tetrahedron IS the
> simplest positive geometry — the minimal complete structure from which all experience is
> generated."

**Contributions:** fundamental_structure, negative_thinking, paticca_samuppada,
sakkaya_ditthi, dukkha_analysis, satta.

### ro-nanananda-papanca (`basecamp/.../buddhism/` + `research-library/research-objects/`)
**"Papañca and Nāma-Rūpa — The Construction of Experience"** — Ñāṇananda's Sermon 1 (and 3).

**Key passages (the passage-level gold):**
- **p_001 (nāma-rūpa = name-and-form, not mind-and-matter):** "Ñāṇananda's central claim:
  nāma-rūpa means 'name-and-form,' not 'mind-and-matter.' The commentaries redefined nāma
  as 'bending'…"
- **p_002 (the child and the rubber ball):** the toddler who cannot speak is given a ball;
  he knows it through contact, not naming — the prototype of pre-verbal experience.
- **p_004 (the fire simile):** "A fire burns dependent on fuel (upādāna, meaning both 'fuel'
  and 'grasping')." — the fuel/running-out model of nibbāna.
- **p_006 (karma as pathway):** "Karma is not a metaphysical law of cosmic justice but the
  conditioning of habitual pathways."
- **p_007 (nibbāna as the fuel running out):** "Nibbāna is not a place, a thing to see, or a
  state to enter after death. It is what happens when the fuel of grasping runs out — here
  and now."
- **p_008 (the child's meditation):** "take an object, close your eyes, do not name it —
  explore through contact (phassa)."

**Contributions:** nama_rupa_recovery, child_and_ball, fire_simile, taṇhakkhayo_as_nibbana,
papanca_tangle.

## 3. THE ESSAYS (the usable prose)

### `workengestation/output/essays/general/blog-assembly-essays/04_recognition_begins_by_drawing_a_boundary.md`
The recognition essay — directly usable:
> "Before an organism can recognize anything, it must distinguish something… A boundary
> appears before an object can become stable… Recognition begins by drawing a boundary."

It explicitly engages the danger of equivocation:
> "The scientific observer identifies patterns. The Tantric Self recognizes its own
> manifestation. These are not equivalent acts. But both begin with the mystery of how
> continuity is carved from flux."

**This is the essay's Part II bridge** — the boundary-drawing that precedes recognition.
