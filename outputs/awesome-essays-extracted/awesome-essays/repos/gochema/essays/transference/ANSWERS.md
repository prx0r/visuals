## I. MECHANICS

**Q1 — viability line?** TBOTB gives TIMING not criteria: "undertaken when the
indications of death are unequivocally present and when, even after performing
ritual deception on three occasions, it is indicated that one will not recover"
(death/transference passages, TBOTB 8599-8603). SILENCE on reanimatable-vs-not:
no criterion exists in-bank. The kuśa test (quotes-karmachakme.txt) tests the
TRAINEE's aperture, not the corpse. Verdict: circularity charge stands.

**Q2 — 3-day window?** "most sutras and tantras state that the period of
unconsciousness [following death] may last for three and a half days" (TBOTB
9380-83); "do not touch the body… until vital energy leaves… a few moments or
up to three days" (TBOTB 21644); inner breath lasts "the time taken to eat a
meal" (TBOTB 9330-32). Karma Chakme: "consciousness remains in the body for up
to three nights and a day… acceptable… up until the seventh day."

**Q3/Q17 — failure, witnesses?** Failure path exists: "If these signs do
emerge, there will be no need to elucidate the intermediate states. But if they
do not appear, the spiritual teacher should place his lips close to the ear"
(TBOTB 8668-71) — fallback to hearing-liberation, i.e. failure is ROUTED not
recorded. Witnesses: teacher/nurses/disciples only; SILENCE on neutrals.
Wrong-timing = "the crime of slaying a deity, and the crime of committing
suicide" (TBOTB 8442-46).

**Q4 — guide-out vs move-in?** Guide-out banked both sides (TBOTB 8393-95,
8976-78; Karma Chakme ii). Move-in: Karma Chakme's entering-body line + "no
lineage of actual practice." NO principled distinction stated anywhere in-bank.
Verdict: the asymmetry is real and undefended — use it.
