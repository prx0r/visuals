## PAID — BOOKS WORTH BUYING (gold, in priority order)

1. **Dyczkowski, Tantrāloka 11 vols** (~the work itself + Jayaratha; anuttaratrikakula.org) — the Hindu-tantra spine. Nothing substitutes.
2. **Ratié, *Le Soi et l'Autre* (Brill, 2011)** — recognition debate, our whole Trika wing's scholarly anchor.
3. **Ratié, *Power of Action* (HUP/Harvard Oriental Series 96, 2021)** — IPVV ch.2.1 edition + study; Proof of God adjacent.
4. **Torella, *Utpaladeva, Philosopher of Recognition* (OUP)** — the other half of the Ratié/Torella axis.
5. **Flood, *The Tantric Body* (Bloomsbury)** — body-as-text thesis; enter-another-body + parapraveśa framing.
6. **Lakshmanjoo, *Manual for Self Realization* / VBT (Universal Shaiva Fellowship)** — lineage voice (we hold epubs; print for the shelf + support the source).
7. **Analayo, *Rebirth in Early Buddhism*** — A-DEBATE backbone (check: PDF already in-repo? `analayo-rebirth-early-buddhism.pdf` — confirm before buying).
8. **Patrul Rinpoche, *Words of My Perfect Teacher* (Shambhala/Padmakara)** — Longchen Nyingtik phowa compilation source.
9. **Eltschinger & Krasser (eds.), *Scriptural Authority, Reason and Action* (ÖAW)** — Ratié ch. + the reason-vs-scripture pattern across schools.
10. **Linder JIP 2020 article / White 2010 festschrift / Jonker 2021** — library/rental tier, not buy tier (single chapters each).
