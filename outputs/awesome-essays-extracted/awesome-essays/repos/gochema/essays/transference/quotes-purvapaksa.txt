## THE OPPONENT'S VERSE (ĪPK upoddhāta 6 — their memory theory)

"tato bhinneṣu dharmeṣu tatsvarūpāviśeṣataḥ | saṃskārātsmṛtisiddhau
syātsmartā draṣṭeva kalpitaḥ || 6 ||"

"Then, in the distinct properties, [being] non-different from its own form —
from the impression, [when] the memory is established, the rememberer would be
inferred, like the seer. (6)"

## NO KNOWER APART (the no-owner engine — Q8's Buddhist answer)

"But that which shines as 'I remember (ahaṃ smartā)' — there, first, that is
indeed the 'I'-awareness (ahaṃ-pratīti); not the self [as] momentary, from the
non-stability (asthiratva). … Even so, no knower (vedaka), distinct from the
known-and-the-knowing (vedya-vedanā), of the form of the rememberer-and-seer
(smartṛ-draṣṭṛ-rūpa), shines."

## MEMORY CANNOT CROSS STREAMS (Q5's Buddhist answer)

"even [with] the impression existing, in another's seeing (paradarśi), the
memory is improper — [thus] the loss-of-the-practical-activity
(vyavahāra-lopa) [is] said."

## THE SEVEN-VERSE REFUTATION FRAME (what Abhinavagupta is answering)

"True, but (satyaṃ kintu)…" — beginning with this, up to "…having-the-power-
of-the-repulsion-of-memory (smṛtyapohanaśaktimān)" — "by this seven-verse
(śloka-saptaka), [Abhinava,] reflecting the non-possibility of the rival views
(paradarśana-anupapatti), refutes it."

## THE RE-CONNECTION PROBLEM (their own concession the proof exploits)

"For the properties, the pleasures-and-the-rest, [being] re-connected
(pratisandhīyamāna), are seen; and of them, [being] distinct, how [is] the
re-connection (pratisandhi)? — the one support (āśraya) must be said."
