## THE ENGINE (why no creator)

**[B1] AN 10.216 Saṁsappanīya — the deed-womb (pi, tid 3713)** —
"Kammassakā, bhikkhave, sattā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū
kammapaṭisaraṇā, yaṁ kammaṁ karonti—kalyāṇaṁ vā pāpakaṁ vā—tassa dāyādā
bhavanti."
— "Beings are owners of their actions, heirs of their actions, born of
their actions, bound by their actions, have their actions as their refuge;
whatever action they do, good or bad, they become heirs of it."
— SLOT: CH.4 engine paragraph · THE line for "cosmology with a different
engine". Scored companion: the crooked-gati passage continues "jimhā gati,
jimhupapatti" (crooked destination, crooked rebirth) → animal realm = snake,
scorpion, centipede, mongoose, cat, rat, owl (the animal list is in-text).

**[B2] DN 26 Cakkavatti-Sīhanāda — time-cosmology / the ten-year floor
(pi, tid 255)** —
"…tesaṁ āyunāpi parihāyamānānaṁ vaṇṇenapi parihāyamānānaṁ vīsativassasahassāyukānaṁ
manussānaṁ dasavassasahassāyukā puttā ahesuṁ."
— "As their lifespan and their beauty kept declining, the children of men
who lived twenty thousand years were men of ten thousand years."
— (SC translation, verified same session: "lifespan has fallen to ten
years. Among the humans who live for ten years, girls will be marriageable
at five.")
— SLOT: CH.6 — the world deteriorates on a schedule: poverty → theft →
weapons → killing → lying → slander → sexual misconduct → lifespan halves,
generation by generation (the full causal ladder is verbatim in the text
above). NOTE: this is MORAL arithmetic, not apocalypse-porn — keep the
ladder.

## THE GOD WHO WAS MADE BY MIND

**[B3] DN 1 Brahmajāla — the creator's delusion (pi, tid 230)** —
"So tattha hoti manomayo pītibhakkho sayampabho antalikkhacaro… Tassa
tattha ekakassa dīgharattaṁ nivusitattā anabhirati paritassanā uppajjati:
'aho vata aññepi sattā itthattaṁ āgaccheyyun'ti."
— "There he is mind-made, fed on rapture, self-luminous, wandering in
midair… After dwelling there alone for a long time, dissatisfaction and
longing arise: 'Oh, if only other beings would come to this state.'"
— SLOT: CH.5 beat 1 — mind-made + fed on rapture + LONELINESS creates the
population.

**[B4] DN 1 — the proclamation (pi, tid 230)** —
"'ahamasmi brahmā mahābrahmā abhibhū anabhibhūto aññadatthudaso vasavattī
issaro kattā nimmātā seṭṭho sajitā vasī pitā bhūtabhabyānaṃ. Mayā ime
sattā nimmitā. Taṃ kissa hetu? Mamañhi pubbe etadahosi: "aho vata aññepi
sattā itthattaṁ āgaccheyyun"ti. Iti mama ca manopaṇidhi, ime ca sattā
itthattaṁ āgatā'ti."
— "I am Brahmā, the great Brahmā, the victor, the unconquered, the
all-seeing, the sovereign, the maker, the creator, the supreme, the
controller, the father of all that are and are to be. These beings were
made by me. And why? Because once I thought: 'Oh, if only other beings
would come to this state.' And so, by MY MIND-PREFERENCE, these beings
came to this state."
— SLOT: CH.5 money quote. The trap IN THE TEXT: "Mayā ime sattā nimmitā"
(they were MADE BY ME) followed one sentence later by "mama manopaṇidhi"
(BY MY MIND-WISH). He concludes creator from mind-wish. And the latecomers
believe him: "ayaṁ kho bhavaṁ brahmā… iminā mayaṁ bhotā brahmunā nimmitā."
— "this Brahmā made us."

**[B5] MN 99 Subha — the path to Brahmā's company (pi, tid 362)** —
"Katamo ca, māṇava, brahmānaṁ sahabyatāya maggo? Idha, māṇava, bhikkhu
mettāsahagatena cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati…"
— "And what, young man, is the path to Brahmā's company? There the
monk abides pervading one quarter with a mind of loving-kindness…"
— SLOT: CH.5 button — the heaven is an ADDRESS REACHABLE BY METHOD
(mettā = the road to the brahma-world). Cosmology-as-practice: same world,
entered by training. Guards against "the Buddhists didn't believe their
own maps" overcorrection.

## THE FAMOUS WHEEL

**[B6] Dhammapada 1–2 Yamakavagga — mind-made (pi, tid 3779)** —
"Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā;
Manasā ce paduṭṭhena, bhāsati vā karoti vā;
Tato naṁ dukkhamanveti, cakkaṁva vahato padaṁ."
(and the pair:) "Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā;
Manasā ce pasannena, bhāsati vā karoti vā;
Tato naṁ sukhamanveti, chāyāva anapāyinī."
— "Phenomena are preceded by mind, ruled by mind, made of mind. If one
speaks or acts with a corrupted mind, suffering follows that one as the
wheel follows the foot of the draught-ox. If one speaks or acts with a
clear mind, happiness follows that one as a shadow that never departs."
— SLOT: CH.9 mental-reading pillar 1 — "manomayā" (MADE OF MIND) is the
line every psychology-of-religion anthology quotes. Pair it against [B1]
in the same breath: the canon contains BOTH.

**[B7] SN 35.107 Lokasamudaya — the world is the origin of contact
(pi, tid 1408)** —
"Lokassa, bhikkhave, samudayañca atthaṅgamañca desessāmi… Katamo ca,
bhikkhave, lokassa samudayo? Cakkhuñca paṭicca rūpe ca uppajjati
cakkhuviññāṇaṁ. Tiṇṇaṁ saṅgati phasso. Phassapaccayā vedanā; vedanāpaccayā
taṇhā…"
— "I will teach you the origin and disappearance of the world… And what is
the origin of the world? With the eye and forms as condition there arises
eye-consciousness; the meeting of the three is contact; with contact as
condition, feeling; with feeling as condition, craving…" (chain continues
to aging-and-death)
— SLOT: CH.3 — the Buddha's OWN usage of "world" (loka) = the sense-field
chain. Minimalism: no Sumeru needed for liberation. Companion: SN 12.44
(tid 744) = same formula for householders.

**[B8] Kangyur Toh 174 — the realms are nonrealms (en, 84k/174.1)** —
"The wise understand the desire realm, the form realm, and the formless
realm to be utterly nonexistent realms. The wise do not observe the desire
realm, the form realm, or the formless realm. By understanding that the
three realms are devoid of inherent existence and fixed qualities, but
arise from many causes and conditions, the wise thus understand the three
realms to be nonrealms."
— (preceding, in-text): "There is no desire realm in the desire realm…
The absence of realms is indicated and articulated by using realms."
— SLOT: CH.9 pillar 3 — the deconstructive cut, from the KANGYUR itself
(not a modern philosopher): realms = vocabulary-without-inherent-existence,
"shown" the way you'd show emptiness. THE answer to "are they all mental?"
: the wise "do not observe" them — but the untaught are shown them BY
realms.

**[B9] Dhp 167–178 — bubble and mirage (en, sc/dhp167-178.1)** —
"Look upon the world as a bubble or a mirage, then the King of Death
won't see you."
— SLOT: CH.9 closing beat — the seeing-instruction itself.

## THE INSTRUMENT (CH.8 — how the map is made)

**[B10] DN 4 Soṇadaṇḍa — the map is declared from knowledge (pi, tid 233)** —
"So imaṁ lokaṁ sadevakaṁ samārakaṁ sabrahmakaṁ sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiṁ
pajaṁ sadevamanussaṁ sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā pavedeti."
— "This world with its gods, its Māras, its Brahmās, this generation with
its recluses and brahmins, its gods and humans — having realized it with
HIS OWN KNOWLEDGE, he declares it."
— SLOT: CH.11 beat 1 money quote — lokavidū ("knower of the world") is the
title; the construction method is `sayaṁ abhiññā` (own-direct-knowledge),
not survey. The formula appears in the sutta's OWN mouth twice (DN 4 —
verified this session at both occurrences).

**[B11] Kangyur Toh 10 — the divine eye, named equipment (en, tid 5164)** —
"With their purified divine eye that far surpasses the human, they see
beings, and they know beings that are dying and being born; are beautiful,
ugly, vile, or exemplary; are in a good form of life and in a terrible
form of life — exactly in accord with the action."
— SLOT: CH.11 beat 1 — the finer sensor, in the tradition's own equipment
list. Pairs with Wheeler (SCHOLARSHIP, S3): both name an instrument as
prior to the result.

**[B12] Vism XII — openings, not roads (in-house BPS Ñāṇamoli PDF)** —
"he split the earth in two and showed Avīci, and he parted the sky in two
and showed the Brahmā-world." · elder Dhammadinna "turned his fan face
upwards and an opening right up to the Brahmā-world appeared. Having
thus aroused fear of hell and longing for the bliss of heaven, the elder
taught the Dhamma."
— Cite: Visuddhimagga, Ch. XII (Supernormal Powers), §§71, 80 — in-house
`library/theravada/visuddhimagga-path-of-purification.pdf` (extracted this
session, lines 22750 / 22860 of pdftotext).
— SLOT: CH.11 beat 1 visual — the map is OPENED FROM HERE (the fan
opening = the cheapest, truest money-shot idea in the film).

**[B13] MN 63 Cūḷamālukya — what he REFUSED (pi, tid 326, read this session)** —
Three banked passages:
(i) The undeclared list: "yānimāni diṭṭhigatāni bhagavatā abyākatāni
ṭhapitāni paṭikkhittāni: 'sassato loko'tipi, 'asassato loko'tipi,
'antavā loko'tipi, 'anantavā loko'tipi, 'taṁ jīvaṁ taṁ sarīran'tipi…
'neva hoti na na hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā'tipi" — "the positions
left undeclared, set aside, rejected by the Blessed One: the cosmos is
eternal / not eternal / finite / infinite / soul-and-body one or two /
the Realized One after death exists… does not, both, neither."
(ii) The poisoned arrow: "Seyyathāpi, mālukyaputta, puriso sallena viddho
assa savisena gāḷhapalepanena… 'na tāvāhaṁ imaṁ sallaṁ āharissāmi yāva
na taṁ purisaṁ jānāmi yenamhi viddho'" — "Suppose a man were struck by an
arrow smeared thickly with poison… 'I will not have this arrow removed
until I know the man by whom I was shot — a khattiya, a brāhmaṇa, a
vess…" (the full birth/caste/shaft/bow list continues in-text).
(iii) The refusal formula + what he DID declare: "Na hetaṁ, mālukyaputta,
atthasaṁhitaṁ… na nibbidāya na virāgāya na nirodhāya na upasamāya na
abhiññāya na sambodhāya na nibbānāya saṁvattati. Tasmā taṁ mayā
abyākataṁ. Kiñca, mālukyaputta, mayā byākataṁ? 'Idaṁ dukkhan'ti…
'ayaṁ dukkhanirodho'ti… 'ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā'ti" — "It is
not beneficial… it does not conduce to disenchantment, dispassion,
cessation… or nibbāna. That is why I have not declared it. And what HAVE I
declared? This is suffering / its origin / its cessation / the path."
— SLOT: CH.3 beat C — THE definitive stance: procedure over topology.
The verdict-question for every later cosmology in the film: not "is it
true" but "does it conduce to cessation" (the Buddha's own test, stated
in his own words). Pairs with DN 1 (same frame: 62 views catalogued).

## ÑĀṆAVĪRA (in-house — Clearing the Path, essays/fire-simile/library/clearing-the-path.pdf, read this session)
> Legacy encoding in the PDF (jàti/taõhà) normalized to standard diacritics
> below; re-verify wording against page numbers at draft (compiler law).

**[N1] — structural, not temporal (the resolution of physical/psychological/temporal):**
"paṭiccasamuppāda has nothing to do with temporal succession (cause-and-
effect). Precedence in paṭiccasamuppāda is structural, not temporal:
paṭiccasamuppāda is not the description of a process."
— Plus his critique of the three-life reading: so long as it is read as
temporal succession, rebirth "is liable to be regarded as some kind of
hypothesis (that there is rebirth and that it is caused by avijjā) to be
verified (or not) in the course of time (like any hypothesis of the
natural sciences)."
— SLOT: CH.9b Beat E — the clean resolution: the chain is a present
STRUCTURE ("as it is, so it was, so it will be"), so neither geography-
outdated nor psychology-vs-physics applies; the hypothesis-frame is the
error.

**[N2] — direct seeing HERE, authority THERE (his both-and):**
"It is possible for a Buddha to see the rebirth that is at each moment
awaiting a living individual who still has taṇhā… the dependence of
rebirth upon taṇhā is a matter of direct seeing, not involving time.
But this is by no means always possible… for an ariyasāvaka, who, though
he sees paṭiccasamuppāda for himself… may still need to accept rebirth on
the Buddha's authority."
— And the trust-formula: "To be a follower of the Buddha it is certainly
necessary to accept on trust that for one who is not rid of avijjā at his
death there is rebirth, but it is by no means sufficient. What is
sufficient is to see paṭiccasamuppāda."
— SLOT: CH.9b Beat E — the owner's "both" intuition, precisely stated:
seen here (the dependence, timeless), trusted there (the successive
existence) — and he uses THE deathless-formula for the chain itself:
"sandiññhiko akāliko ehipassiko… immediate, timeless, evident" (MN 8
quoted).

**[N3] — not-a-hypothesis + the Buddha is not an agnostic:**
"The Buddha does not explain how rebirth takes place; he states simply
that, unless craving has ceased, rebirth does take place." · any
acceptance built on scientific explanations of rebirth "is built on
quicksand" · and: "A curious view, that the Buddha was an agnostic on the
question of rebirth and refused to pronounce on it, seems to be gaining
currency. Even a very slight acquaintance with the Suttas will correct
this idea."
— SLOT: CH.9b Beat B2 (the cascade) — the counter to "could rebirth be
outdated too?": outdated applies to hypotheses; this isn't one.

**[N4] — the man who banked his own death (letters, context only):**
Letter 1 of the collection is titled "'to be opened in the event of my
death'". The decision, his words: "The ravages of amœbiasis play havoc
with the practice of mental concentration, and if I cannot practise
mental concentration I have no further use for this life… there is no
doubt at all (barring accidents) which I should choose. For me at least,
the more intelligent of these two courses of action is suicide."
— And the rebirth-expectation question, answered by his own position: he
recorded sotāpatti (27.6.1959, banked in rebirth resource 05) — a
stream-enterer, not arahat — so HIS OWN FORMULA [N2] applies to him: at
his death, avijjā not fully gone → rebirth taken on trust-and-seeing. The
only-arahat line he cites frames the alternative: "Only the arahat has no
anguish in the face of death" (Theragāthā 606–607, quoted in CTP).
— SLOT: CH.9b Beat E coda — handle soberly, no melodrama; the film's
point is that his epistemology and his death agree.

**[N7] AJAHN ÑĀṆAMOLI (living, Hillside Hermitage) — 18hh "Saṃsāra"

as a reference point, from which you die and then come back in some sort

— SLOT: CH.9b Beat D — the living school's one-sentence version of N1–N2:
no travel, re-arising is knowable HERE. (Transcript is PDF-extracted;

**[N8] The same talk — the immediacy formula:**
"A change of rebirth is more accurately reflected upon as a change of

— SLOT: CH.7 (fractal) — the living traditional voice: rebirth = change

**[N9] "With birth, death applies" (Ñāṇamoli, Path Press 2010 — IN-HOUSE
`library/hillside-hermitage/HILLSIDE-HERMITAGE.md`, verbatim bank):**
"temporal things (events in time) are possible *only* because there is
an atemporal structure (*nature* of time) – time is *secondary* to one's

— SLOT: CH.9b Beat D — the akālika hinge in the living heir's own words;

**[N5] ÑĀṆANANDA — the expansion (in-house — essays/fire-simile/SOURCE-RAW.txt,
the fire-simile sermons, read this session):**
(a) "It is due to that grasping conditioned by the fire of craving that

— SLOT: CH.9b Beat D — THE expansion of N1–N2: rebirth as germination-
