# LOGICVID SESSION — parallels & counters: the IPVV kāryakāraṇatāvimarśa (kārikās 20–21) vs. the Tantrāloka

*Freestyle Logicvid. The task: take the V3-I passage (the close of the kāryakāraṇatāvimarśa —
"action is the one will-to-do that joins agent and object; the difference of things is real,
not mere ignorance; will = cause + agent + action") and hound the TANTRĀLOKA for arguments
that parallel or counter it. Method: find the text's own statements of the same structure, and
any place where it pushes back. No frameworks.*

---

## ROUND 0 — what the V3-I passage claims (the claims to test)

1. **Action = the one will-to-do (parāmarśa) that joins agent and object.** "There is a single
   re-recollection marked by the will-to-do — the moment 'I will make this' — which rests in
   the other consciousness-essence and binds the two together."
2. **The difference is REAL, not mere ignorance.** The refutation of the Advaita view: "if all
   is one, the difference is ignorance — then *of whom* is this ignorance?" The one-form
   Brahman cannot have it; the separate souls would be raised up by it; "un-explainable
   (anirvācya)" is the speech of a yakṣa. And: "our cognition rests in difference as it does
   not rest in non-difference"; the refutation itself would be a manifestation, hence
   difference, hence ignorance — self-undermining. "Even the revealed teaching would be
   ignorance." (Note: "even Brahman with ignorance is admitted by some, so this is not an
   absolute monism either.")
3. **The three powers are one action-power under different aspects.** "The three subtle powers
   dwell in harmony." Action-tattva is seen in the agent by presence-and-absence.
4. **Will (icchā) = cause-ness + agent-ness + action.** "The cause does not cause, the agent
   does not act, the action does not move — all three are the one will, by which the Lord,
   wishing to stand, becomes the world."

---

## ROUND 1 — PARALLEL: the Tantrāloka says the same thing about action = will

**The Tantrāloka's own statements (Vol 2):**
- "The will itself pervaded by its own heat has assumed three aspects" (TĀ Vol 2, 20359) —
  the will as the one source of the three powers. **Direct parallel to claim 3** (one
  action-power under three aspects).
- Note 532: "The will and its object are inherently one, so the state of the object is
  essentially that of the will" (TĀ Vol 2, 20378). **Direct parallel to claim 1** (the will
  joins agent and object; the object's state is the will's).
- From the earlier sessions: "Action is not something different from knowledge; rather it is
  knowledge itself which, in order to serve as a means, has assumed the nature of a succession
  (krama)" (TĀ 1/231) — and the dynamism chain (will → knowledge → action) from the Q2/Q3
  session. **Parallel to claim 4** (the three powers are one act of consciousness).

**The convergence:** The Tantrāloka and the IPVV agree: action is not an external event but the
will of consciousness. The will "pervaded by its own heat assumes three aspects" = the one
action-power seen as will/knowledge/action = the one self-apprehension under three modes (the
master-key's powers). The object is not external; its state is the will's.

> **PENETRATION 1 (parallel):** The Tantrāloka confirms the IPVV's central claim: action = the
> will of consciousness, and the three powers are ONE action-power under three aspects ("the
> will itself pervaded by its own heat has assumed three aspects"; "the will and its object are
> inherently one"). This is the SAME structure as the master-key's powers-as-modes-of-vimarśa.

## ROUND 2 — PARALLEL (the strongest): the difference is REAL — the pratibimbavāda

**The Tantrāloka's own statement (Vol 2, 5195–5199):**
"The Buddhist... serves to illustrate that the world is just an unreal appearance. It appears,
but has no independent nature of its own, like a reflection in a mirror. **However, Abhinava,
following the lead of Utpaladeva, takes care to stress that the reflections are real. The face
reflected in the mirror is my own. It is a true reflection, not a mistake.**"

**The parallel to claim 2 (difference is real):** This is EXACTLY the IPVV's refutation of the
ignorance/illusion view. The world is not a mere unreal appearance (the Buddhist/Yogācāra or
Advaita "ignorance" view); the reflections are REAL — "a true reflection, not a mistake." The
difference of things is real because it is the self's own reflection, not a false
superimposition.

> **PENETRATION 2 (the strongest parallel):** The Tantrāloka gives the IPVV's "difference is
> real" claim its ground: the reflections are REAL, "a true reflection, not a mistake." The
> world's difference is not a mere appearance to be dismissed as ignorance; it is the self's
> own reflection in the mirror of consciousness. This parallels the IPVV's rejection of the
> un-explainable-illusion view — and it also grounds the master-key's "the object is recognized
> as the self": the reflection is "my own" face.

## ROUND 3 — the COUNTER: the "difference is real" vs. the master-key's "difference is contracted"

**The question:** Here is a real tension to push. The master-key sessions (impurities) said the
difference (māyīya) is the CONTRACTED power — the outward turn that constitutes bondage. But
V3-I and the Tantrāloka say the difference is REAL ("a true reflection, not a mistake"). Are
these contradictory? Is difference real, or is it the impurity?

**The resolution (from the text's own logic):** The key is to distinguish TWO senses of
"difference":
- **Difference as real manifestation** (the pratibimbavāda): the difference of things is real
  because it is the self's own reflection — the world is the self's manifestation. This is
  what V3-I and TĀ 3/1-4 affirm: the reflections are real.
- **Difference as SEPARATION** (māyīya, the impurity): the *false* reading that the difference
  indicates *independent external substances* — the ego's externalization, the exhaustiveness-
  claim. This is what the master-key's impurities session called the contracted power.

**The text's own statement of the distinction** (from the earlier sessions, TĀ 1/52 + ad 1/52):
"the difference blue/not-blue arises through an act of reflective awareness (vimarśa)... its
arising ultimately culminates in a state of oneness (tādātmya)." Difference is real AND
culminates in oneness — because it is the self's own differentiation, not an external
separation. And the impurities session: māyīya = "the sense of duality" = difference *misread
as external separation*.

**The synthesis:** Difference is REAL (the reflection is true, it is the self's own face), and
difference is NOT the impurity — the *impurity* is the misreading of that real difference as
external separation. The same content (difference) is real-as-manifestation and
bonding-as-separation. The difference is not to be *removed* (it is real); the *misreading* of
it (as external, as exhaustive) is to be removed. This is exactly recognition-not-attainment:
nothing is removed from the world; the false reading of it is removed.

> **PENETRATION 3 (the counter resolved):** The "difference is real" (V3-I, TĀ pratibimbavāda)
> and "difference is the contracted power" (master-key/impurities) are NOT contradictory. The
> real difference = the self's own reflection ("a true reflection, not a mistake"); the impurity
> = the MISREADING of that real difference as external separation. **Recognition does not remove
> the difference (it is real); it removes the misreading (the exhaustiveness/externality claim).
> Nothing added, nothing removed — the difference is real and the bondage is its misreading.**
> This unifies the two, and it is the deepest resolution: the "difference is real" claim and
> the "recognition removes the false self-positioning" claim are the same thesis.

## ROUND 4 — the deepest consequence: the counter deepens the master-key

**The question:** What does this parallel/counter do to the whole structure?

**The derivation:** 
- The V3-I passage and the Tantrāloka's pratibimbavāda together establish: **the difference is
  real because it is the self's own reflection.**
- The master-key established: **recognition = the reversal of the direction of
  self-apprehension (outward → inward).**
- The counter-resolution established: **recognition does not remove the difference (it is real,
  the self's reflection); it removes the misreading (the externality).**

**The synthesis:** The outward turn (the direction-thesis) is NOT the production of the
difference — the difference is real, the self's own reflection. The outward turn is the
MISREADING of that real reflection as external, separate, exhaustive. So the reversal of
direction is not a reversal away from the world (the world stays, it is real); it is a reversal
of how the real world is read — from "external separate things" to "the self's own reflection,
my own face."

> **PENETRATION 4 (the deepest):** The pratibimbavāda REFINES the master-key. The outward turn
> is not the creation of difference (difference is real, the self's reflection); it is the
> misreading of the real difference as external. **Recognition reverses the direction not away
> from the world but away from the misreading — from "external things" to "the self's own
> face."** The world is not left behind; it is seen as the self's reflection. "The face
> reflected in the mirror is my own."

2. "The will and its object are inherently one" — parallels V3-I claim 1.

   difference is real, not mere illusion).

**The refined synthesis:**

- The reversal of direction is not away from the world but away from the misreading — the world

## THE LOGICVID LESSON

Hounding the IPVV's kāryakāraṇatāvimarśa against the Tantrāloka finds STRONG parallels and ONE
deep counter, which resolves into the deepest refinement yet:

**PARALLELS:** (1) Action = the will ("the will itself pervaded by its own heat has assumed
three aspects" — the three powers are one will); (2) "the will and its object are inherently
one"; (3) **the difference is real — "the reflections are real, a true reflection, not a
mistake" (the pratibimbavāda)**, which grounds the IPVV's refutation of the mere-ignorance
view.

**THE COUNTER RESOLVED:** "Difference is real" (V3-I, TĀ) and "difference is the contracted
power" (master-key/impurities) are not contradictory. The real difference = the self's own
reflection; the impurity = the misreading of that real difference as external separation.

**THE REFINEMENT:** Recognition does not remove the difference (it is real — "the face in the
mirror is my own"); it removes the misreading (externality). The reversal of direction is not
away from the world but away from the misreading — the world seen as the self's own face. **The
world is not left behind; it is recognized as the self's reflection.** This unifies the V3-I
realism with the whole master-key/recognition structure, and it extends directly into the
rasa-work (the recognition of the reflection-as-self is the wonder).
