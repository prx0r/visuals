## 1. THE CLEANEST FORMULATION (from `recognition-formulation.md`)

**The universal level:**
```
C∞ = ⟨P, V, S⟩
```
- **P** = prakāśa (manifestation)
- **V** = vimarśa (reflexive determination)
- **S** = svātantrya (freedom of self-articulation)

(These are analytical aspects of one reality, not separable components.)

**The determination operator:**
```
δᵢ = ⟨Kᵢ, Aᵢ, Vᵢ, Tᵢ, Cᵢ, Xᵢ⟩
```
- Kᵢ limited knowledge · Aᵢ limited action · Vᵢ finite value · Tᵢ temporal succession ·
  Cᵢ causal/spatial situation · Xᵢ exclusive subject–object determination.

**The finite manifestation:**
```
δᵢ(C∞) = ⟨Sᵢ, Wᵢ, Γᵢ⟩
```
- **Sᵢ** = the finite knower (a nested embodied ecology of agencies)
- **Wᵢ** = its organism-relative objective field
- **Γᵢ** = the relation of perception, action, valuation, and exclusion connecting them

**Bondage:** Bᵢ = δᵢ + (identification of δᵢ as exhaustive)
**Recognition:** Rᵢ: δᵢ(C∞) is recognized as a MODE of C∞ — WITHOUT destroying δᵢ.

> **The finite body-mind does not disappear. It ceases to be apprehended as an
> independently existing and exhaustive source of manifestation and agency.**

## 2. THE FOUR COMPONENTS (from `recognitionandsiva.md`)

Recognition is NOT merely a change in a self-model. It has four necessary components:

```
Recognitionₓ(i) := Transformationₓ(i) ∧ Reidentificationₓ(i) ∧ Veridicalityₓ(i) ∧ Stabilizationₓ(i)
```
1. **Transformation** — the embodied system changes its self–world organization.
2. **Reidentification** — it judges that the present subject is identical with a
   previously unrecognized ground.
3. **Veridicality** — that identity judgment is TRUE (the essential condition).
4. **Stabilization** — the transformation becomes robust under perturbation.

**Without veridicality, it is not recognition — it is conversion, reinterpretation, or an
altered self-model.** This is the crucial honesty: recognition does not produce its object;
it correctly reidentifies something already present.

**The generic structure (ordinary recognition):**
```
Recog(a,b) := Present(a) ∧ Acquainted(b) ∧ Identify(a,b) ∧ a=b
```
The final condition (a=b) is essential — if a≠b, it is misidentification regardless of
subjective certainty.

## 3. THE THREE TARGETS OF RECOGNITION (the graded levels)

Recognition admits three targets, from minimal to maximal:

### A. Process-inexternal recognition (the minimal, defensible)
```
Θᵢ^PI : Dᵢ ≡_inext Oᵢ
```
> "I am not a spectator inside this living process; this manifest field is the intrinsic
> existence of the process itself."

The strongest recognition licensed directly by process inexternalism. **It does not
establish a universal ground.**

### B. Participatory recognition (the Proclean middle)
```
Θᵢ^Part : Dᵢ = π_Oᵢ(M₀)
```
> Ground(Dᵢ) = Ground(Dⱼ) = M₀, but Dᵢ ≠ Dⱼ.
> "This local field is not self-grounding; it is a finite participation in a manifestation
> exceeding it."

One source + many genuinely distinct participants. This is the Iamblichean participatory
recognition (not identity).

### C. Full Śaiva recognition (the maximal, requires the 5 axioms)
```
Θᵢ^Siva : GroundSubject(Dᵢ) = S, and Dᵢ = Modeᵢ(S)
```
> "The ground manifesting as this finite subject and world is the one universal subject,
> Śiva."

**The five Śaiva axioms (S1–S5):**
- **S1** Universal manifest ground: ∀i, Ground(Dᵢ) = M₀.
- **S2** The ground is one subject: M₀ = S.
- **S3** Internal manifestation: all subjects/objects/relations are determinations internal
  to S.
- **S4** Modal localization: ∀i, Dᵢ = Modeᵢ(S).
- **S5** Svātantrya: universal articulation is not constrained by anything external.

**The full formal statement:**
```
Pratyabhijñāᵢ := Success(T_rec^Siva) ∧ Judge(Θᵢ^Siva) ∧ (S1 ∧ S2 ∧ S3 ∧ S4 ∧ S5) ∧ Stable_P
```
**If the transformation occurs but S1–S5 are false, it is a Śaiva-FORM self-transformation,
not veridical recognition of Śiva.**

## 4. THE MODAL IDENTITY (reconciling one Śiva with distinct fields)

The tension (one ground, many private fields) is resolved by distinguishing:
- identity of **ground**;
- identity of **mode**;
- identity of **phenomenal token**.

```
GroundSubject(Dᵢ) = GroundSubject(Dⱼ) = S    (identity of ground)
Dᵢ ≡_{mode,i} S                              (identity of mode)
Dᵢ ≢_{token} Dⱼ                              (no identity of token)
```
> The same universal subject is locally articulated under different modes, but the modes
> are not one shared empirical field.

## 5. WHAT RECOGNITION CHANGES (and what it does NOT)

**Recognition changes:** Xᵢ (exclusive self-determination), eᵢ (standpoint treated as
exhaustive), gᵢ (ground attribution), ωᵢ (ownership), αᵢ (agency), Πᵢ (policies/metacontrol).

**Recognition does NOT remove:** Oᵢ (the body-process), Lᵢ (finite constraints), Dᵢ (the
local field), ∂Dᵢ (field privacy), Wᵢ (the organism-relative world).

> **Recognition removes exclusive identification — not finite embodiment.**

**The distinction (from `recognition-formulation.md`):**
- **Limitation** (Lᵢ): makes a perspective possible.
- **Bondage** (Bᵢ = Lᵢ + AbsoluteIdentification(Sᵢ) + Externalization(Wᵢ)): treating that
  perspective as an independent, exhaustive self.

## 6. THE MODERN COUSIN — CTMU (from `ctmu.md`) + the honest boundary

**CTMU (Langan)** is "a modern logico-linguistic cousin of reflexive manifestation monism":
```
reality = one self-contained process in which subject/object, syntax/state, cognition/world
are differentiated poles of one self-articulating identity (SCSPL, M:L↔U)
```

**The structural correspondence to the Śaiva:**
| Śaiva | CTMU |
|-------|------|
| prakāśa–vimarśa | self-presenting self-processing |
| aham–idam | intension–extension |
| svātantrya | self-configuration / telic recursion |
| Śiva | M (the global self-contained identity) |

**But the honesty (why CTMU doesn't solve the frontier):**
> CTMU moves too quickly from "reality is self-contained" to "reality is globally cognitive
> and teleological." Operational self-reference ⟹̸ phenomenal self-presence; self-organization
> ⟹̸ unrestricted agency. **CTMU lacks the aperture problem** — it does not give a criterion
> for one phenomenal boundary, one field, nested subjects, fusion/fission, felt valence.

**The correct use:** CTMU is a *metaphysical hypothesis generator*, not an established
theory. It strengthens the claim that difference/sameness, subject/object, model/world must
be internally coupled within one reality — but the frontier remains:
> Apt(Oᵢ) ⟺ PhenomenalField(Oᵢ) — unsolved.

## 7. THE UNRESOLVED INFERENCE (the honest boundary — shared by all three)

All three formalizations converge on the SAME unresolved question:

> **Why must the common ground of finite manifestation be ONE self-determining subject,
> rather than a common ground that supports multiple genuinely local subjects?**

- `recognition-formulation`: the scholars confirm Pratyabhijñā asserts this and tries to
  demonstrate it; they do not eliminate the contested inference (the epistemic-ontological
  gap: "external reality accessible only through consciousness ⟹̸ its existence consists in
  consciousness").
- `recognitionandsiva`: "recognition cannot answer that merely by occurring. It can be
  called recognition of Śiva only after that ontology has been independently justified."
- `ctmu`: the same challenge survives in all three systems — why does universal
  self-contained articulation divide into these private phenomenal apertures?

**This is the paper's Argument 10 — the universalization (C), held open.** The three
formalizations make it precise.
