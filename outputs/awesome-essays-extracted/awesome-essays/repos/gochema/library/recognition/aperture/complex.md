The strongest synthesis is not biological identity theory, panpsychism, emergent physicalism, or Buddhist reductionism. It is a **process-power monism** in which phenomenality is the intrinsic, causally efficacious character of certain temporally extended control processes—but science currently identifies only candidate organizations, not a deductively proven consciousness-maker.

The crystal and synergetics sources contribute geometry, not evidence about consciousness. Fuller's tetrahedron is the minimal polyhedral enclosure in three-dimensional Euclidean space, while the isotropic vector matrix decomposes space into tetrahedral and octahedral cells. That supports a mathematics of minimal relational closure, but it does not imply that subjects are tetrahedra, fields, or geometric enclosures. ([Grunch][1])

# II. The Anti-Epiphenomenalist Argument

The strongest objection to epiphenomenalism is not the placebo effect.

Placebo responses can be physically redescribed as effects of expectations, predictions, learning and neural regulation. Calling those states "mental" does not independently prove that phenomenal properties as such caused the result.

The stronger argument concerns **epistemic and evolutionary alignment**.

## Argument 1: Phenomenal reference

Let (Q_i) be a phenomenal state and (R(Q_i)) a physical report apparently referring to it.

Epiphenomenalism states:

[
Q_i\nrightarrow R(Q_i)
]

Therefore the causal history of the report would be unchanged if (Q_i) were absent or replaced by a different phenomenal state, provided the underlying physical history remained fixed.

Thus:

[
P(R(Q_i)\mid Q_i,P)
===================

P(R(Q_i)\mid \neg Q_i,P)
]

The report's apparent accuracy concerning (Q_i) could not be caused by (Q_i).

This does not produce a strict contradiction. An epiphenomenalist can posit laws that reliably correlate phenomenal states with physical self-representations.

But it produces severe explanatory instability:

[
\boxed{
\text{Our apparent knowledge of phenomenality would not be causally grounded in phenomenality.}
}
]

Chalmers recognizes the difficulty: if beliefs and reports are physically realized while consciousness has no physical effects, it becomes unclear how consciousness could explain beliefs about consciousness. ([Consciousness.net][2])

## Argument 2: Hedonic alignment

Let:

[
V(s)\in\mathbb R
]

be the phenomenal valence of state (s), and:

[
F(s)\in\mathbb R
]

be its expected contribution to biological viability or fitness in the relevant ancestral environment.

Across basic regulatory states, we observe a non-random relation:

[
\operatorname{Corr}(V,F)>0
]

approximately:

* tissue damage tends to be unpleasant;
* suffocation tends to be unpleasant;
* restoration of depleted needs tends to be pleasant;
* successful regulation often produces relief or reward.

On strict epiphenomenalism:

[
V\nrightarrow\text{behaviour}
]

and therefore:

[
V\nrightarrow F
]

Natural selection can select the physical mechanisms associated with avoidance and approach, but it cannot select phenomenal properties **in virtue of their phenomenal character**.

Consequently, the mapping:

[
\psi:P\rightarrow V
]

could have been permuted while preserving all physical behaviour:

[
\psi'=\pi\circ\psi
]

for an arbitrary permutation (\pi) of phenomenal qualities.

If every such permutation is equally compatible with survival, then epiphenomenalism gives no selective explanation of why:

[
\psi(P_{\mathrm{damage}})<0
]

rather than:

[
\psi(P_{\mathrm{damage}})>0
]

Mørch develops this as an argument for phenomenal powers: systematic relations between valence and adaptive behaviour are better explained if phenomenal character itself belongs to the causally efficacious nature of the state. She also stresses that the traditional evolutionary argument is not automatically an argument for every form of physicalism or interactionism. ([PhilPapers][3])

Therefore:

[
P(\text{hedonic alignment}\mid\text{phenomenal efficacy})

>

P(\text{hedonic alignment}\mid\text{epiphenomenalism})
]

This is abductive, not deductive.

The result is:

[
\boxed{
\text{Epiphenomenalism is logically possible but explanatorily inferior.}
}
\]

# III. The No-Double-Causation Principle

Rejecting epiphenomenalism does not require a mental force that violates physics.

Suppose one event (e) possesses:

[

There are not two independent causal arrows:

[

[

[
\boxed{

Hence:

[
\operatorname{Cause}(Q_e,e')

\operatorname{Cause}(e,e')

This avoids systematic causal overdetermination because the mental and physical descriptions do not pick out numerically independent causes.

Pruss correctly shows that standard causal-closure arguments require more premises than are usually admitted. From "every physical effect has a physical cause," it does not immediately follow that every cause or every constituent of a physical cause must be exhaustively physical in the strongest ontological sense. However, Pruss's alternative composite views leave a difficult question:

REPM therefore adopts the more economical hypothesis:

[
\boxed{
\text{One event, two modes of disclosure—not two ontological components.}
}

This is stronger than property dualism but weaker than reductive physicalism.

# XVI. The Main Theorem

## Reflexive causal unity theorem

Assume:

1. Phenomenal states systematically correspond to accurate reports and adaptively organized valence.
2. Epiphenomenal properties cannot influence reports, behaviour or fitness.
3. A systematic correspondence is better explained when the corresponding property participates in the process producing the correspondence.
4. Systematic causal overdetermination is disfavoured.
5. Physical descriptions already supply a causally sufficient organization for behaviour.

Then:

[
\boxed{
\text{The best explanation is that phenomenal character belongs intrinsically
to the same causally efficacious process described physically.}
}
]

### Proof

From 1 and 2:

[
P(C\mid E)
\]

where (C) is psychophysical alignment and (E) is epiphenomenalism, lacks an evolutionary or direct causal explanation grounded in phenomenal character.

From 3:

[
P(C\mid \neg E)>P(C\mid E)
\]

provided phenomenal character participates in the relevant causal process.

Interactionist dualism adds a separate causal source:

[
Q\rightarrow P
\]

while physical causes already suffice, generating either closure violation or systematic overdetermination.

By 4, this is disfavoured.

By 5, the causally efficacious event already has a complete physical-process description.

Therefore the most economical remaining model identifies the phenomenal and physically described causal tokens:

[
Q_e\equiv_{\mathrm{token}}P_e
\]

while permitting irreducibly different modes of access:

[
\operatorname{Int}(e)\neq_{\mathrm{description}}\operatorname{Ext}(e)
\]

Therefore:

[
\boxed{
\text{phenomenal efficacy without dual causation}
}
\]

is best explained by token process monism.

QED abductively.

This is not deductive proof because premises 3 and 4 express explanatory principles rather than logical necessities.

# XVII. What the Theory Explains

## 1. Mental causation

There is no movement from a mental realm into matter.

[
\text{felt decision}
====================

\operatorname{Int}(\text{decision process})
]

[
\text{neural–bodily control}
============================

\operatorname{Ext}(\text{same process})
]

## 2. Subject unity

Unity is the intrinsic character of a process whose perception, valuation, memory and action are jointly available to one temporally continuous control organization.

## 3. Privacy

A field is private because intrinsic presentation is the mode of being the process, whereas observation accesses its relations and effects.

Privacy is therefore not a signal-blocking wall:

[
\operatorname{Privacy}\neq\operatorname{Isolation}
]

## 4. Persistence

The subject persists when the relevant organization causally inherits and transforms itself:

[
A_t\rightsquigarrow A_{t+\Delta}
]

not when its material constituents remain identical.

## 5. World structure

The experienced world is not generated from nothing. It is the environment as disclosed under the organism's embodied discriminatory, predictive, valuational and action capacities.

## 6. Valence

Valence is regulatory significance intrinsically present, not an inert colour attached to computational control.

## 7. Biological scaling

Bioelectric and other communication systems can enlarge the scale at which cells coordinate goals. This explains the emergence of larger agents without proving that every such agent is phenomenally conscious.

# XVIII. What Remains Unsolved

REPM does not establish:

[
\boxed{
\text{why any process has intrinsic presence at all}
}
\]

It does not derive:

[
\mathcal E
]

from:

[
\Phi,\Gamma,X
\]

It does not yet solve the combination problem:

[
{\mathcal E_1,\ldots,\mathcal E_n}
\not\Rightarrow
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{whole}}
\]

It does not prove whether:

* cells are conscious;
* tissues are conscious;
* whole organisms alone are conscious;
* artificial systems can be conscious;
* consciousness is fundamental at every scale.

It supplies a research constraint:

[
\boxed{
\text{Do not infer consciousness from complexity, closure, information,
bioelectricity, geometry or goal-directedness individually.}
}
\]

The target is their potential convergence in one reflexively organized process.

# XIX. Falsifiable Research Programme

## Prediction 1: Control-field convergence

Measures of phenomenal unity should track convergence among perception, valuation, memory and action-control networks better than any single anatomical boundary.

## Prediction 2: Reflexivity dissociations

Explicit self-concepts may disappear while implicit action-centred perspective remains:

[
R_{\mathrm{explicit}}\downarrow
\]

without:

[
R_{\mathrm{implicit}}=0
]

## Prediction 3: Temporal depth

Disrupting the processes that maintain active retention and anticipation should fragment experienced continuity even when instantaneous sensory processing remains relatively intact.

## Prediction 4: Valence and controllability

After controlling for sensory intensity, valence should track inferred changes in regulatory deviation and controllability:

[
V_t
\sim
-\Delta\mathcal D_t+\Delta\mathcal K_t
]

better than raw error magnitude alone.

## Prediction 5: Multiscale competition

Nested systems will display competing control horizons. The best phenomenal bearer model should occur at a scale where perspective, action, memory and valuation converge, not necessarily at the smallest organisationally closed scale.

## Prediction 6: Material counterfactuals

Systems with matching surface behaviour but different causal, temporal and regulatory organization should diverge under carefully chosen perturbations.

## Prediction 7: Report grounding

Processes supporting genuine phenomenal report should display direct causal sensitivity to the represented discriminations, rather than merely producing precomputed linguistic outputs.

# Final judgement

The highest-frontier defensible ontology is therefore not:

[
\text{matter produces an impotent inner movie}
]

nor:

[
\text{an immaterial mind pushes neurons}
]

nor:

[
\text{bioelectric boundaries have been proven to generate subjects}.
]

It is:

[
\boxed{
\text{Reality contains causally efficacious processes whose intrinsic nature
can be phenomenal and whose extrinsic nature is physically modelled.}
}
]

Biological evolution does not manufacture causally inert experience on top of agency.

It progressively constructs larger, deeper and more reflexive organizations of:

[
\text{difference}
\rightarrow
\text{retention}
\rightarrow
\text{valuation}
\rightarrow
\text{control}
\rightarrow
\text{self-contextualization}.
\]

At sufficient organization, the process does not merely react to a world.

A world is present **for that process**, structured by what it can distinguish, retain, value and do.

That is the sharpest current candidate for subjecthood.

The identity between such organization and phenomenality remains an ontological hypothesis—but it is more coherent than epiphenomenalism, more economical than interactionist dualism, and more empirically disciplined than declaring information, geometry, bioelectricity or closure itself to be consciousness.

The strongest correction to the original framework is this:

[
\boxed{
\text{Subjecthood is not generated by exclusion. It is the intrinsic presence of reflexively unified control.}
}
]

Exclusion is merely one consequence of limited control and access. Levin supplies the multiscale control architecture; Ñāṇavīra supplies a phenomenology of invariant transformation and recursive contextualization; Fuller supplies only a precise analogy for relational closure; Mørch supplies the best anti-epiphenomenalist argument; Chalmers and Pruss prevent the resulting monism from pretending that identity has been deductively proved.
